## Context

Slice 4 of 19, `numbers-and-dates` (`epic:slice-04-numbers-and-dates`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 4; decision 6, "The functions of `Math` that IEEE 754 does not fix", items 1 and 4). The third of three stories that write the `Math` functions the platform `libm` would answer differently on different targets (HOST-135). It depends on `story:swell-num-math-double-double` (the pair arithmetic, the gates, `data/math/SOURCE`) and not on `story:swell-num-math-exp-and-log`: it writes `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `asin`, `acos`, `atan` and `atan2`. It lifts no test262 case; `story:math-functions` builds the builtins on it.

On the base (`c6d44d1`; the unit reads every place named in this story again on its base) none of these functions exists in `swell-num`. The design text on these algorithms was written and not run: its numbers (the 1,280 bits of 2/π, the 192 bits kept of the product, the series degrees, the accuracy) are claims, and this unit measures them.

Design it follows, by name, with the bullets and items of decision 6 it builds on: "The functions of `Math` that IEEE 754 does not fix", item 1 (`sin`, `cos`, `tan`: for |x| at most π/4 no reduction, above it Payne and Hanek's reduction for every argument (Muller, 11.2): the 53-bit mantissa times a window of the bits of 2/π held in a table of 1,280 fractional bits (20 `u64` words; the bits up to position 971 + 53 + 192 = 1,216 are needed, 64 of margin), 192 bits of the product kept, the quotient modulo 4 and the fraction giving r = x - k π/2 as a pair with relative error at most 2^-100; kernels on |r| at most π/4: the Maclaurin series of `sin` through r^21 and of `cos` through r^22, `tan` the quotient of the two kernels in double-double, `-cos / sin` in the odd quadrants) and item 4 (`atan`, `atan2`, `asin`, `acos`: |y/x| above 1 swapped and π/2 minus the result taken; c the nearest of 0, 1/8, ..., 1 to q; atan(c) from a table of 9 pairs; u = (q - c)/(1 + q c), |u| at most 1/16; the series through u^19; the quadrant adds a multiple of π/2 or π as pairs; `asin(x)` is `atan2(x, sqrt((1 - x)(1 + x)))` and `acos(x)` is `atan2(sqrt((1 - x)(1 + x)), x)` with the root of the exact pair; `atan2` follows the draft's table of special values (`#sec-math.atan2`) before it reduces and scales y and x by a power of two when their exponents differ by 60 or more); the paragraphs "Constants and how a reader checks them" (π by Machin's formula, `atan(k/8)` by its series, 2/π with its 1,280 bits, each to 1,400 bits by integers only), "The accuracy the engine promises" (group A, `sin cos atan`: less than 1 ulp; group B, `tan asin acos atan2`: at most 2 ulp; `cos(0)` is 1 and `acos(1)` is +0) and "How a test holds the bound with no second implementation" with "The tool that makes the lines", including its step 6 (the arguments of `sin`, `cos` and `tan` nearest to a multiple of π/2, found in `bc` by continued fractions, for 40 values of q from -20 to 960); "Which `f64` methods an engine crate may call"; ECMA-262 21.3.2 for each function.

**Prerequisite of the design author, not in the `Writes` of this story: the reference data of `Math`.** The sentence below is written into `docs/design/engine.md` by the design change that is merged into `wave/s10-1` before the unit starts (it is the same sentence as in `story:swell-num-math-double-double` and `story:swell-num-math-exp-and-log`), and the unit follows it as written; the start gate of the acceptance is its first line. The file of this story has about 1,800 lines: 200 arguments for each of the 7 functions and the 40 nearest-to-a-multiple arguments for each of `sin`, `cos`, `tan`. The sentence:

> **Reference data of Math** (decision 6, in the paragraph "How a test holds the bound with no second implementation", in place of a single file and its one test). Each of the three stories that write `Math` functions in `swell-num` commits its own reference file, in the line format and by the procedure of "The tool that makes the lines": `data/math/reference-hypot.txt` (`story:swell-num-math-double-double`: the lines of `hypot`, the only function it writes), `data/math/reference-exp-log.txt` (`story:swell-num-math-exp-and-log`) and `data/math/reference-trig.txt` (`story:swell-num-math-trigonometry`). Each file has one line `<sha256>  <file>` in `data/math/SOURCE`, which `math-data-check` reads, and is read by the test file of its story (`crates/num/tests/math_dd.rs`, `math_exp_log.rs`, `math_trig.rs`, modules of the crate's one test binary).

**Not run, to be measured by the unit.** The unit finds the degree of each series by the design's rule (cut where the first omitted term is below 2^-80 of the result on the whole reduced interval), states it beside the design's (`sin` r^21, `cos` r^22, `atan` u^19) and reports a difference; it checks by integer arithmetic that the table of 2/π holds the bits it claims and that 192 bits of the product are enough for the 40 nearest arguments (the reduced fraction's error against the exact one), and states the closest approach of a double to a multiple of π/2 that its search found beside the design's 2^-61; it measures the largest error in ulp of each function over the data and over a denser walk of its own (no platform `libm`), states it beside the group bound, and reports a function that misses its group.

## Change

Failing tests first: the tests below are written before the code and fail on the base (the functions do not exist).

- `crates/num/src/math/reduce.rs` (new): `reduce(x: f64) -> (u8, Dd)` (the quadrant modulo 4 and r, by Payne and Hanek for |x| above π/4: the mantissa as a `u64`, a 128-bit window of `TWO_OVER_PI` selected by the exponent, the 192-bit product by `u128` partial products, the integer part modulo 4 and the fraction multiplied by π/2 as a pair) and the table `TWO_OVER_PI: [u64; 20]` (the first 1,280 fractional bits of 2/π; `π/2` as a pair). `crates/num/src/math/trig.rs` (new): `sin`, `cos`, `tan` (kernels in double-double, Horner, coefficients as bit pairs, the quadrant picks the kernel and the sign) and `crates/num/src/math/atan.rs` (new): the kernel over the table of 9 values of `atan(k/8)` as pairs, `atan`, `atan2` (the draft's special values first: NaN, the infinities and zeros with their signs, then the reduction with the swap and the scaling by a power of two when the exponents differ by 60 or more), `asin`, `acos` (a magnitude above 1 gives NaN; the root of the exact pair `(1 - x)(1 + x)`). `crates/num/src/math.rs` gets `pub mod reduce; pub mod trig; pub mod atan;` and the `pub use` lines (the one hunk shared with the exp-and-log story). Every loop is over a fixed number of limbs or terms; no loop depends on the argument; the work is bounded by the table's 20 words.
- The constants and the test that recomputes them. `TWO_OVER_PI` (1,280 bits), π/2 as a pair, the 9 values of `atan(k/8)` as pairs and the series coefficients: `crates/num/tests/math_trig.rs` recomputes each from its definition with exact integer arithmetic (`crates/num/tests/support/exact.rs`): π by Machin's formula to 1,400 bits, 2/π as the integer quotient, `atan(k/8)` by its series to 1,400 bits, and compares bit for bit. A constant that no test recomputes does not ship.
- The reference data and its test. `data/math/reference-trig.txt` (new): the format and the procedure of `story:swell-num-math-exp-and-log` (one line per case: function, argument bits, the two enclosing Numbers), 200 arguments per function drawn by the design's Knuth LCG, the special values (±0, ±1, the powers of two, the thresholds), and for `sin`, `cos` and `tan` the 40 arguments nearest to a multiple of π/2 found by `bc` through continued fractions (the convergents and semiconvergents of the fractional part of 2^q (2/π) whose denominator is a 53-bit mantissa, for q from -20 to 960; a q with no such denominator is left out and counted in the header). The lines are made once, outside the repository, by a POSIX `sh` script driven by `bc -l` that is not committed and that no task, gate or other session calls; it runs in the background without `slot` (it generates, it does not build or test); the unit states its wall time and the header names the tool, its version and the steps. The test reads the file with `include_str!`, holds group A to "one of the two" and group B to at most 2 ulp from the nearer, runs in under a second and states its entry count and time.
- The exact cases, by hand: `sin(±0)` is ±0, `cos(±0)` is 1, `tan(±0)` is ±0, `sin(±∞)`, `cos(±∞)` and `tan(±∞)` are NaN, `asin(±1)` is ±π/2 (the Numbers `1.5707963267948966`), `asin(2)` is NaN, `acos(1)` is +0, `acos(-1)` is π, `atan(±∞)` is ±π/2, `atan2` entry by entry of the draft's table (the unit counts them: `atan2(±0, -0)` is ±π, `atan2(+0, +0)` is +0, `atan2(±y, ±∞)` and so on), the sign of every zero, the argument `2^1023` and the largest Number for `sin`, `cos` and `tan` against the data, and the 40 nearest arguments. Every function gets NaN, ±∞, ±0 and a subnormal as arguments in one table.
- The `f64` operations and casts of this story, each with what fixes its result: `+ - * /` and `sqrt` (IEEE 754-2019 5.4.1: correctly rounded), `abs`, `copysign` and `floor` (exact, 5.9 and 5.5.1), `to_bits` and `from_bits` (exact), comparisons, `u64` and `u128` arithmetic (checked or explicitly wrapping; the product of two `u64` fits `u128`), and `as f64` of a `u64` below 2^53 (exact; the Rust Reference, "Numeric cast"). No `mul_add`, no `sin_cos`, no `atan2` method of `f64`, no `round_ties_even`.
