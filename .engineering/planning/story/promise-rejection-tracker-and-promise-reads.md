## Domain relations

- Agent → rejection tracker calls: one to many, each a notification to the host with the promise and the operation, made while the engine runs and answering nothing — declared (`spec/domains/host.yaml:1182`, the command `swell.host.HostPromiseRejectionTracker` with its outcome `tracked` and the event `PromiseRejectionTracked`; its comment at `:1179` names the promise as unmapped).

- Host → promise reads: many to one promise, each through a `Root` of the host, none changing the promise but `mark_promise_as_handled` — inferable (inferred from `docs/design/host-interface.md` lines 1422 to 1424 and section 13; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model holds no engine value).

## Change
