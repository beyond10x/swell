## Context

Slice 8 of 19, `classes` (`epic:slice-08-classes`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 8). This story runs private fields and `#x in`, and is where a private field meets every other part of the slice. Private fields: the field kind of an entry of [[PrivateElements]], added by the one add function of `object/private_names.rs` (PrivateFieldAdd: a `TypeError` when the object has the field, the case of a constructor that returns another object and stamps it; the add does not read [[Extensible]]), compiled by `AddPrivateField` into pass 2 of the instance initializer and into the static runs, in source order with the public fields (item 4 of "Classes": "the private add for a private key"); PrivateGet and PrivateSet of a field through `GetPrivate` and `SetPrivate` of `story:private-names-and-private-methods`, which also owns `Target::Private` in `crates/compiler/src/expression.rs`, so every assignment form with a private reference as its target (`=`, compound, `&&=`, `||=`, `??=`, `++`, `--`, destructuring targets, `for (this.#x of ...)`) and private references in optional chains (`o?.#x`, `o?.a.#x`) reach a field through code that exists before this story, which tests them on fields and writes no compiler code for them; and `#x in o` through `PrivateIn` (a `TypeError` when o is no object, else whether PrivateElementFind finds the name, for fields, methods and accessors). It lifts the holds `private field is not supported before slice 8` and `private name in is not supported before slice 8` of `story:class-element-syntax-and-early-errors`, the last two holds of the slice.

It takes the cases where a private field or `#x in` meets the other parts (a static block that reads a private field, the private fields of a derived class added after `super()`, private fields beside private methods and public fields). One rule decides every list, the scan's (`docs/plan/slice-08/scan.awk`): a case goes to the first story, in the order of the edges, whose closure (its own parts and those of every story it depends on, directly or not) holds every part the case needs. Every case of this list needs `PF` (a private field or `#x in`, the part only this story holds), and its closure, `B NT SP C D PM E PF A`, holds every other part, so the rule puts here each case with `PF` and no part outside the slice. The cases where the parts of orders 3 and 4 meet without `PF` (449 in revision 2, 415 of them `B D PM`) are not here since revision 3: with the edges of `story:class-static-blocks` to `story:derived-classes-and-super-call` and `story:private-names-and-private-methods`, its closure holds `C`, `D` and `PM`, so the rule puts them on its list, with their tests and the fixes of their meeting; the design leaves the order of the instance elements nothing of its own (the compiled initializer of item 4, pass 1 then pass 2; the call after each SuperCall is item 7's `Dup; InitInstance; Pop`), and this story fixes only where a private field meets the other parts.

Edges: `story:derived-classes-and-super-call`, `story:public-class-fields`, `story:private-names-and-private-methods`, `story:class-static-blocks` (each a part of the cases with a private field where the parts meet; `story:public-class-fields` and `story:private-names-and-private-methods` also write the functions this story extends). Order 5, alone in its order.

A private field may be added to a non-extensible object (the feature `nonextensible-applies-to-private` is excluded, `conformance/test262/features.txt`).

The list is `docs/plan/slice-08/story-private-fields.cases.txt`: the 984 cases of the key `private-fields` of `docs/plan/slice-08/scan.tsv` (every class with a private field or `#x in`, outside eval and with no part of another slice; no negative case): `language/statements/class` 435, `language/expressions/class` 409, `language/identifiers` 60 (`start-unicode-*-class*.js`: a class with a private field per code point of a Unicode version, large sources, which the runner's time bound covers), `language/expressions/compound-assignment` 48, `language/expressions/logical-assignment` 21, `language/expressions/in` 11. In revision 1 the 446 folded cases joined it and 2 cases left it for `story:class-static-blocks` (`static-init-sequence.js`, `static-init-abrupt.js`, which need fields and static blocks but no private name). In revision 2, 2 cases joined it from `story:public-class-fields`: `language/{statements,expressions}/class/elements/super-access-from-arrow-func-on-field.js` (`super.x` in an arrow in a field initializer), which need the field part and the lift of `super` in an arrow by `story:derived-classes-and-super-call`, and so the closure of this story (`docs/plan/slice-08/scan.awk`, the part `A`). In revision 3, 449 cases left it for `story:class-static-blocks` by the rule above, those 2 among them: every case of the list without `PF`. A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story goes to `docs/plan/slice-08/close-later.tsv` by the coordinator's commit, as `story:new-target-meta-property` states.
