## Context

The parser keeps a regular expression literal as a node with its body and flags, its pattern unparsed (`crates/syntax/src/lib.rs` lines 21 and 22, `crates/syntax/src/ast.rs` lines 68 to 75), and the early errors check its flags only (`crates/syntax/src/early.rs` line 578, `fn regexp`, IsValidRegularExpressionLiteral without its call of ParsePattern, `#sec-primary-expression-regular-expression-literals-static-semantics-early-errors`). So a literal whose pattern is not valid parses, and 150 negative cases of `test/language/literals/regexp/` fail in both their runs on `the case parsed; a SyntaxError was expected at parse`. This story makes the parser check each literal with ParsePattern of `swell-regexp` (`story:regexp-crate-and-pattern-parser`), so a pattern error is an early error of the script, the module, the eval code or the body given to `Function`, and it closes HOST-142. The literal is still refused when it is evaluated (`story:regexp-objects-exec-and-literals` lifts that). It runs beside `story:regexp-compiler-and-backtracking-matcher`, which writes `crates/regexp` only, and `story:case-mapping-tables-and-string-case-methods`, which writes `crates/unicode`, `crates/unicode-gen` and the case methods of `crates/swell`.

Lifts of the consumer (`docs/plan/slice-05/consumer-regexps.tsv`): none at run time; the 12 literals pass the new early errors (each is a valid pattern; the two with `{` and `}` as pattern characters by the grammar of B.1.2), which the test `the_patterns_of_the_consumer_table_parse` below shows with patterns written for it of the same constructs (nothing of the downloaded files is copied).

The list of this story is `docs/plan/slice-05/story-literal-early-errors.cases.txt`: 154 failing cases (308 runs), all negative parse cases of `test/language/literals/regexp/` (`named-groups/` 41 of them), whose first failing run ends on `the case parsed; a SyntaxError was expected at parse` and whose text uses no part of a later story (the `v` flag, a property escape, a group name outside ASCII), found by `docs/plan/slice-05/scan.awk` (its head gives the command) over the first failing run of each failing case of slice 5 on `e444aaf`. Four of them (`early-err-arithmetic-modifiers-add-remove-i.js`, `-add-remove-multi-duplicate.js`, `-reverse-add-remove-i.js`, `-reverse-add-remove-multi-duplicate.js`) carry a modifier `i`: their pattern fails its parse, which comes before the compile step that refuses `i` (decision 7, "Refusals until the later stories of slice 5"), so they pass here. The other 13 negative cases of that directory with that first failure (26 runs) are on the list of `story:identifiers-and-group-names-outside-ascii` (a group name outside ASCII, refused at parse until then). A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit of the list file on the wave branch whose body names that part; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

Design it builds on: decision 7 of `docs/design/engine.md`, "How `swell-regexp` charges its work" (item 2: the implementation of the trait over the `Meter` of the parse) and "A regular expression literal: checked at parse time, compiled at each evaluation" (rule 1), "No Rust recursion in the parser and the compiler of a pattern" and "Refusals until the later stories of slice 5"; decision 3, "Depth bounds" (the nesting of a pattern counts against `parser_nesting` from zero at its start, the same `SyntaxError`, an early error of the script for a literal, never a `RangeError`); decision 2 (`swell-syntax` depends on `swell-regexp` from this story).
