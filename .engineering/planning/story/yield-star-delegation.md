## Context

Slice 7, third wave (`epic:slice-07-generators`). `yield*` delegates a generator to another iterator (YieldExpression : `yield *` AssignmentExpression, `#sec-generator-function-definitions-runtime-semantics-evaluation`). It depends on `story:generator-functions-and-suspended-frames`, which gives it the suspended frame, the resumption as `pub(crate)` functions (the checks with the move, and the run in a loop of its own), GeneratorValidate, GeneratorResume and GeneratorResumeAbrupt as `pub(crate)` functions of `builtins/generator.rs` for this story, and the refusal it lifts. The consumer of the slice needs slice 10, not this story.

On its base (the join after the second story), `yield *` parses with its early errors (`story:generator-syntax-and-early-errors`), and a script that holds one ends after them on `yield* is not supported before slice 7`, held back from the `*` by `crates/syntax/src/parser/expression.rs` (the function of YieldExpression); the compiler answers `CompileError::Tree` for `Expr::Yield` with `delegate`. The refusal this story removes is that text. The search for what expects it, `grep -rn 'yield\* is not supported\|"yield\*"' crates conformance docs/design taskfiles tests website/docs`, prints on its base the record in `parser/expression.rs`, line 1 of `crates/syntax/tests/sources/refused/yield-star.js` and the test `the_refusal_of_yield_star_is_counted` of `crates/swell/tests/generators.rs`; after it, nothing. No fixture of the runner holds `yield*`, so no line of `crates/test262` moves.

The list is `docs/plan/slice-07/story-yield-star.cases.txt`: the 49 cases of the key `yield-star` of `docs/plan/slice-07/scan.tsv` (the head of `scan.awk` gives the command), 98 runs, all failing on `4971b9b` on `generator function is not supported before slice 7`. By directory: `language/expressions/yield` 41, `language/statements/for-of` 4, `language/statements/generators` 2, `language/expressions/generators` 2. 3 are negative parse cases that the first story already passes, each a grammar error that now comes before the held refusal (read from their sources; no run of a join exists yet): `language/statements/generators/yield-star-after-newline.js`, `language/expressions/generators/yield-star-after-newline.js` and `language/expressions/yield/star-in-iteration-stmt.js`, 6 runs. So the checks count F, the cases of the list with a failing run in the base run, not the size of the list. A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit of the list file on the wave branch whose body names that part; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

Design it builds on: `docs/design/engine.md`, decision 3, "Suspended frames", item 6, its paragraph on `yield*` (a delegating frame resumes a generator of any realm of the agent by an instruction on its own run loop, so a chain of n generators holds n frames, counted against `frame_depth`, takes no Rust frame and no re-entry per level, and spends 2 to 10 steps per level and `next`), item 4 (the `RangeError` at the `yield*` that would push past `frame_depth`) and the paragraph after item 6 ("A step stands for a bounded amount of work on these paths too"); "The iterator of `for`-`of`, of an array pattern and of a spread", item 1 (the Iterator Record as `Entry::Iterator` on the operand stack, `crates/swell/src/interp/frame.rs` line 48).

## What slice 10 reuses

Nothing directly: `yield*` in an async generator (slice 10) delegates through `%AsyncFromSyncIteratorPrototype%` and `await`, by another path. The loop of a delegation compiled as instructions of the delegating frame is the form slice 10's `yield*` follows.

## Domain relations

- Delegating generator → the iterator it delegates to: one to one for the duration of one `yield*`; the delegating frame holds the Iterator Record on its operand stack and drops it when the delegation ends — inferable (inferred from `crates/swell/src/interp/frame.rs:48`, `Entry::Iterator`, and `docs/design/engine.md`, decision 3, "Suspended frames", item 6; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").
- Chain of delegating generators → frames on the frame stack: one frame per level while the innermost runs, no Rust frame per level — inferable (inferred from the same paragraph of item 6 and `crates/swell/src/interp/mod.rs:62`, the frame stack; no ess/1 document declares it).

- `swell`. `interp/yield_star.rs` (new): the delegation step (`#sec-generator-function-definitions-runtime-semantics-evaluation`, the steps of `yield *`). For a normal completion: when the [[NextMethod]] of the record is `%GeneratorPrototype%.next` of any realm of the agent (`Behaviour::Builtin` of its builtin function, `object/builtin_function.rs` line 86) and the iterator is a generator object, GeneratorValidate with the `TypeError` of that function's realm, then the state branches of GeneratorResume and GeneratorResumeAbrupt (`builtins/generator.rs`, `pub(crate)` since the second story for this caller: a completed generator answers `{ value: undefined, done: true }` with no frame moved, an abrupt completion completes a suspended-start one), and for a suspended generator the checks and the move of a resumption (`interp/suspend.rs`, the second story), so the inner frame runs above the delegating one on the same run loop and its suspension or its return hands the inner result to the delegating frame; otherwise Call of [[NextMethod]], a re-entry as the step of a `for`-`of`. For a throw: GetMethod(iterator, "throw") (`ops/objects.rs` line 65); `%GeneratorPrototype%.throw` and a generator object resume in the loop with the throw; `undefined` closes the iterator (IteratorClose, `ops/iteration.rs` line 197) and throws a `TypeError`; another function is called. For a return: GetMethod(iterator, "return"); `undefined` answers done with the received value; `%GeneratorPrototype%.return` and a generator object resume in the loop with the return; another function is called. A result that is not an Object throws a `TypeError`. IteratorComplete (`ops/iteration.rs` line 129) becomes `pub(crate)` for the test of `done`. `interp/dispatch.rs`: one arm per new instruction in `execute` (line 243), each calling a function of `yield_star.rs`; `interp/mod.rs`: the `mod` line.
- Bounds, as item 6 states them: a chain of n generators each delegating to the next holds n frames on the frame stack while the innermost runs, counted against `frame_depth`, past which the `RangeError` of item 4 is thrown at the `yield*` that would push; no Rust frame and no re-entry per level; per `next`, each level spends at least 2 and at most 10 steps (the step of its resumption and the instructions of one turn of the loop), `return` and `throw` the same with the Get of the method; a level reads one property and moves one frame, and nothing walks the chain. A termination inside a chain leaves every level whose frame is on the stack `executing` (item 5). `STACK_BYTES_PER_REENTRY` (`host/limits.rs` line 46, 14 KiB) does not change: the delegation adds no re-entry, and a path that needs more stack is taken apart, not paid for by the constant.
