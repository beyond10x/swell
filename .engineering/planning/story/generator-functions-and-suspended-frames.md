## What slice 10 reuses

`interp/suspend.rs` whole: the type of a suspended frame with its trace and its credit, the move of the running frame off the frame stack and back, the resumption with a normal, throw or return completion at the saved instruction, its bounds (a resumption from Rust is a re-entry; `frame_depth`; the steps charged to the entry that resumes) and what a termination leaves (Change, "Bounds"). The compiled resumption after a suspension point (`JumpIfTag` and `EndFinally` after the suspending instruction, the return path through `exit_return`). The form in which a suspending frame hands its resumer what the resumption answers, by instructions of its own code and a `finally` around its body, with nothing added to `return_from_frame` or `unwind`. Not reused: the generator object, `%GeneratorFunction.prototype%`, `%GeneratorPrototype%` and the iterator results.

## Domain relations

- Generator object → suspended frame: one to zero or one; the object owns the frame; the frame exists from the start instruction until the generator completes (popped and credited) or the sweep that frees the object drops it, and it outlives the entry that made it — inferable (inferred from `crates/swell/src/interp/frame.rs:110`, the `Frame` that an entry of the frame stack is and that moves whole, and `crates/swell/src/interp/mod.rs:62`, the frame stack it leaves; ECMA-262 `#sec-properties-of-generator-instances`, [[GeneratorContext]]; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the slots of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").
- Running generator frame → its generator object: one to one while it runs; the frame references the object through a local slot its code reserves and does not own it — inferable (inferred from `crates/swell/src/interp/frame.rs:121` and `:217`, the locals and their trace; no ess/1 document declares it, for the same reason).
- Suspended generator → the entry that made it: no coupling; a termination of that entry leaves the generator and its frame as they were — inferable (inferred from `crates/swell/src/interp/mod.rs:213`, `drop_frames`, which drops only the frames on the stack, and `docs/design/host-interface.md` section 10, "every object keeps the state it had"; no ess/1 document declares it).
