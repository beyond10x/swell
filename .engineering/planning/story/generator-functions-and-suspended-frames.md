## What slice 10 reuses

`interp/suspend.rs` whole: the box of a suspended frame with the guard beside it in its holder, its trace at the holder's kind site and its credit through the agent's queue; the move of the running frame off the frame stack into its holder and back; the resumption with a normal, throw or return completion at the saved instruction, its checks in the order of item 5 (`frame_depth`, the step, `reentry_depth` and the interrupt, then the move) and what a termination leaves; the hand-over of a suspension's value as a return hands its value over; the resumption from Rust as one re-entry. The compiled resumption after a suspension point (`JumpIfTag` and `EndFinally` after the suspending instruction, the return path through `exit_return`), and the `finally` around a body that marks its end. Not reused: the generator object, `%GeneratorFunction.prototype%`, `%GeneratorPrototype%` and the iterator results.

## Domain relations

- Generator object → suspended frame: one to zero or one; the object holds the frame in a box in its kind and owns it; the frame exists from the call that makes it until the generator completes (popped and credited), a termination drops it while it runs, or the sweep that frees the object drops it, and it outlives the entry that made it — inferable (inferred from `crates/swell/src/interp/frame.rs:110`, the `Frame` that an entry of the frame stack is and that moves whole, `crates/swell/src/interp/mod.rs:62`, the frame stack it leaves, and `docs/design/engine.md`, decision 3, "Suspended frames", item 2; ECMA-262 `#sec-properties-of-generator-instances`, [[GeneratorContext]]; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the slots of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").
- Running generator frame → its generator object: one to one while it runs; the frame references the object through a local slot its code reserves and does not own it — inferable (inferred from `crates/swell/src/interp/frame.rs:121` and `:217`, the locals and their trace, and "Suspended frames", item 2; no ess/1 document declares it, for the same reason).
- Suspended generator → the entry that made it: no coupling; a termination of any entry leaves a suspended generator and its frame as they were — inferable (inferred from `crates/swell/src/interp/mod.rs:213`, `drop_frames`, which drops only the frames on the stack, "Suspended frames", item 5, and `docs/design/host-interface.md` section 10; no ess/1 document declares it).

- Bounds, as "Suspended frames" states them. Steps (item 5): each new instruction is a step; a resumption spends the step of a call beside the step of the call of `next`, `return` or `throw`; the instructions a resumed frame runs are steps of the entry that resumed it, also when an earlier entry made the generator; a resumption reads the step budget and the interrupt as a call does, a suspension as a return does. Depth (item 4): a suspended frame is not on the frame stack and takes no place against `frame_depth`; `next`, `return` and `throw` of a suspended-yield generator and `next` of a suspended-start one are checked as a call; an abrupt completion given to a suspended-start generator and a call on a completed one are not. Re-entry (item 6): a resumption from Rust (`next`, `return` and `throw` called from code, the step of a `for`-`of`, a spread, an array pattern, IteratorClose, a call of the host) is one re-entry; `STACK_BYTES_PER_REENTRY` (`host/limits.rs` line 46, 14 KiB) and the stack tests (`limits_scripts`, `budgets`, `eval_stack`) do not change and pass on seven eighths of `min_stack_bytes()`; a resumption path that needs more is taken apart, as the run loop was, and the unit reports its bytes per re-entry before and after.
