## Context

Slice 12 brings `BigInt` (`epic:slice-12-bigint`). This story brings the relational operators `<`, `>`, `<=` and `>=` and the loose equality `==` and `!=` with a BigInt operand: IsLessThan (`#sec-islessthan`, steps 3 and 4: a String against a BigInt through StringToBigInt, a BigInt against a Number exactly, NaN and the infinities) and IsLooselyEqual (`#sec-islooselyequal`, steps 6 to 13: a BigInt against a String through StringToBigInt, against a Number by its mathematical value, against a Boolean through ToNumber of the Boolean, against an Object through ToPrimitive). It is in order 3, built at the same time as `story:bigint-arithmetic-and-bitwise-operators` and `story:bigint-constructor-and-prototype`; it depends on `story:bigint-values-and-literals` (the record and its reader, the charges of `ops/bigint.rs`) and through it on `story:swell-num-bigint-core` (`cmp_f64`, `cmp`, `string_to_bigint`).

Measured by the decomposition on `f962c324` with the full run of that commit (`test262-slice 12 381 760 0 0 381 39 342 0`): no BigInt can be made, so every case fails on the refused literal. The list of this story, `docs/plan/slice-12/story-compare.cases.txt`, holds the 42 cases of the key `compare` of `docs/plan/slice-12/scan.tsv`, all failing, 84 runs, every one on "BigInt literal is not supported before slice 12": `language/expressions/less-than` 8, `greater-than` 8, `equals` 7, `does-not-equals` 7, `less-than-or-equal` 6, `greater-than-or-equal` 6. The two cases `equals/bigint-and-object.js` and `does-not-equals/bigint-and-object.js` compare with a wrapper object (`Object(1n)`), whose ToPrimitive reads `BigInt.prototype.valueOf`; they are on the list of `story:bigint-json-number-and-joined-cases`. A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit on the wave branch whose body names that part and adds it, in the same commit, to `docs/plan/slice-12/close-later.tsv` (path, the story that takes it, one reason), which `sh docs/plan/slice-12/lists.sh` counts as a list; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

No host requirement of slice 12 is this story's.

Line numbers in this body are those of `f962c324`; every anchor is found by its name (a function, an arm, a test).

## Change

Failing tests first: the tests below are written before the code and fail on the base of this story (the comparison of a BigInt with a Number answers through ToNumber, which throws a `TypeError`, and its loose equality answers `false` for every pair of a BigInt and another type, `ops/testing.rs` lines 152 to 193).

- `crates/swell/src/ops/testing.rs`, `is_loosely_equal` (lines 152 to 193 on `f962c324`): new arms inside its `match (x, y)`, after the arm `(_, Value::Boolean(_))` and before the arms of an Object: `(Value::BigInt, Value::String)` and its mirror (step 6 and 7 of the clause: StringToBigInt of the String, `undefined` answers `false`, otherwise the BigInts compared by IsLooselyEqual, which `is_strictly_equal` answers), and `(Value::BigInt, Value::Number)` and its mirror (step 13: `false` for NaN and ±∞, otherwise `cmp_f64` is `Equal`); the arms of an Object with a BigInt already convert the Object (lines 170 to 189) and stay. `is_less_than` (lines 195 to 232): after the branch of two Strings, steps 3 and 4 of the clause: a BigInt against a String (StringToBigInt of the String; `undefined` answers `None`), a BigInt against a BigInt (`cmp`), a BigInt against a Number (`cmp_f64`; NaN answers `None`, ±∞ compare as the clause says), and a Number against a BigInt; ToNumber stays for the rest. Each comparison of two BigInts or of a BigInt with a Number charges a step per limb read past the first, before it reads them (`ops/bigint.rs` of `story:bigint-values-and-literals`). StringToBigInt is a private function of `ops/testing.rs` that this story writes, called by both clauses: a step per code unit of the String charged before its units are read, then `swell_num::bigint::string_to_bigint` (`None` is `undefined` to the clause), its result a temporary `swell_num` value, not a heap record: before the value is computed, `bigint_check_bits` of the lower bound `from_radix_digits_bits` gives for the digits after their leading zeros (the `RangeError` before computing; a String that is no `StringIntegerLiteral` answers `undefined` whatever its length, so the form is read before the limit), then its limbs and scratch charged by `bigint_charge` of `from_radix_digits_cost`, credited when the comparison returns. `story:bigint-constructor-and-prototype`, built at the same time, makes its own call of `swell_num::bigint::string_to_bigint` for ToBigInt; neither reads the other's.
