## Context

The epic asks every run of slice 3 to pass, and a case that needs a later slice is moved by a rule line of the `swell-slices` block with its reason, in the change that moves both slices' counts, each move with its evidence in `conformance/test262/moves.tsv` (`AGENTS.md`, "A case that cannot pass in its slice"; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12). This story makes that move for slice 3 once every builtin of the slice exists, as `story:slice-02-conformance-close` did for slice 2, and it owns the block, its paragraph and its table for these lines. It also writes, in the same change, the lines of the two lists that three stories of the wave would otherwise each edit: the four requirements of the host interface that slice 3 closes (`conformance/host/requirements.txt`) and the omissions of the sections the slice touches (`conformance/test262/sections.txt`). It writes no engine file.

Measured by the decomposition on the base (`309b850`; the run lines of `slot task test262` on it, whose line reads `test262-slice 03 7635 15074 0 0 7635 2762 4873 0`: the epic's 7,580 cases and 14,992 runs predate the moves of slices 1 and 2), as an estimate before the code: of the 4,873 failing cases of slice 3 a static scan of each case and the harness files it includes (comments removed, string contents blanked, read for the names of the parts of later slices; the slice a refusal of the parser names in the first failed run; the directories whose method is a later slice's) finds 620 that need a later slice (`docs/plan/slice-03/move-estimate.tsv`: path, slice, evidence; `awk -F'\t' '{ c[$2]++ } END { for (k in c) print k, c[k] }'` over it): to slice 4: 202 (`Date` 138, a member of `Math` other than `max` 53, `parseInt` and `parseFloat` 8, `toFixed` 3); to 5: 133 (`RegExp` and regular expression literals 107, text outside ASCII for case mapping and `localeCompare` 13, `normalize` 9, `match`, `replace`, `split` 4); to 6: 224 (later syntax 140: arrow functions, `let`, `const`, templates, spread, `for-of`, a default parameter, a computed name, 16 of them only in `nativeFunctionMatcher.js`; `Array.from` 21; `Object.fromEntries` and `Object.groupBy` 23; the Array iterator methods 16; `Symbol` 24); to 7: 3; to 8: 18 (`class`); to 9: 17 (`Proxy`, `Reflect`, `Map`, `Set`); to 10: 6; to 12: 3; to 13: 14 (typed arrays). 4,253 failing cases stay: 4,050 name the builtins of one story of the wave, 168 those of two or three, 35 none the scan knows (`docs/plan/slice-03/cross-story.tsv`).
