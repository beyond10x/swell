## Context

Slice 10 brings jobs, `Promise`, `async` functions and generators and `for await` (`epic:slice-10-promises-and-async`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 10). The first wave of the slice is the smallest set of stories after which the one `async` function of the first consumer, `fetch_json` of surf's `resources/testharness.js` (lines 5032 to 5039 of the downloaded files of `docs/plan/waves.md`, "The order after slice 6": two `await` on promises of the host), runs to its end in an embedder: `story:job-queue-and-microtask-checkpoint` and this story side by side, then `story:promise-constructor-then-and-jobs`, then `story:async-functions-and-await`. It needs no open story of slice 7 and no item of "Suspended frames" (decision 3).

On the base (`397ffff`) the parser refuses at the token: `async function` in a statement (`crates/syntax/src/parser/statement.rs` lines 261 to 265), `async function` and `async <name>` in `primary_kind` (`crates/syntax/src/parser/expression.rs` lines 1023 to 1036, beside `await` before an operand: `await expression`), an `async` call before `=>` (`crates/syntax/src/parser/function.rs`, `arrow_refusal`, lines 573 to 584), `for await` (`statement.rs`, `for_head`, lines 701 and 702: `await expression`), and an `async` method (`expression.rs`, `accessor_kind`, lines 1473 to 1482), each with `<construct> is not supported before slice 10`. ## Domain relations

- AwaitExpression → the `async` function whose body holds it: many to one; an AwaitExpression stands in the body of an `async` function or arrow, never in its parameters, never in a plain function or a generator inside it, and in a script never outside one — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-async-function-definitions` and `#sec-async-function-definitions-static-semantics-early-errors`, and from `crates/syntax/src/ast.rs:675`, the `Function` node that holds its body; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the grammar of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").

- `async` function declaration → the scope that binds it: as a function declaration does, a var-scoped hoisted binding at the top level of a function or a script and a lexical one in a block or a `case` block, with no Annex B binding (B.3.2 and B.3.3 name a plain FunctionDeclaration only) — inferable (inferred from `crates/syntax/src/early.rs`, the frames of the lexical names of a block, and ECMA-262 `#sec-block-static-semantics-early-errors`; no ess/1 document declares it, for the same reason).
