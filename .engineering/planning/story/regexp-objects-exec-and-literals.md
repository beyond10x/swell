## Context

After `story:regexp-literal-early-errors` and `story:regexp-compiler-and-backtracking-matcher` a pattern parses and matches in `swell-regexp` and a literal is checked at parse, but evaluating a literal still throws the refusal (`crates/compiler/src/expression.rs` line 281 emits `ThrowSyntaxError` of `REGEXP_REFUSAL`, `crates/syntax/src/error.rs` line 26), and `RegExp` is not defined: 843 runs over all slices end on `RegExp is not defined` on `e444aaf` (741 of slice 5, 68 of 19, 24 of 9, 10 of 18). This story brings the RegExp objects: the constructor, `%RegExp.prototype%` but its methods keyed by well-known symbols, RegExpExec and RegExpBuiltinExec, and the literal evaluated to a new object at each evaluation; it closes HOST-143 and HOST-191 and holds the acceptance 5 of the epic (`/(a+)+$/` against 10,000 `a` followed by `!` ends with `Terminated(StepBudget)`).

Lifts of the consumer (`docs/plan/slice-05/consumer-regexps.tsv`): after this story a literal of each of the 12 constructs of the table evaluates to a RegExp whose `source` and `flags` answer, which the test `the_constructs_of_the_consumer_table_evaluate` below shows with literals written for it of the constructs of each row (nothing of the downloaded files is copied); the call of row 2 answers (`RegExp.prototype.test`, `resources/testharness.js` line 584), and so do the two constructions of a RegExp from a String (`testharness.js` lines 1389 and 4817) and the calls of `test` on lines 2025, 4822 and 4827. The other rows call `match`, `replace` and `split` with a regular expression (`story:regexp-symbol-methods-and-string-match-search`); until that story `replace`, `replaceAll` and `split` given a RegExp end in a counted refusal, never in a String answer (Change).

The list of this story is `docs/plan/slice-05/story-objects-exec.cases.txt`: 927 failing cases (1,853 runs) of slice 5 that need no part of a later story of the slice, found by `docs/plan/slice-05/scan.awk` (its head gives the command) over the first failing run of each failing case of slice 5 on `e444aaf`. By directory: `built-ins/RegExp/` (its files and the subdirectories not named here) 462, `RegExp/prototype/exec` 75, `built-ins/Object` 69, `RegExp/prototype/test` 43, `language/literals/regexp` 27, `language/white-space` 26, `RegExp/regexp-modifiers` 24 (`m` and `s` modifiers and the errors of modifiers through the constructor), `RegExp/prototype/flags` 13, `language/statementList` 12, `RegExp/CharacterClassEscapes` 12, `RegExp/prototype/source` 11, `RegExp/match-indices` 11, the rest, 142 cases, in directories of 10 cases or fewer, among them the four `S15.5.4.1[6-9]_A1_T14.js` of `String/prototype/to(Locale)?(Lower|Upper)Case`, whose text is ASCII and which call a case method on a RegExp. Four of the 462 (`built-ins/RegExp/syntax-err-arithmetic-modifiers-*.js`) carry a modifier `i` in a pattern that fails its parse, which comes before the compile step refuses `i`. On the base 470 of them end on a counted refusal (the literal) and 274 on `RegExp is not defined`. A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit of the list file on the wave branch whose body names that part; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

Design it builds on: decision 7 of `docs/design/engine.md` with its paragraphs "How `swell-regexp` charges its work" (item 2: the implementation over the agent; item 3: positions are code unit indices, nothing converts), "What one step of the matcher is" (RegExpInitialize charges one step per code unit of the pattern before ParsePattern), "Where a RegExp object keeps its program" (`ObjectKind::RegExp`), "A regular expression literal: checked at parse time, compiled at each evaluation" (rule 2), "No Rust recursion in the parser and the compiler of a pattern" and "Refusals until the later stories of slice 5"; decision 3 ("The step budget", "Depth bounds": the pattern of the constructor past `parser_nesting` is a `SyntaxError` the script catches, HOST-143); section 12, "A part before its slice", the third form as named for RegExpInitialize; `docs/design/host-interface.md`, decision 13, "A refusal raised by `RegExp`" (HOST-191).
