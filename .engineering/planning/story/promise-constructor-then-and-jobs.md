## Domain relations

- Agent → promise jobs: one to many, owned; a job lives from HostEnqueuePromiseJob until the checkpoint dequeues it, across entries, a root while queued — declared (`spec/domains/host.yaml:165`, the relation `jobs` of the agent, `owns`, many; `:340`, `swell.host.Job`, its `realm_id` optional for a promise job, `:351`, and its lifecycle `Enqueued` → `Ran`, `:364`). The model has no state for a job a termination dequeued without running it; the report names the gap.

- Promise → its reactions: one to many, owned while the promise is pending; on settlement each becomes a job and the promise holds none — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-properties-of-promise-instances`, [[PromiseFulfillReactions]] and [[PromiseRejectReactions]], and `#sec-triggerpromisereactions`; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the slots of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").

- Resolving functions → their promise: two to one, sharing one [[AlreadyResolved]] record — inferable (inferred from `#sec-createresolvingfunctions` and `crates/swell/src/object/builtin_function.rs:83`, the slots of a built-in function; no ess/1 document declares it, for the same reason).
