## Context

The slice counts say when a case can first pass, not that it will (`docs/design/engine.md`, section 12). The epic asks every run of slice 2 to pass, and `AGENTS.md` ("A case that cannot pass in its slice") moves a case that needs a later slice by a rule line of the `swell-slices` block with its reason, in the change that moves both slices counts. This story makes that move for slice 2, once every part of the slice exists, as `story:test262-run-path` did for slice 1; it owns the block for these lines (the 23 lines of `test/harness` are `story:eval-and-function-constructor`, which the `Function` decision ties to that change).

Measured by the decomposition on the base (`6319af2`), as an estimate before the code: of the 5,245 cases of slice 2, 2,125 pass; of the 3,120 that fail, a static scan of each case and its includes (comments removed; string contents blanked for the builtins, kept for the syntax, since `eval` reads strings) together with the first cause of its first failed run finds about 1,000 that need a later slice once the other stories of the slice are in: 964 by the scan (by target slice: 3: 86, 4: 87, 5: 122, 6: 474, 7: 50, 8: 85, 9: 2, 10: 52, 11: 4, 12: 2; later syntax such as `let`, `const`, arrows, classes, generators, default and rest parameters, templates, spread, identifiers outside ASCII, regular expression literals; later builtins such as `Object` statics, `globalThis`, `parseInt`, `Math` beyond `max`, `Date`, `RegExp`, `Symbol`; 11 of the 13 cases that call `$262.evalScript`, `story:test262-run-causes`) and 34 more of the 42 cases with the feature `exponentiation` (one line `feature exponentiation 4`, which also moves 24 cases of slice 3). After `story:eval-and-function-constructor` slice 2 holds 5,267 cases, so about 4,269 stay. The 45 cases whose only later part was a plain method or a shorthand property stay in slice 2 (`story:syntax-language-core` takes both). The decomposition lists are under `.out/decompose-slice-02/` of the coordinator worktree (`s2bucket.tsv`, `move-estimate.tsv`); this story measures again with the per-run file of `story:test262-run-causes`.

A failing case of slice 2 that needs no later slice is a defect of the story that owns the failing part (`story:syntax-language-core`, `story:number-boolean-and-global-functions-early`, `story:with-arguments-and-dynamic-names`, `story:eval-and-function-constructor`, `story:test262-run-causes` for `$262`), which the coordinator resumes; it is never moved. A case that passes is never moved. This story writes no engine file.

Every move is checked case by case, so that the acceptance cannot be met by moving whatever fails. Two checks, each where it can hold: the gate keeps checking, on a fresh clone of any later commit, what the committed files say (every line of `moves.tsv` corresponds to a rule line of the block and names evidence present in the case or in `later-parts.tsv`, and no line moves a case into slice 2 or out of slices 1 to 19); what needs a run (every moved case of slice 2 fails on the base of this story, and none fails first on a part of slice 2) is this story's acceptance, run once by the unit and quoted in its report. Measured on the design as it is (`awk` over the count lines after "Its output on this document"): slices 1 to 19 hold 44,931 cases and 85,794 runs; every move stays within slices 2 to 12, so those two numbers do not change.

Design it builds on: none beyond what the stories it depends on build on; the `swell-slices` block, its table and its paragraph of groups are this story's (section 12 lets a story own the block).

## Change

Failing check first: the gate line `test262-slice 02` with failed runs is the failing state, and the planted script below fails before the task exists; the story ends when the line reads every run passed and the check passes.

- Measure: before the change, `slot task test262 TEST262_OUT=.out/test262-base` (the base run, kept for the acceptance), then the failed runs of slice 2 from `.out/test262-base/runs.tsv`. For each failing case, the slice it needs: the highest of the slice a refusal names, the slice of every later-slice construct and builtin in the case and its includes (the scan, with its tokens in `conformance/test262/later-parts.tsv`), and a reading of the case where the scan is ambiguous (a `.toString()` or `.valueOf()` whose receiver it cannot tell). A case whose only failure is a part of slice 2 goes back to its owner and the measurement is repeated after the fix.
- `conformance/test262/later-parts.tsv` (new): one line per token of the scan, `<token><TAB><slice><TAB><item>`: the text the scan looks for (a construct such as `=>` or `class`, a builtin such as `Object.defineProperties` or `Symbol`), the slice that builds it and the item of "What each slice builds" or the refusal that says so.
- `conformance/test262/moves.tsv` (new): one line per case whose slice the change moves, `<path><TAB><from><TAB><to><TAB><kind><TAB><evidence>`, sorted by path; `kind` is `part` (the evidence is a token of `later-parts.tsv` whose slice is `<to>`, present in the case or one of its includes: a construct whose refusal stopped the run is named by its token, such as `let` for `let declaration is not supported before slice 6`, and so is a construct a counted refusal of `eval` or `Function` met) or `feature` (a feature of the case that a `feature <name> <to>` line of the block names); the evidence is the one that sets the target, the highest slice the case needs.
- `taskfiles/test262.yml`: a task `test262-moves-check` (the summary names it) that reads only committed files and the pinned checkout: `TEST262_MOVES` (default `conformance/test262/moves.tsv`), `TEST262_LATER_PARTS` (default `conformance/test262/later-parts.tsv`), the `swell-slices` block and the count lines of `TEST262_DESIGN`, and the case sources and harness files of the checkout of `TEST262_CACHE`/`TEST262_COMMIT`; never `.cache/inventory/`, never a `runs.tsv`, never another commit. It fails, naming the line, when: a `to` is 2 or not a slice from 1 to 19 (no move into an excluded slice); the slice the block gives the case (its longest `dir` prefix raised by the block's `feature` and `flag` lines, the features and flags read from the case's front matter in the checkout) is not `to`; a `part` evidence is not a token of `later-parts.tsv` with slice `to`, or `grep -F` finds it neither in the case nor in a harness file its front matter includes; a `feature` evidence is not among the case's `features:` or the block has no `feature <evidence> <to>` line; a line of `later-parts.tsv` names slice 1, 2 or one outside 3 to 19. It prints `test262-moves: <n> case(s) moved, <count per target slice as to:count>; slices 1 to 19: <cases> <runs>`.
- `tests/gate/test262-moves-check-refuses-a-line-without-evidence.sh` (new, a planted-defect script that `gate-selftest` runs): on a copy of `moves.tsv` under `.out/gate-tests/`, the unchanged copy passes; a copy in which one `part` evidence is replaced by a token the case does not hold fails naming that path; a copy with one `to` set to 2 fails naming that path.
- `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12: the rule lines of the block, one per directory when every case of slice 2 in it moves to one slice, one per file otherwise, and `feature exponentiation 4`; beside the block, a paragraph with each group (by target slice and kind), its count and its reason, as slice 1 has one, the counts summing to the number of lines of `moves.tsv`; the table and the block after "Its output on this document" with the new counts of every slice the lines touch, the 22 lines still summing to 53,801 cases and 103,360 runs and slices 1 to 19 to 44,931 and 85,794; the sentence on slice 2 under the table, with the command that measured it (`awk -f slices.awk` over the block and the inventory, counting slice `02`).
- `conformance/test262/expected.txt`: the lines of the run, in the same change.

Writes: `docs/design/engine.md` (section 12 as listed), `conformance/test262/moves.tsv` (new), `conformance/test262/later-parts.tsv` (new), `taskfiles/test262.yml`, `tests/gate/test262-moves-check-refuses-a-line-without-evidence.sh` (new), `conformance/test262/expected.txt`.
