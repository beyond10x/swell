## What later stories reuse

## Domain relations

- Async generator object → its suspended frame: one to zero or one, owned in a box, from AsyncGeneratorStart until it completes, a termination drops the frame, or the sweep that frees the object drops it — inferable (inferred from `crates/swell/src/interp/suspend.rs:52`, `SuspendedFrame`, and `crates/swell/src/object/generator.rs`, the generator's kind; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the slots of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").

- Async generator object → its requests: one to many, owned, in the order of the calls, each with its completion and its capability, removed when completed — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-asyncgeneratorrequest-records` and `#sec-properties-of-asyncgenerator-intances`, [[AsyncGeneratorQueue]]; no ess/1 document declares it, for the same reason).
