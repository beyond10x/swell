## Context

Slice 10 brings jobs, `Promise`, `async` functions and generators and `for await` (`epic:slice-10-promises-and-async`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 10). This story compiles and runs async generators (`#sec-asyncgenerator-objects`): AsyncGeneratorStart, `yield` (AsyncGeneratorYield, with the `await` of the operand and of a `return`), `await`, `%AsyncGeneratorPrototype%` with `next`, `return` and `throw`, and the request queue (AsyncGeneratorRequest records, AsyncGeneratorEnqueue, AsyncGeneratorValidate, AsyncGeneratorCompleteStep, AsyncGeneratorResumeNext as AsyncGeneratorDrainQueue and AsyncGeneratorAwaitReturn), on the suspended frame of slice 7 and the `await` of `story:async-functions-and-await`; it lifts the held refusal of `story:async-generator-syntax-and-early-errors`. It depends on that story and on `story:for-await-and-async-from-sync-iterator` (396 of its cases use `for await`, and `%AsyncIteratorPrototype%` is the [[Prototype]] of `%AsyncGeneratorPrototype%`); it needs no open story of slice 7 (the generator object, `interp/suspend.rs` and `builtins/generator.rs` are on `397ffff`).

Why it is not cut further: `next`, `return` and `throw` all enqueue a request and share the one drain of the queue, `return` adds AsyncGeneratorAwaitReturn to it, and a `yield` completes the request at the head; a story with `next` alone would leave the queue in a form the other two change, and the cases of each method run through the other two (a `for await` closes with `return`). The parts that can be shown alone are cut out: the syntax (`story:async-generator-syntax-and-early-errors`), `yield*` (`story:yield-star-in-async-generators`), methods (`story:async-generator-methods-in-object-literals`) and the constructor (`story:async-generator-function-constructor`).

The refusal this story removes: `async generator function is not supported before slice 10`, held by `story:async-generator-syntax-and-early-errors`. The text leaves the code. A `yield*` in an async generator gets a held refusal of its own text, `yield* in an async generator is not supported before slice 10`, at the `*` (recorded as every held refusal is; `story:yield-star-in-async-generators` lifts it), so that this story compiles no `yield*` of an async generator. The search for what expects the old text, `grep -rn 'async generator function is not supported\|"async generator function"\|async generator function` (slice 10)' crates conformance docs/design taskfiles tests website/docs`, prints on its base the records of the held refusal in `crates/syntax/src/parser/function.rs`, the documentation of `held` and of `parse_script`, line 1 of `crates/syntax/tests/sources/refused/{async-generator,async-generator-forms}.js`; after this story it prints nothing.

The list of this story is `docs/plan/slice-10/story-ag-functions.cases.txt`: the 1,162 cases of the key `ag-functions` of `docs/plan/slice-10/later.tsv` (an async generator with no `yield*`, no `async *` method and no `%AsyncGeneratorFunction%`, and the 56 negative parse cases with an async generator and `for await`; the command is in the head of `docs/plan/slice-10/scan-later.awk`). By directory: `language/statements/for-await-of` 445, `language/expressions/async-generator` 408, `language/statements/async-generator` 199, `built-ins/AsyncGeneratorPrototype` 47, `language/eval-code/direct` 36, `built-ins/Function` 3 (`Function.prototype.toString` of async generators, whose span starts at `async`), and the rest in directories of fewer cases; 56 are negative parse cases and 872 have the flag `async`. Design it builds on, by name (the text of the slice, merged at `843f178`): `docs/design/engine.md` decision 3, "Suspended frames", items 1 to 6 (a generator's frame in a box of its object; a resumption is a call; a suspended frame is no root), "`async` functions on suspended frames", items 2 to 5 (an `await` makes two functions with slots and goes through the hooks of a `then`; a resumed `await` has spent 3 steps; what a termination leaves), "The microtask checkpoint is one entry"; decision 4, "Queued jobs" and "A built-in function with slots". Three sentences are missing and are put to the design author as questions of this story, to be answered before it starts (the story follows the answers; the defaults are these): (1) the holder of an async generator's frame is its object, as for a generator (item 2 of "Suspended frames"), and the two functions of an `await` in it hold that object where those of an `async` function hold the activation (item 3 of "`async` functions on suspended frames" names the activation only); (2) the request queue lies in a box of the async generator kind, charged by capacity as it grows, as the reactions of a promise are ("Queued jobs"), so the record keeps its 200 bytes; (3) a termination while an async generator runs leaves it executing with no frame (credited) and the promise of the request at the head pending for good, and every later request settles nothing (the `async` function's item 5 applied to the generator, whose state stays executing so that AsyncGeneratorValidate never resumes it).

## What later stories reuse

The async generator object, its kind, its queue and the instructions of its start and its `yield`, which `yield*` in an async generator, `async *` methods and `%AsyncGeneratorFunction%` take as they are; `%AsyncGeneratorFunction.prototype%` and `%AsyncGeneratorPrototype%`, the `prototype` and the `constructor` that `story:async-generator-function-constructor` links.

## Domain relations

- Async generator object → its suspended frame: one to zero or one, owned in a box, from AsyncGeneratorStart until it completes, a termination drops the frame, or the sweep that frees the object drops it — inferable (inferred from `crates/swell/src/interp/suspend.rs:52`, `SuspendedFrame`, and `crates/swell/src/object/generator.rs`, the generator's kind; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the slots of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").

- Async generator object → its requests: one to many, owned, in the order of the calls, each with its completion and its capability, removed when completed — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-asyncgeneratorrequest-records` and `#sec-properties-of-asyncgenerator-intances`, [[AsyncGeneratorQueue]]; no ess/1 document declares it, for the same reason).
