## Context

Slice 4 of 19, `numbers-and-dates` (`epic:slice-04-numbers-and-dates`; `docs/design/engine.md` decision 6, bullet "`Math.random`", completed for slice 4 on the design branch). This story makes `Math.random` callable: the generator in `swell-num`, the state in each realm record derived from the agent's `random_seed` and the realm's creation index, the builtin, and the tests of six host requirements. It depends on `story:math-constants-and-exact-functions` (the `MathBuiltin` table it adds a variant to) and on `story:math-functions` (the same file, `builtins/math.rs`, and the same test module: this story comes after it so that the join of the two is a join of two arms and not of two rewrites).

On the base (`c6d44d1`; the unit reads every place named in this story again on its base) `Agent.random_seed` (in `crates/swell/src/host/agent.rs`) is stored and never read (`#[expect(dead_code, reason = "Math.random: slice 4")]`), `Math` has `max` only, `create_realm` (in `agent.rs`) counts nothing, and `RealmRecord` (in `crates/swell/src/realm/mod.rs`, made in its constructors and at its one struct literal) has no generator state. `crates/num/src/siphash.rs` has SipHash-2-4 with a 16-byte key.

The list is `docs/plan/slice-04/story-math-random.cases.txt`: the 6 cases of the key `math-random` of `docs/plan/slice-04/scan.tsv`: `built-ins/Math/random/` `S15.8.2.14_A1`, `length`, `name`, `prop-desc`; `built-ins/Object/getOwnPropertyDescriptor/15.2.3.3-4-109` and `language/expressions/property-accessors/S11.2.1_A4_T8` (both read `Math.random` as a function). By edge: all 6 read the exact functions (the edge to `story:math-constants-and-exact-functions`) and `S15.8.2.14_A1` and the two outside `built-ins/Math` read the functions of `story:math-functions` through `Math` (the edge to it). The guard is the 13 cases of `built-ins/Math` that pass on the base (`max`, `prop-desc`, `proto`, `Symbol.toStringTag`).

Design it follows, by name: `docs/design/engine.md` decision 6, bullet "`Math.random`" (the generator, the derivation of the state, the creation index, "What a test asserts"); decision 4 (the keyed hash, SipHash-2-4, in `swell-num`); `docs/design/host-interface.md` HOST-26, HOST-27, HOST-144, HOST-146, HOST-147, HOST-212 and HOST-213; ECMA-262 21.3.2.27.

## What later stories reuse

`swell_num::random::Xoshiro256` is used by no later story of slice 4. The agent's realm-creation index is a plain counter that no other code reads.

## Domain relations

- Agent -> realms: one to many, the index n counts every `create_realm` that starts, in order, and is never reused - inferable (inferred from `Agent::create_realm`). Realm -> its generator state: one to one, four `u64` words set when the record is made and moved only by a call of that realm's `Math.random` - inferable. Agent `random_seed` -> state of realm n: a pure function of both, by SipHash-2-4 - stated (decision 6, "`Math.random`").
