## Context

Slice 9 brings `Map`, `Set`, `WeakMap`, `WeakSet`, `Proxy` and `Reflect` (`epic:slice-09-collections-proxy-reflect`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 9). This story brings the Proxy exotic object (`#sec-proxy-object-internal-methods-and-internal-slots`): `ProxyCreate`, the constructor `Proxy` and `Proxy.revocable`, the object kind with [[ProxyTarget]] and [[ProxyHandler]], a Proxy arm in every internal method of the dispatch, and the traps `get`, `set`, `has`, `deleteProperty`, `apply` and `construct` with the invariants of `#sec-invariants-of-the-essential-internal-methods` their clauses check. The other seven internal methods ([[GetPrototypeOf]], [[SetPrototypeOf]], [[IsExtensible]], [[PreventExtensions]], [[GetOwnProperty]], [[DefineOwnProperty]], [[OwnPropertyKeys]]) get their Proxy arm here too, which checks for revocation and forwards to the target without reading the handler, until `story:proxy-descriptor-prototype-and-extensibility-traps` (order 3) replaces the forwarding with their traps; the cases of those seven traps are on that story's list. It is in order 2 and depends on `story:reflect-namespace` (cases of its list call `Reflect.*` inside traps; the key order of `docs/plan/slice-09/scan.awk`).

The engine assumes today that no Proxy exists: the walks of the prototype chain in `crates/swell/src/object/ordinary.rs` (OrdinaryHasProperty line 322, OrdinaryGet line 343, OrdinarySet line 374, each a loop that calls [[GetOwnProperty]] and [[GetPrototypeOf]] of each parent; the comment of line 14, "no proxy exists before slice 9"), IsArray (`crates/swell/src/ops/testing.rs` line 46 and the comment of line 3, "No Proxy exists before slice 9") and GetFunctionRealm (`crates/swell/src/ops/objects.rs` line 575, "Proxies (step 3) come with their story"). This story changes each: a walk that reaches a parent whose [[HasProperty]], [[Get]] or [[Set]] is not the ordinary one (a Proxy) calls that parent's internal method and answers its result, as the clauses' "Return ? parent.[[Get]](P, Receiver)" orders; IsArray and GetFunctionRealm follow [[ProxyTarget]] in their loop, a revoked Proxy a `TypeError`.

Design it builds on, by name: `docs/design/engine.md` decision 3 ("Depth bounds": "`JSON.stringify`, `toString` chains, `Proxy` trap chains: each step that calls script is a re-entry, bounded as one", line 803; "The step budget") and decision 4 ("Memory budget", "Collector"); decision 9, "The Rust stack" (`STACK_BYTES_PER_REENTRY`, 14 KiB, `crates/swell/src/host/limits.rs` line 46); `docs/design/host-interface.md` section 15 (the override checks are "the checks the `Proxy` internal methods use", which `story:host-object-overrides` reuses). ## What later stories reuse

The object kind `Proxy` (`object/proxy.rs`) and its arms in the dispatch, which `story:proxy-descriptor-prototype-and-extensibility-traps` fills for the seven remaining traps; `object/invariants.rs` with the checks of this story, which that story completes and `story:host-object-overrides` calls on an override's result; the walks of the chain that hand over to a parent's own method, which host objects with overrides use; the trap lookup `GetMethod(handler, name)` with its revocation check.

## Domain relations

- A Proxy → its target and its handler: many to one each (several proxies may share one target or handler), both set at creation, both cleared by revocation, never set again — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-proxycreate` and `#sec-proxy.revocable`; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the slots of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").
- A revoke function → its Proxy: one to zero or one, cleared at the first call — inferable (inferred from `#sec-proxy-revocation-functions`; no ess/1 document declares it, for the same reason).
