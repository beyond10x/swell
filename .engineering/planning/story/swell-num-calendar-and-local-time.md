## Context

Slice 4 of 19, `numbers-and-dates` (`epic:slice-04-numbers-and-dates`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 4, and decision 6, "numbers and dates, whole"; decision 2: `swell-num` depends on nothing). This story has no prerequisite of the design and no edge to wait for. It writes only `crates/num`, so it shares no file with an open story of slice 10; it shares the generated `crates/num/tests/main.rs` with the other stories that add a test file to `swell-num` (see Writes). It is the arithmetic that `story:date-object-constructor-now-utc-and-getters` (the zone story: the constructor with components, `Date.UTC`, the getters) reads, as pure functions over Numbers and integers, so that the calendar and the conversions are tested without an engine.

On the base (`c6d44d1`; the unit reads every place named in this story again on its base) `crates/num/src/date.rs` has `make_time`, `make_day`, `make_date`, `get_utc_epoch_nanoseconds` and the record `IsoDateTime` (year `i64`). Missing: Day, TimeWithinDay, DaysInYear, YearFromTime, MonthFromTime, DateFromTime, WeekDay, HourFromTime, MinuteFromTime, SecondFromTime, msFromTime, MakeFullYear (`#sec-makefullyear`, used by the constructor and `Date.UTC`: it has no owner on the base), LocalTime and UTC (21.4.1.3 to 21.4.1.31, `#sec-time-values-and-time-range`), and the reading of an identifier that is an offset string (`#sec-istimezoneoffsetstring`, `#sec-parsetimezoneoffsetstring`). `TimeClip` is not here: `story:date-object-clock-and-time-value` writes it once (its `new Date(value)` and `setTime` need it with no calendar). The hook record `IsoDateTime` of the engine (`crates/swell/src/host/hooks.rs`) has `year` as `i32` where `swell-num` has `i64`; `story:date-object-constructor-now-utc-and-getters` narrows it (see its Change), and no year of a valid time value is lost: a year of a time value, and of a local time at most one day beyond it, is at most 275,760 in magnitude. Nothing here narrows it.

Design it follows, by name (the numbers are the design's; this story states none of its own): `docs/design/engine.md` decision 6, the bullets "`MakeDay` far outside the time range" (NaN above a balanced year of 2^53 in magnitude; below it exact, no loop) and "`UTC ( t )` and the local time that does not exist" (at most 57 asks of the epoch hook, NaN when none is found within 2^28 ms, `t` more than 8.64 × 10^15 + 86,400,000 away answers NaN with no call); `docs/design/host-interface.md` decision 17, items 1 to 5 (the identifier read once per conversion; an offset string makes no offset or epoch call; the clamp to ±86,399,999,999,999 ns, HOST-209; the empty list for every local time, HOST-210; one step before every hook call, HOST-211); ECMA-262 `#sec-utc-t` with its note "The algorithm must not limit t to the time value range", `#sec-localtime`, `#sec-getnamedtimezoneepochnanoseconds`; decision 13 (no panic).

## What later stories reuse

`story:date-object-constructor-now-utc-and-getters` calls every function below; `story:date-setters` calls `make_*`, `local_time` and `utc_from_local`; `story:swell-num-date-text` and `story:date-text-formats` read `DateFields` and `parse_time_zone_offset_string`. The record `DateFields`, the two conversions and the offset functions are the whole interface; no engine type enters `swell-num`.

## Domain relations

- A time value → its fields: a pure function, no relation to an entity — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-year-number` to `#sec-ms-from-time`; no ess/1 document declares it).
- Agent → host time zone: one to one through the hooks `system_time_zone_identifier`, `get_named_time_zone_offset_nanoseconds` and `get_named_time_zone_epoch_nanoseconds` — declared as hooks (the three methods in `crates/swell/src/host/hooks.rs`), and as the trait of the entity `swell.host.Agent` (`spec/domains/host.yaml`, the entity `swell.host.Agent`, whose description says the clock, the time zone and the random bytes are "not stated at all": the relation is inferred from `hooks.rs`, with no declared form). This story reaches the hooks only through closures given by its caller.

## Change

Failing tests first: the tests below are written before the code and fail on the base (the functions do not exist: the test module does not compile).

- The fields. `crates/num/src/date.rs`: `pub struct DateFields { pub year: i64, pub month: u8 (0 to 11), pub date: u8 (1 to 31), pub weekday: u8 (0 is Sunday), pub hour: u8, pub minute: u8, pub second: u8, pub millisecond: u16 }` and `pub fn date_fields(t: f64) -> Option<DateFields>`: `None` for NaN, an infinity, a Number that is not integral, and a magnitude above `LOCAL_TIME_LIMIT` = 8.64 × 10^15 + 86,400,000 (the time range plus one day, as the design states; written as that expression, so that the story does not define `MAX_TIME_VALUE`, which `time_clip` of the clock story defines); else the fields of `t`: `t as i64` (exact: `t` is integral and below 2^63 after the tests, and the cast is the Rust reference rule, truncation toward zero), then `div_euclid` and `rem_euclid` of 86,400,000 for Day and TimeWithinDay (integer arithmetic: no `f64` division, since the quotient of a division in `f64` can round up to the next day), the civil-from-days method over eras of 146,097 days, in `i64`, which gives YearFromTime, MonthFromTime, DateFromTime, WeekDay, HourFromTime, MinuteFromTime, SecondFromTime and msFromTime in one decomposition. `pub const MS_PER_DAY: f64 = 86_400_000.0`.
- `pub fn make_full_year(year: f64) -> f64` (`#sec-makefullyear`): NaN for NaN; else `t = year.trunc() + 0.0`; 1900 + `t` when 0 ≤ `t` ≤ 99 (so -0.5 gives 1900), else `t` (the infinities stay). Its cases: NaN, ±∞, -0.5, -0, 0, 99, 99.9, 100, -1.
- `MakeDay` keeps its code. Its module header (the paragraph that says "not decided, and reported for slice 4") is rewritten as the design bullet says, and its test stands at a balanced year of 2^53 - 65 (finite: the first day of that year holds a Number) and 2^53 + 2 (NaN), in place of the boundary 2^53 exactly, for which `MakeDay(2^53, 0, 1)` is NaN.
- The offset of a zone. `pub fn offset_milliseconds(offset_ns: i128) -> i64`: the answer clamped to ±86,399,999,999,999 ns (HOST-209), then truncated toward zero to whole milliseconds (`/ 1_000_000` on integers); the clamp is here so that no caller can skip it. `pub fn parse_time_zone_offset_string(identifier: &str) -> Option<i128>`: `Some(ns)` when the identifier conforms to the offset format of `#sec-istimezoneoffsetstring` (a sign, an hour 00 to 23, optional minutes, seconds and a fraction of up to 9 digits; at most 19 code units: a longer one is a named zone by its length; the separator of the fraction is `.` or `,`; the sign is the ASCII `+` or `-` only) and the value `#sec-parsetimezoneoffsetstring` reads from it; `None` for a named zone. Its cases are the clause's own (`+01:00`, `-23:59:59.999999999`, `+24:00`, `+0100`, a 20-unit string, `UTC`, `Europe/Berlin`, the comma as the separator of a fraction (`+01:00:00,5` is `Some(3_600_500_000_000)`, as `+01:00:00.5` is) and the minus sign U+2212 (`-01:00` written with U+2212 is `None`: the grammar takes the ASCII sign only)).
- The conversions, with the host as closures, generic over the error the caller returns, so that a termination of the budget inside a hook call goes up unchanged. `pub fn local_time<E>(t: f64, identifier: &str, offset_ns: &mut dyn FnMut(&str, i128) -> Result<i128, E>) -> Result<f64, E>` (LocalTime, 21.4.1.25: NaN and the infinities answer themselves with no call; an offset-string identifier answers `t + offset_milliseconds(parsed)` with no call; else one call of `offset_ns` and `t + offset_milliseconds(answer)`) and `pub fn utc_from_local<E>(t: f64, identifier: &str, possible: &mut dyn FnMut(&str, &IsoDateTime) -> Result<Option<(i128, i128)>, E>, offset_ns: &mut dyn FnMut(&str, i128) -> Result<i128, E>) -> Result<f64, E>` (UTC, 21.4.1.26: a `t` that `date_fields` refuses answers NaN with no call; an offset-string identifier answers `t - offset_milliseconds(parsed)` with no call; else the fields of `t`, `possible` once, the first instant when the list is not empty, and for a skipped local time the search for `tBefore` below, then the last instant of that list, one call of `offset_ns`, and `t - offset_milliseconds(answer)`). The identifier is the one the caller read once for the conversion (the design: never cached, passed to both hooks as answered). Who holds the list of possible instants: the caller's closure alone, and `swell-num` holds none. `possible` answers `None` for an empty list and else the first and the last instant of the list, read before it returns; the closure charges 16 bytes per element when the host hands it the list, before it reads an element, and credits them when it drops the list, which is before it returns, so no list and no charge outlives the closure call and every charge is credited by the time the conversion ends (`docs/design/host-interface.md` decision 17, item 7: "credits them when it drops the list (the list kept for `tBefore` is dropped when the conversion ends)"; here the only thing kept for `tBefore` is the last instant, one `i128`, and the count of elements is the closure's to charge, so the signature needs no count).
- The search for `tBefore`, as the design bullet states it and this story builds it: a gallop below `t` at distances 1, 2, 4, ... 2^28 ms, then a bisection between the last empty probe and the first non-empty one; at most 57 asks of `possible` in all and one of `offset_ns` (the caller adds the identifier: 59 hook calls for one `UTC`, the design's count; this story states no other); none found within 2^28 ms answers NaN. A probe `t - 2^k` that falls below -`LOCAL_TIME_LIMIT`, where `date_fields` answers `None`, counts as a probe with no instant and costs no call; a test stands at the lower edge (`t` = -8.64e15 - 86,400,000 + 1 with a host that answers empty) and at the upper edge (`t` = 8.64e15 + 86,400,000), and a host that answers empty for every local time gets 30 asks of `possible` and NaN (the design's 31 calls with the identifier: HOST-210 is the engine test of this).
- Charges. No loop depends on a script value: `date_fields` is straight-line integer arithmetic and the search is bounded as stated, so `swell-num` charges nothing; the memory is the list the host answers, which the caller's closure charges and credits as stated above (`story:date-object-constructor-now-utc-and-getters` writes the closure); this crate allocates nothing: the closure hands back two `i128` and the stack record holds the rest.
- The `f64` operations and casts of this story, each with what fixes its result on every platform: `trunc` is exact (IEEE 754-2019, 5.9 roundToIntegral); the cast `t as i64` after the integral and range tests is the Rust reference rule (numeric casts, "Numeric cast": truncation toward zero, saturating, NaN to 0, none of which is reached after the tests); `i64 as f64` and `u8 as f64` of values below 2^53 are exact; `make_time` and `make_date` keep their IEEE `+` and `×` in the order of the clause (kept by the existing test `fp-evaluation-order`); `make_full_year` uses `trunc` and `+ 0.0` only. No std transcendental function and no method that "Which `f64` methods an engine crate may call" forbids.
- Tests, in `crates/num/tests/calendar.rs` (new; `crates/num/tests/main.rs` is generated, rewritten by `sh taskfiles/test-main.sh write`): a day-by-day walk against a second implementation that counts days with the Gregorian rule by looping over months (the oracle shares no code with the function), checking year, month, date and weekday; the gate count walks 1900-01-01 to 2100-12-31 (73,414 days) and the full count, at `SWELL_FULL_COUNTS=1`, every day from -1000-01-01 to 3000-12-31 (1,461,335 days: 4,001 years of 365 days and 970 leap days): the unit measures the full walk in a job of the pool and keeps it under 2.5 CPU seconds or, if it does not, cuts the full count to every day from year 0 to 3000 and says so; the test prints its entry count and its time. The boundaries `-271821-04-20T00:00:00.000Z` (-8.64e15), `+275760-09-13T00:00:00.000Z` (8.64e15), one millisecond either side, and `LOCAL_TIME_LIMIT`; the weekday of 1970-01-01 (Thursday, 4); `make_full_year` over its cases; `make_day` at 2^53 - 65 and 2^53 + 2; `offset_milliseconds` of 0, ±86,399,999,999,999 and ±86,400,000,000,000 (the clamp), `i128::MAX` and `i128::MIN`, and of 1,999,999 ns (truncation toward zero: 1) and -1,999,999 (-1); `parse_time_zone_offset_string` over its cases; `local_time` with a fake zone of +3,600 × 10^9 ns (one call), with an offset-string identifier (no call), with NaN (none); `utc_from_local` over a fake zone with the rules of a daylight saving change written in the test (offset 0 before an instant A, 3,600 × 10^9 ns after): an ordinary time (one call of each closure), a repeated local time (the first instant), a skipped local time (`tBefore`: the offset before the transition), a 24-hour gap, a gap of 80 hours (NaN), the count of asks never above 57, a host that answers empty everywhere (30 asks, NaN), the two edges of the range, an error from a closure going up unchanged. Each test under 1.5 CPU seconds in the default mode; none touches the engine.
