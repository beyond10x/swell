## Context

Slice 6 is delivered before slices 4 and 5 because it closes most of the first gap of the consumer, the testharness.js tests of the HTML parser of surf (`docs/plan/waves.md`, "The order after slice 3"): 191 refused constructs of slice 6 in `resources/testharness.js` and the scripts of `html/syntax/parsing/html5lib_url.html`, measured with the parser of `main` (`6a43e18`). This story lifts none of the 191: the measurement holds no computed property name and no object spread or rest. It comes right after the first five stories because it needs no builtin of slice 3 and lifts the largest refusal of the parser that is left without one: the first failing run of 156 cases of slice 6 ends on `computed property name is not supported` (`awk -F'\t' '$2 == "06" && $5 != "Passed" && !($3 in s) { s[$3] = 1; if ($6 ~ /computed property name is not supported/) c++ } END { print c }' .out/test262/runs.tsv` prints `156`; 2,061 runs over all slices end on it). Object spread and object rest are the two `feature` lines `object-spread` and `object-rest` of the slice (493 cases carry one; most of them also need array patterns, `for`-`of` or `Symbol`, and are on the lists of those stories).

The base refuses a computed name in `crates/syntax/src/parser/expression.rs`: `property_key` (line 886, the refusal at line 892), `accessor_kind` (line 915: the refusal at line 919, and `spread element` for `...` in an object literal at line 918) and the name after `get` or `set` (line 941); `story:object-destructuring` keeps both refusals in an object pattern (a computed key and a rest element `...rest`). `PropertyKey` (`crates/syntax/src/ast.rs`, line 141) has no computed form and `PropertyValue` (line 149) no spread. Fixtures of earlier waves that rely on the refusals: `crates/syntax/tests/sources/refused/computed-property-name.js`, `computed-accessor-name.js` and `spread-in-object.js`, removed; `spread-in-array.js` and `spread-in-call.js` stay (`story:array-destructuring-and-spread`).

Found by the token scan `docs/plan/slice-06/scan.awk` (run as `story:let-const-and-block-scopes` gives it, but with a copy of `meta.tsv` whose includes leave out `propertyHelper.js`, whose three later tokens are a branch behind `typeof`, a case of a bigint value and the name `checkSet`; the comment at the head of `lists-b.awk` gives the command; over the first failing run of each failing case of slice 6 and of those six directories on `main`, `6a43e18`) and the assignment `docs/plan/slice-06/lists-b.awk` (`gawk -v root=<checkout> -v meta=.cache/inventory/meta.tsv -f docs/plan/slice-06/lists-b.awk <scan output>`, the cases of the five lists of the first part taken out), which puts a case on the list of the first story of the slice that holds every part the case needs and whose own construct the case uses.
