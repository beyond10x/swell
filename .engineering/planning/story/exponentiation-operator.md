## Context

Slice 4 of 19, `numbers-and-dates` (`epic:slice-04-numbers-and-dates`; `docs/design/engine.md` decision 6, bullet "`**` and `Math.pow`", added on the design branch; ECMA-262 13.6 `ExponentiationExpression`, 13.15 `**=`, 6.1.6.1.3 `Number::exponentiate`). This story makes the operators `**` and `**=` parse, compile and run, over the one function `swell_num::math::exponentiate` that `story:swell-num-math-exp-and-log` writes and that `Math.pow` calls too. It depends on `story:swell-num-math-exp-and-log` (the function), on `story:math-functions` (its `Math.pow` and the cases that print both), on `story:math-constants-and-exact-functions` and `story:number-functions` (cases that call `Math.abs`, `Math.clz32`, `Math.fround` and `Number.isSafeInteger` next to an operator).

On the base (`c6d44d1`; the unit reads every place named in this story again on its base) the parser refuses both operators naming slice 4: in `crates/syntax/src/parser/expression.rs` the arms `Punct::StarStarEq` (the assignment operators) and `Punct::StarStar` (the binary operators) return `self.refuse("exponentiation operator", 4)`; the precedence table follows them (`Mul | Div | Rem => 10` is the tightest); `BinaryOp` (in `crates/syntax/src/ast.rs`) has no variant for them; `crates/compiler/src/expression.rs` (the `BinaryOp` match), `crates/compiler/src/bytecode.rs` (the `Instruction` enum and the list of instructions with a stack effect) have none; `crates/swell/src/interp/dispatch.rs` (the binary arms) and `crates/swell/src/ops/operators.rs` (`BinaryOperator`) have none. Two fixtures expect the refusal: `crates/syntax/tests/sources/refused/exponentiation.js` and `exponentiation-assignment.js`.
