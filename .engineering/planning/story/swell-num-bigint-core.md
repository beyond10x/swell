## Context

Slice 12 brings `BigInt` (`epic:slice-12-bigint`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 12, and decision 6, "`BigInt`" and "Big integers in slice 4"). This story is the first of the slice and has no case of test262: it writes the signed big integer of `swell-num` on which every other story of the slice computes, and it settles the three questions the epic leaves "to be settled by this epic's first story". It is in order 1, the only story of its order; `story:swell-num-bigint-powers-shifts-and-bits` and `story:bigint-values-and-literals` depend on it.

Line numbers in this body are those of `f962c324`; every anchor is found by its name (a function, a type, a quoted sentence), since the stories of slice 11 land in the base first and move lines.

What exists on `f962c324`: `crates/num/src/nat.rs` (`Nat`, 64-bit limbs least significant first, `u128` intermediates, no zero limb at the top; `pub(crate)`; written for slice 4, whose operands are at most 2,048 bits, so it allocates with `vec!` and `Vec::push` and takes no budget). The engine has no BigInt value: the module comment of `crates/num/src/lib.rs` (line 27) says "slice 12 adds `bigint.rs`".

The epic's three open questions, answered here (the design is the source, and its text changes in this story), and the threshold decision 6 leaves open:

1. The size of a limb: the epic's Outcome says 32-bit limbs with 64-bit intermediates; the design corrected that on 2026-11-24 to 64-bit limbs with `u128` intermediates (decision 6, "`BigInt`": "one type serves slices 4 and 12"). This story follows the design; the epic's sentence is stale (reported, not edited; the close lists it for the coordinator).
2. Whether the `BigInt` part of `swell-num` is written in slice 4 or here: the design answers it ("Big integers in slice 4"): slice 4 wrote the magnitude `Nat`; slice 12 "puts a sign and the rest on top of the same magnitude (`bigint.rs`), and decides how a heap `BigInt` holds its limbs" (the heap record is `story:bigint-values-and-literals`'s).
3. Whether ECMA-262 words an allowance for a `RangeError` when a BigInt result is too large (design, "Not checked"): the unit reads the pinned draft (`.cache/ecma262/<commit>/ecma262.txt` of the primary checkout's `.cache`, copied into the worktree as `AGENTS.md`, "Worktrees", says; the commit is `74c33d40...` on `f962c324`) for "implementation limit", "implementation-defined" and "RangeError" in the clauses of 6.1.6.2 (`#sec-ecmascript-language-types-bigint-type`) and of `BigInt` (21.2), and writes what it found into decision 6. `docs/design/host-interface.md` already found none ("The draft words no allowance for these outcomes ... If the unit finds an allowance, it quotes the clause and sentence in its report, and the omission lines are not needed: the coordinator takes them out of the close before it starts. 4. The size above which Karatsuba is used (decision 6 gives none): decided by the decomposition: the smallest operand length, in limbs, at which the limb operations Karatsuba counts are fewer than schoolbook's for two operands of that length; the same count is what the engine charges as steps (decision 3), so the threshold makes the charge smallest. The type `BigInt { negative: bool, magnitude: Nat }`, zero never negative, with:
  - construction and reading: from `u64` and `i64`, from limbs (`&[u64]`, least significant first, and a sign; trimmed), `limbs()`, `is_negative()`, `is_zero()`, `bit_len()` (the bit length of the magnitude, as `u64`), equality and `cmp`;

  - `string_to_bigint(units: &[u16]) -> Option<BigInt>` (`#sec-stringtobigint`): `StringIntegerLiteral` after the `StrWhiteSpace` at either end is trimmed: empty is `0n`; a decimal integer with an optional sign; `0x`, `0o`, `0b` (either case) with no sign; no separator, no exponent, no fraction, no `n`, no `Infinity`; `None` for anything else (the engine's `SyntaxError`). The engine's callers read a String's units and call it themselves (`story:bigint-comparison-and-loose-equality`, `story:bigint-constructor-and-prototype`);

  - Numbers: `to_f64` (6.1.6.2.22's 𝔽(ℝ(x)): rounded to nearest, ties to even, from the top 54 bits and a sticky bit; ±∞ from 2^1024 − 2^970 up), `from_f64(f64) -> Option<BigInt>` (NumberToBigInt, `#sec-numbertobigint`: `None` for NaN, ±∞ and a non-integral Number; `-0` is `0n`; exact for every integral Number), and `cmp_f64(&self, f64) -> Option<Ordering>` (the comparison of 7.2.13 IsLessThan step 4 and of IsLooselyEqual step 12: `None` for NaN; exact against every Number, ±∞ included, with no rounding of the BigInt);

The lower bound is what the engine compares with `bigint_bits` before it computes; the exact length after;
