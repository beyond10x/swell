## Context

Eleven builtins of `Array` that read an array or make a new one from it, and one property: `indexOf`, `lastIndexOf`, `includes`, `at`, `slice`, `concat`, `with`, `toReversed`, `toSpliced`, `toLocaleString`, `Array.of`, and `Array.prototype[@@unscopables]`. None exists on the base (the probe of `story:builtin-group-modules`).

Measured on the base (`309b850`, the run lines of `slot task test262` on it): their eleven directories under `test/built-ins/Array/` hold 571 cases of slice 3, 555 failing (`indexOf` 189 of 195, `lastIndexOf` 187 of 193, `slice` 49 of 50, `toSpliced` 27, `includes` 20, `concat` 19, `with` 17, `toReversed` 15, `Array.of` 13, `at` 11, `toLocaleString` 8). The static scan of the failing cases of the slice (the decomposition read each case and the harness files it includes for the names of the builtins missing on the base and of the parts of later slices) finds 512 failing cases that name a builtin of this story, no builtin of another story of the wave and no part of a later slice: the list `docs/plan/slice-03/story-array-search.cases.txt` (512 lines; by directory line: `built-ins/Array` 506, `built-ins/Object` 3, `built-ins/Function` 2, `built-ins/ThrowTypeError` 1). A failing case that names builtins of two stories of the wave is on `docs/plan/slice-03/cross-story.tsv` and on no list, and one that needs a later slice is on `docs/plan/slice-03/move-estimate.tsv`; the ten lists, 4,050 cases, were read against the scan again when this plan was revised, and no case of a list names a builtin of another story. That list is this story's bound; a case of it that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part and the coordinator takes it off the list in the wave record. Outside their directories the scan cannot tell `slice`, `concat`, `includes`, `at` and `lastIndexOf` of an Array from those of a String: such a case counts for this story and for `story:string-access-and-search-methods` and is on neither list (10 cases). The 7 cases of `toLocaleString` that stay also need `Object.prototype.toLocaleString` (`story:object-integrity-and-prototype-methods`) and pass on the wave branch.

Not delivered here, with the reason: `Array.from` (`sec-array.from`) and the methods `keys`, `values`, `entries` and `[@@iterator]` (`sec-array.prototype.keys` and the three others) need the iterators of slice 6. An `Array.from` that took every argument as an array-like would answer wrongly for a String with a surrogate pair, which the String iterator reads as one code point and an array-like read as two code units, so it is absent and not a stand-in (`docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, "A part before its slice"); `conformance/test262/later-parts.tsv` already lists `Array.from` under slice 6. The cases of their four directories (44 of slice 3, 6 of them passing on the base by the `TypeError` of the missing function) are not on this story's list: by the ruling of 2026-11-21 the close (`story:slice-03-conformance-close`) moves each of these directories whole to slice 6 by a `dir` line, its passing cases included.

Code it builds on, checked on the base: `same_value_zero` carries `expect(dead_code, reason = "Array.prototype.includes: story:harness-builtins")` (`crates/swell/src/ops/testing.rs`, line 74), which this story fulfils; `@@isConcatSpreadable` is a well-known symbol of the realm after `story:builtin-group-modules`, read like the five others and under no mark; Invoke is an operation of `crates/swell/src/ops/objects.rs` after that story, complete and tested there, under a mark whose reason names this story and `story:object-integrity-and-prototype-methods`; `@@unscopables` exists since slice 2 and HasBinding of a `with` statement reads it (`crates/swell/src/env/object.rs`); `array_species_create`, `index_key`, `StringBuilder` and CreateDataPropertyOrThrow are `pub(crate)`; the group module `builtins/array/search.rs` is that story's, with an id type without a variant and an empty list, and `mod.rs` needs no line for a builtin of the group. One test of an earlier wave states an absence in its name: `crates/swell/tests/language/with-unscopables-not-observable-before-slice-6.js` (its script, a `with` over an object without `@@unscopables`, stays right; with `Array.prototype[@@unscopables]` the property is observable in slice 3, so the file is renamed).
