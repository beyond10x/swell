## Context

Slice 6 is delivered before slices 4 and 5 because it closes most of the first gap of the consumer, the testharness.js tests of the HTML parser of surf (`docs/plan/waves.md`, "The order after slice 3"): 191 refused constructs of slice 6 in `resources/testharness.js` and the scripts of `html/syntax/parsing/html5lib_url.html`, measured with the parser of `main` (`6a43e18`). The measurement names none of these builtins (`docs/plan/waves.md`, the builtins the scripts call): this story lifts none of the 191. It delivers the three builtins of slice 6 that read an iterable: `Array.from` (`#sec-array.from`), `Object.fromEntries` (`#sec-object.fromentries`) and `Object.groupBy` (`#sec-object.groupby`, GroupBy `#sec-groupby`). Their directories, `test/built-ins/Array/from/` (47 cases), `Object/fromEntries/` (25) and `Object/groupBy/` (14), are of slice 3 on the base and move to slice 6 whole by `story:slice-03-conformance-close` (ruled on 2026-11-21: "iterators, slice 6"), with `Array/prototype/keys/`, `values/` and `entries/` (`story:iterator-protocol-and-array-string-iterators`). On the base calling each throws `the value is not a function` (`awk -F'\t' '$3 ~ /^test\/built-ins\/(Array\/from|Object\/fromEntries|Object\/groupBy)\// && $6 ~ /the value is not a function|Cannot use a property of undefined/' .out/test262/runs.tsv | wc -l` prints `46`).

The list of this story is `docs/plan/slice-06/story-builtins.cases.txt`: 77 failing cases (150 runs) of slice 6 and of the six directories `story:slice-03-conformance-close` moves to slice 6 (`test/built-ins/Array/from/`, `Array/prototype/keys/`, `values/`, `entries/`, `Object/fromEntries/`, `Object/groupBy/`), on no list of the first five stories of the slice, whose text and harness files use `Array.from`, `Object.fromEntries` or `Object.groupBy`, no other part of slice 6 than those of the stories this one depends on, and no part of a later slice. Found by the token scan `docs/plan/slice-06/scan.awk` (run as `story:let-const-and-block-scopes` gives it, but with a copy of `meta.tsv` whose includes leave out `propertyHelper.js`, whose three later tokens are a branch behind `typeof`, a case of a bigint value and the name `checkSet`; the comment at the head of `lists-b.awk` gives the command; over the first failing run of each failing case of slice 6 and of those six directories on `main`, `6a43e18`) and the assignment `docs/plan/slice-06/lists-b.awk` (`gawk -v root=<checkout> -v meta=.cache/inventory/meta.tsv -f docs/plan/slice-06/lists-b.awk <scan output>`, the cases of the five lists of the first part taken out), which puts a case on the list of the first story of the slice that holds every part the case needs and whose own construct the case uses. By directory: `built-ins/Array` 41, `built-ins/Object` 36. A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit of the list file on the wave branch whose body names that part; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

Design it builds on: `docs/design/engine.md`, decision 3, "Iterators held by a builtin" (lines 611 to 652, merged on `wave/s3-1`): the three builtins of this story are the ones it names. Each holds its `IteratorRecord` and every value it still needs across a call (the result of `next` while `done` and `value` are read, the values or groups it collects, the object it builds, its callback) on the kept stack, `Agent::keep` and `Agent::release` to its mark on every way out (item 1); a throw of `next`, `done` or `value` or a result that is no object ends it with no close, an abrupt completion of its own work between two steps closes first (IfAbruptCloseIterator, `#sec-ifabruptcloseiterator`), and a termination closes nothing; the call path charges the step of each `next`, `return`, `mapfn` and callback, and the loop charges one more step per iteration through `charge_steps`, whatever `next` is (items 2 and 3). No new design.

## Domain relations

- None new (each builtin makes an Array or an ordinary object; the groups of `Object.groupBy` are a list the builtin holds while it runs, rooted, and drops when it returns).

## Change

Failing tests first: the tests below are written before the code and fail on the base (the three are not functions).

- `crates/swell/src/builtins/array/from.rs` (new): `Array.from(items, mapfn, thisArg)`: the iterable path through GetIteratorFromMethod and IteratorStepValue with IteratorClose when `mapfn` or the define throws, and the array-like path (`#sec-array.from`, steps 5 and 7 on); `this` a constructor or not; CreateDataPropertyOrThrow and the final `length` Set; one step per element through `charge_steps`, beside the call of `next`, in both paths. `builtins/array/mod.rs`: the registration on `%Array%` and the arm in the `ArrayBuiltin` match (line 173, no wildcard).
- `crates/swell/src/builtins/object/from_entries.rs` (new): `Object.fromEntries(iterable)` through AddEntriesFromIterable (`#sec-add-entries-from-iterable`: each entry an object, its `0` and `1` read, IteratorClose on an abrupt completion; one step per entry through `charge_steps`), and `Object.groupBy(items, callback)` through GroupBy with the property keys coercion (`#sec-groupby`, AddValueToKeyedGroup), one step per element through `charge_steps`; the groups are an ordered list charged to the memory budget while the call runs. `builtins/object/mod.rs`: the registration on `%Object%` and the arms in the `ObjectBuiltin` match (line 142, no wildcard).
- `crates/swell/src/ops/iteration.rs` is read and not written: `story:for-of-statement`, which lands before this story, has removed the marks of `IteratorRecord`, GetIterator, IteratorStepValue and IteratorClose; GetIteratorFromMethod, if it is a function of its own, is called by GetIterator.
- Tests, each in both collection modes (`test-collect`): `crates/swell/tests/builtins_array_from.rs` (new): `from_an_iterable_and_from_an_array_like`, `from_charges_its_loop_when_next_is_a_builtin` (`Array.from` of an Array Iterator, whose `next` runs no instruction: `last_entry_steps()` over 1,000 elements exceeds that over 10 by at least 2 × 990, a step for each call of `next` and one for each iteration), `from_keeps_its_values_across_a_collection_in_next` (in the mode `collect-every-safe-point`), `from_calls_mapfn_with_value_and_index_and_closes_the_iterator_when_it_throws`, `from_with_a_constructor_this_constructs_it`, `from_charges_a_step_per_element` (`last_entry_steps()` after `Array.from(big)` exceeds that after `Array.from(small)`, made by an earlier script with 1,000 and 10 elements, by at least 990), `from_re_enters_through_mapfn_on_the_promised_stack` (`reach_through` of `tests/support/stack.rs`, seven eighths of `min_stack_bytes()`). `crates/swell/tests/builtins_object_from_entries.rs` (new): `from_entries_reads_0_and_1_of_each_entry`, `from_entries_closes_the_iterator_when_an_entry_is_not_an_object`, `from_entries_charges_a_step_per_entry` (`last_entry_steps()` after `Object.fromEntries(big)` exceeds that after `Object.fromEntries(small)`, arrays of 1,000 and 10 pairs made by an earlier script, by at least 990), `from_entries_re_enters_through_a_user_next_on_the_promised_stack`, `group_by_groups_in_order_and_keys_by_to_property_key`, `group_by_charges_a_step_per_element` (the same form), `group_by_re_enters_through_its_callback_on_the_promised_stack`. `crates/swell/tests/language/` (new files): `builtin-array-from.js`, `builtin-object-from-entries.js`, `builtin-object-group-by.js`.
