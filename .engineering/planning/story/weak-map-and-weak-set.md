## Context

Slice 9 brings `Map`, `Set`, `WeakMap`, `WeakSet`, `Proxy` and `Reflect` (`epic:slice-09-collections-proxy-reflect`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 9). This story brings `WeakMap` and `WeakSet` (`#sec-weakmap-objects`, `#sec-weakset-objects`) with `WeakMap.prototype.getOrInsert` and `getOrInsertComputed` (the feature `upsert` on `WeakMap`), symbols as keys (`symbols-as-weakmap-keys`: CanBeHeldWeakly, `#sec-canbeheldweakly`), and the collector's clearing of their entries. It settles the epic's first open question ("How `WeakMap` and `WeakSet` entries are cleared by the collector": decision 4 of `docs/design/engine.md` says weak references are cleared after marking and names only `WeakRef` and `FinalizationRegistry`) by the default of question D2 of the decomposition (Change). It is in order 3 and depends on `story:map-and-set` (17 cases of its list are `Map.prototype` and `Set.prototype` cases that call a method of `Map` or `Set` on a `WeakMap` or `WeakSet` and expect a `TypeError`; the constructor loop AddEntriesFromIterable with an adder, which this story calls with `set` and `add`).

Measured by the decomposition on `62c7cf59` with the full run on the code of wave big-1 before the close of slice 4 (2026-11-26; its line `test262-slice 09 1785 3541 0 0 1785 267 1518 0` equals `conformance/test262/expected.txt` at `62c7cf59`): `WeakMap` and `WeakSet` are not defined. The list of this story, `docs/plan/slice-09/story-weak.cases.txt`, holds the 247 cases of the key `weak` of `docs/plan/slice-09/scan.tsv` (the command is in the head of `docs/plan/slice-09/scan.awk`), all failing (493 runs: 202 on `Expected a TypeError but got a ReferenceError`, 171 on `WeakMap is not defined`, 94 on `WeakSet is not defined`, the rest on a `Test262Error` or `Reflect is not defined`): `WeakMap/prototype` 117 (`getOrInsertComputed` 22, `delete` 22, `set` 20, `has` 20, `getOrInsert` 17, `get` 13, the rest 3), `WeakSet/prototype` 66, `WeakMap` 24, `WeakSet` 19, `Map/prototype` 10, `Set/prototype` 7, `Object/seal` 2, `Object/prototype` 2 (`toString`). A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit of the list file on the wave branch whose body names that part; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

Design it builds on, by name: `docs/design/engine.md` decision 4 ("Collector": precise, deterministic, its workspace "a mark word per slot and a worklist of at most one entry per slot, lives with the arenas and is charged with their capacity, so a collection allocates nothing"; "Weak references are cleared after marking (9.9)"; "Memory budget"; "Objects", the keyed hash map of `crates/swell/src/heap/map.rs`) and decision 3 ("The step budget", "Depth bounds"). The design does not state how an entry whose value reaches its own key (an ephemeron) is marked; this story takes the default of question D2.

## Domain relations

- A `WeakMap` or `WeakSet` → its entries: one to many, each entry's key held weakly (the entry lives while the key is reachable otherwise), a `WeakMap` entry's value held strongly only while its key is — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-weakmap-objects`, the note on keys that become inaccessible, and `#sec-weakset-objects`; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the slots of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").

- The collector, `crates/swell/src/heap/collect.rs` (decision D2, default A, linear ephemeron marking): `WeakCollection::trace` does not mark keys; for each entry whose key is marked it pushes the value, and for each entry whose key is not marked yet it links the entry into a list of pending entries headed at the key's slot (a head word per slot in the collector's workspace, beside the mark word, so the workspace grows by one word per slot and is charged with the arenas' capacity as the mark words are; no allocation during a collection); when the mark phase marks a slot whose pending list is not empty, it pushes the values of that list's entries and empties it. After marking, before the sweep, every weak table reached in the mark phase drops its entries whose key is unmarked (the table's bytes credited by the sweep's accounting), so a key that is reachable only through values of entries whose keys are unreachable is freed with them. A WeakSet entry has no value; it is only cleared. `a_collection_allocates_nothing` and `a_collection_at_the_memory_limit_finishes` (`collect.rs` lines 542 and 614) pass as they are; the worklist stays at most one entry per slot (`the_worklist_holds_at_most_one_entry_per_slot`, line 564).
