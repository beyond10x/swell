## Context

Slice 12 brings `BigInt` (`epic:slice-12-bigint`; `docs/design/engine.md` decisions 3, 4 and 6). This story brings `%BigInt%` and `%BigInt.prototype%` (ECMA-262 21.2): the constructor `BigInt ( value )` with NumberToBigInt and ToBigInt (`#sec-bigint-constructor-number-value`, `#sec-numbertobigint`, `#sec-tobigint`), `BigInt.asIntN` and `BigInt.asUintN` (21.2.2.1 and .2), `BigInt.prototype.toLocaleString`, `toString ( [ radix ] )`, `valueOf` and `@@toStringTag` (21.2.3), thisBigIntValue (`#sec-thisbigintvalue`), the BigInt object with its [[BigIntData]] and ToObject of a BigInt (`#sec-toobject`), which a method called on a BigInt primitive in sloppy code and `Object(1n)` reach. It is in order 3, built at the same time as `story:bigint-comparison-and-loose-equality` and `story:bigint-arithmetic-and-bitwise-operators`; it depends on `story:bigint-values-and-literals` (the record, `new_bigint`, `bigint_check_bits`, `bigint_charge`, ToString and `string_to_bigint`) and on `story:swell-num-bigint-powers-shifts-and-bits` (`as_int_n`, `as_uint_n` and their costs; through it `story:swell-num-bigint-core`'s `to_radix`, `from_f64` and their costs).

Measured by the decomposition on `f962c324` with the full run of that commit (`test262-slice 12 381 760 0 0 381 39 342 0`): no BigInt can be made and the global `BigInt` does not exist. The list of this story, `docs/plan/slice-12/story-builtin.cases.txt`, holds the 100 cases of the key `builtin` of `docs/plan/slice-12/scan.tsv`, all failing, 199 runs: 127 fail on "BigInt literal is not supported before slice 12", 50 on "BigInt is not defined", the other 22 on an assertion whose message is a `ReferenceError` of the missing global or `typeof BigInt` reading `"undefined"`. By directory: `built-ins/BigInt` 75 (`prototype` 25, `asIntN` 14, `asUintN` 14, the constructor's own files 21, `parseInt/nonexistent.js` 1), `built-ins/String/prototype` 10 (six `cstm-*-on-bigint-primitive` cases, of `match`, `matchAll`, `replace`, `replaceAll`, `search` and `split`, that define a method of `BigInt.prototype` under a well-known symbol, `indexOf` 2, `isWellFormed` and `toWellFormed` 1 each), `built-ins/Object` 5 (`prototype/toString` 3, `create` 1, `bigint.js` 1), `built-ins/Array` 4 (`fromAsync` 2, one of them a sloppy `this` boxed, `prototype/sort` 1, `prototype/toString` 1), `built-ins/Error/isError` 1, `harness/deepEqual-primitives-bigint.js` 1, and in `language/expressions` `typeof`, `unary-minus`, `strict-equals` and `strict-does-not-equals` 1 each (a wrapper made by `Object(1n)`). A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit on the wave branch whose body names that part and adds it, in the same commit, to `docs/plan/slice-12/close-later.tsv` (path, the story that takes it, one reason), which `sh docs/plan/slice-12/lists.sh` counts as a list; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

## Domain relations

- A BigInt object → its [[BigIntData]]: one to one, not owned (the BigInt is a heap value of its own, shared with every other holder), kept alive by the object's `trace`; immutable after the object is made — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-properties-of-bigint-instances` and `#sec-toobject`, and from `crates/swell/src/object/ordinary.rs` lines 50 to 53 and 97 to 112, `OrdinaryData::Symbol` and its arm in `trace`, the form this relation takes; no ess/1 document declares it).
