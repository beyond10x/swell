## Context

The first consumer calls `match`, `replace` and `split` with a regular expression on 11 of its 12 literals (`docs/plan/slice-05/consumer-regexps.tsv`, every row but row 2; and `replace` with the RegExp made at `resources/testharness.js` line 1389, called on line 1441). After `story:string-replace-replace-all-and-split` and `story:regexp-objects-exec-and-literals`, `replace` and `split` hand an argument to its method keyed by `@@replace` or `@@split`, but `%RegExp.prototype%` has none, so they take the String path and convert the RegExp to a String: an answer of that unfinished state, which this story ends. `String.prototype.match` and `search` do not exist (`crates/swell/src/builtins/string/`, the lists of its three files). This story brings `RegExp.prototype [ @@match ]`, `[ @@replace ]`, `[ @@search ]` and `[ @@split ]`, and `String.prototype.match` and `search`, which make a RegExp from a String and invoke its method. After it every regular expression of the harness runs: each literal evaluates and each call on it answers.

## Change

Failing tests first: the tests below are written before the code and fail on the base of the unit (no method keyed by a well-known symbol on `%RegExp.prototype%`, no `match` or `search` on `%String.prototype%`).

- `crates/swell/src/builtins/regexp/symbol.rs` (new): `RegExp.prototype [ %Symbol.match% ]` (`#sec-regexp.prototype-%symbol.match%`: `flags` read by Get; without `g` RegExpExec; with it `lastIndex` set to 0 and every match collected, an empty match advancing `lastIndex` by AdvanceStringIndex, by a code point with `u` or `v`), `[ %Symbol.replace% ]` (`#sec-regexp.prototype-%symbol.replace%`: the results collected first, then for each its captures by ToString, `groups` by ToObject, a functional replacement called with (matched, the captures, the position, the String and the groups when there are any) and its result converted, a String replacement through GetSubstitution of `story:string-replace-replace-all-and-split`, the pieces joined in order of position with the rule that skips a result before the last position), `[ %Symbol.search% ]` (`#sec-regexp.prototype-%symbol.search%`: `lastIndex` saved and restored with SameValue), `[ %Symbol.split% ]` (`#sec-regexp.prototype-%symbol.split%`: SpeciesConstructor of `%RegExp%`, `flags` read and `y` added, Construct of the splitter with them, the loop by `lastIndex` with RegExpExec, the captures pushed, the limit by ToUint32). Each is a method of `%RegExp.prototype%` keyed by its symbol, named `[Symbol.match]` and so on, made as `String.prototype[@@iterator]` is (`builtins/string/mod.rs` line 303), defined by `builtins/regexp/mod.rs` after the properties of that story. They call RegExpExec, RegExpBuiltinExec, RegExpCreate and AdvanceStringIndex of `story:regexp-objects-exec-and-literals` and GetSubstitution of `story:string-replace-replace-all-and-split`, and write none of them.
- SpeciesConstructor (`#sec-speciesconstructor`), which no builtin has yet (`builtins/array/mod.rs` line 487 has ArraySpeciesCreate only): a `pub(crate)` operation of `crates/swell/src/ops/objects.rs`, with a test of its own.
- `crates/swell/src/builtins/string/pattern.rs` (new), a group file as `story:builtin-group-modules` makes them: `String.prototype.match` (`#sec-string.prototype.match`) and `search` (`#sec-string.prototype.search`): RequireObjectCoercible; for an argument neither undefined nor null, GetMethod of `@@match` or `@@search` and its Call; otherwise ToString of the this value, RegExpCreate of the argument with undefined flags, and Invoke of its method with the String. Registration in `builtins/string/mod.rs`: the `mod pattern;` line, a variant `Pattern(pattern::PatternBuiltin)` of `StringBuiltin`, its arm and its list lines, beside those of `story:string-replace-replace-all-and-split`.
- Steps and memory: each match is charged by the matcher; each pass of a loop of `@@match`, `@@replace` and `@@split` charges a step besides; the list of results of `@@replace` and the arrays of `@@match` and `@@split` are charged before each growth, the results held across the calls of the replacer rooted, so a collection at a safe point keeps them; each String built goes through `StringBuilder` (bounded by `string_length`).
- Re-entry: a functional replacement, a user `exec`, the Get of `flags` and the species constructor are calls of the call path; each builtin does its work in functions of its own, `STACK_BYTES_PER_REENTRY` and the stack tests do not change and pass on seven eighths of `min_stack_bytes()`.
