## Context

The first consumer calls `match`, `replace` and `split` with a regular expression on 11 of its 12 literals (`docs/plan/slice-05/consumer-regexps.tsv`, every row but row 2; and `replace` with the RegExp made at `resources/testharness.js` line 1389, called on line 1441). After `story:string-replace-replace-all-and-split` and `story:regexp-objects-exec-and-literals`, `replace` and `split` hand an argument to its method keyed by `@@replace` or `@@split`, and `%RegExp.prototype%` has only the two methods of `story:regexp-objects-exec-and-literals` keyed by `@@replace` and `@@split` that answer the counted refusal `RegExp.prototype[Symbol.replace] is not supported before slice 5` (and `[Symbol.split]`), so a RegExp given to `replace`, `replaceAll` or `split` is refused, not answered wrong; this story replaces them. `String.prototype.match` and `search` do not exist (`crates/swell/src/builtins/string/`, the lists of its three files). This story brings `RegExp.prototype [ @@match ]`, `[ @@replace ]`, `[ @@search ]` and `[ @@split ]`, and `String.prototype.match` and `search`, which make a RegExp from a String and invoke its method. After it every regular expression of the harness runs: each literal evaluates and each call on it answers.

Lifts of the consumer: rows 1 and 3 to 12 of the table (`match` on lines 581 and 5004, `replace` on lines 587, 3651, 4012, 4433, 4558 and 4816 of `testharness.js` and line 251 of `test.js`, `split` on line 4664 of `testharness.js` and line 71 of `template.js`), and `replace` with the RegExp of line 1389. The test `the_calls_of_the_consumer_table_answer` below shows it by running the engine: for each of the 12 rows of `docs/plan/slice-05/consumer-regexps.tsv` it evaluates a literal of the construct and flags of that row and makes the call the harness makes on it (`test`, `match`, `replace` with a String or a function, `split`), with the patterns, inputs and expected answers written in the test (nothing of the downloaded files is copied); on the base of the unit it fails (`match` does not exist and `replace` and `split` given a RegExp are refused). The table stays the list of what that test covers.

The list of this story is `docs/plan/slice-05/story-symbol-methods.cases.txt`: 413 failing cases (822 runs) of slice 5 whose text calls one of these methods with a regular expression or which are in their directories, and which need no part of a later story of the slice, found by `docs/plan/slice-05/scan.awk` (its head gives the command) over the first failing run of each failing case of slice 5 on `e444aaf`. By directory: `built-ins/String/prototype/split` 82, `RegExp/prototype/Symbol.replace` 67, `Symbol.match` 52, `String/prototype/match` 47, `RegExp/prototype/Symbol.split` 41, `String/prototype/search` 37, `String/prototype/replace` 24, `RegExp/prototype/Symbol.search` 22, `RegExp/named-groups` 22, `RegExp/lookBehind` 10, `built-ins/RegExp/` 4, `String/prototype/replaceAll` 4, `language/literals/regexp` 1. Three of the four of `replaceAll` (`getSubstitution-0x0024-0x003C.js`, `getSubstitution-0x0024N.js`, `getSubstitution-0x0024NN.js`) evaluate the literal `/./g` and give it a `Symbol.replace` of their own: they need `replaceAll` of `story:string-replace-replace-all-and-split` and the literal and IsRegExp of `story:regexp-objects-exec-and-literals`, and this story is the first after both. The cases under `test/annexB/` that these methods would lift (`annexB/built-ins/RegExp/named-groups/non-unicode-malformed.js`; the two of `annexB/built-ins/RegExp/prototype/Symbol.split/` also call `RegExp.prototype.compile`) stay counted in slice 18 until `story:slice-05-conformance-close` decides them. ## Change

Failing tests first: the tests below are written before the code and fail on the base of the unit (no method keyed by a well-known symbol on `%RegExp.prototype%`, no `match` or `search` on `%String.prototype%`).

- `crates/swell/src/builtins/regexp/symbol.rs` (new): `RegExp.prototype [ %Symbol.match% ]` (`#sec-regexp.prototype-%symbol.match%`: `flags` read by Get; without `g` RegExpExec; with it `lastIndex` set to 0 and every match collected, an empty match advancing `lastIndex` by AdvanceStringIndex, by a code point with `u` or `v`), `[ %Symbol.replace% ]` (`#sec-regexp.prototype-%symbol.replace%`: the results collected first, then for each its captures by ToString, `groups` by ToObject, a functional replacement called with (matched, the captures, the position, the String and the groups when there are any) and its result converted, a String replacement through GetSubstitution of `story:string-replace-replace-all-and-split`, the pieces joined in order of position with the rule that skips a result before the last position), `[ %Symbol.search% ]` (`#sec-regexp.prototype-%symbol.search%`: `lastIndex` saved and restored with SameValue), `[ %Symbol.split% ]` (`#sec-regexp.prototype-%symbol.split%`: SpeciesConstructor of `%RegExp%`, `flags` read and `y` added, Construct of the splitter with them, the loop by `lastIndex` with RegExpExec, the captures pushed, the limit by ToUint32). Each is a method of `%RegExp.prototype%` keyed by its symbol, named `[Symbol.match]` and so on, made as `String.prototype[@@iterator]` is (`builtins/string/mod.rs` line 303), defined by `builtins/regexp/mod.rs` in place of the two methods of that story keyed by `@@replace` and `@@split` that answer the counted refusal, which go with their test `replace_and_split_given_a_regexp_are_counted_refusals_until_their_symbol_methods` (`crates/swell/tests/regexp_objects.rs`). - SpeciesConstructor (`#sec-speciesconstructor`), which no builtin has yet (`builtins/array/mod.rs` line 487 has ArraySpeciesCreate only): a `pub(crate)` operation of `crates/swell/src/ops/objects.rs`, with a test of its own.
- `crates/swell/src/builtins/string/pattern.rs` (new), a group file as `story:builtin-group-modules` makes them: `String.prototype.match` (`#sec-string.prototype.match`) and `search` (`#sec-string.prototype.search`): RequireObjectCoercible; for an argument neither undefined nor null, GetMethod of `@@match` or `@@search` and its Call; otherwise ToString of the this value, RegExpCreate of the argument with undefined flags, and Invoke of its method with the String.
