## Context

Slice 9 brings `Map`, `Set`, `WeakMap`, `WeakSet`, `Proxy` and `Reflect` (`epic:slice-09-collections-proxy-reflect`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 9), with the feature `set-methods` (the epic's Outcome). This story brings the seven methods of that feature on `Set.prototype` (`#sec-set.prototype.union` and the six after it: `union`, `intersection`, `difference`, `symmetricDifference`, `isSubsetOf`, `isSupersetOf`, `isDisjointFrom`) and their abstract operations GetSetRecord (`#sec-getsetrecord`), SetDataHas and the iteration of a set-like's `keys`. It is in order 3 and depends on `story:map-and-set` (the `Set` kind, its table and cursors, `%Set.prototype%`, the `set_data` brand check).

Measured by the decomposition on `62c7cf59` with the full run on the code of wave big-1 before the close of slice 4 (2026-11-26; its line `test262-slice 09 1785 3541 0 0 1785 267 1518 0` equals `conformance/test262/expected.txt` at `62c7cf59`): the list of this story, `docs/plan/slice-09/story-set-methods.cases.txt`, holds the 129 cases of the key `set-methods` of `docs/plan/slice-09/scan.tsv` (the command is in the head of `docs/plan/slice-09/scan.awk`), all under `built-ins/Set/prototype/` and all failing (258 runs, each on `Set is not defined`). The cases of these seven directories that also define a `class` (slice 8) or use a BigInt (slice 12) are on `docs/plan/slice-09/close-later.tsv`, not on this list (the conformance close moves or checks them). A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit on the wave branch whose body names that part and adds it, in the same commit, to `docs/plan/slice-09/close-later.tsv` (path, the slice or story that takes it, one reason), which `sh docs/plan/slice-09/lists.sh` counts as a list, so the partition still holds, and which check (7) of `story:slice-09-conformance-close` reads; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

Design it builds on, by name: `docs/design/engine.md` decision 3 ("The step budget", "Depth bounds") and decision 4 ("Memory budget"). No sentence of the design is missing.
