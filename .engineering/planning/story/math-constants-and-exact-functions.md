## Context

Slice 4 of 19, `numbers-and-dates` (`epic:slice-04-numbers-and-dates`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 4). This story has no prerequisite of the design and no edge to wait for: the first consumer, the browser surf, reads `Math` seven times in the scripts its first tests load (`resources/testharness.js` lines 1708 `Math.floor(max_array_length / 2)`, 1709 `Math.min(length_after_offset + offset, arr.length)`, 1710 `Math.max(upper_bound - max_array_length, 0)`, 1783 and 1812 `Math.abs(actual[i] - expected[i])` and `Math.abs(actual - expected)`, 3018 `Math.ceil(timeout_full / interval)`, and `html/syntax/parsing/resources/common.js` line 5 `Math.max(expected_lines.length, actual_lines.length)`; the files are the downloaded files of `docs/plan/waves.md`, "The order after slice 3"). The arguments are Numbers made by the script; no result depends on the clock or the time zone. `Math.max` exists; `floor`, `min`, `abs` and `ceil` do not, so on the base `Math.floor` is not a function.

On the base (`c6d44d1`) `%Math%` (`crates/swell/src/builtins/math.rs`, 87 lines) has `max` and `@@toStringTag` only; its header says "the other members are slice 3", which is stale (slice 3 did not write them; they are slice 4). The 8 constants and 11 functions of this story are exactly those that IEEE 754 makes exact or that integer arithmetic makes exact, so no algorithm of `swell-num` is needed and the result is the same on every platform: `abs`, `ceil`, `floor`, `trunc`, `sign` (the sign and the zeros), `round` (ties toward positive infinity), `fround` (`x as f32 as f64` is the round-to-nearest-even of IEEE 754 binary32, a language operation and not a library call), `clz32`, `imul` (both over ToUint32), `min`, and `sqrt` (`f64::sqrt` is correctly rounded by IEEE 754). The functions that the standard library answers through the platform `libm` (`sin`, `exp`, `ln`, `powf`, `powi` and the rest) are `story:math-functions` and are not used here.

The list of this story is `docs/plan/slice-04/story-math-exact.cases.txt`: the 133 cases of the key `math-exact` of `docs/plan/slice-04/scan.tsv` (no part of a later slice, no `Date`, no `**`, no function that needs an algorithm), made by the command in the head of `docs/plan/slice-04/scan.awk` over the first failing run of each failing case of slice 4 in the base run `runs-s10-1-close07-join.tsv` (1,612 cases in 3,184 runs, 261 pass). By directory: `built-ins/Math` 104 (`trunc` 11, `ceil`, `floor` and `round` 10 each, `min` and `sqrt` 9, `clz32` 8, `fround` 7, `abs` 6, `imul` and `sign` 4, each of the eight constants 2), `built-ins/Object` 17 (`getOwnPropertyDescriptor` and the three `entries`, `values` and `getOwnPropertyDescriptors` cases that read a Math member), `language/expressions` 8, and 4 more (one each in `built-ins/RegExp`, `built-ins/String`, `language/statements`, `language/types`).

Design it follows, by name: `docs/design/engine.md` decision 6 ("numbers and dates, whole": the sentence that `sqrt`, `abs`, `floor`, `trunc`, `round_ties_even` and `mul_add` come from `std` and the rest is written in `swell-num`, which the bullet "`Math.round`" and the list "Which `f64` methods an engine crate may call" correct for the two methods that the story does not use), decision 3 ("The step budget"), decision 13 (no panic: no `unwrap`, no `expect`, no unchecked indexing), `docs/design/host-interface.md` HOST-135 (the same script prints the same bytes on every release platform).

## What later stories reuse

The argument loop of `Math.max` and `Math.min` as one helper (`coerce_all`: ToNumber of every argument in order, then the work; a step per argument; the list reserved through the memory budget before it is made), which `Math.hypot` of `story:math-functions` uses as it is; the table of the value properties (name, value) beside the table of functions, to which `story:math-functions` adds its names; `MathBuiltin` as an enum without a wildcard in its dispatch.
