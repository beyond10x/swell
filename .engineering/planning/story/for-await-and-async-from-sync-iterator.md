## Context

Slice 10 brings jobs, `Promise`, `async` functions and generators and `for await` (`epic:slice-10-promises-and-async`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 10). This story brings `for await` in an `async` function (`#sec-for-in-and-for-of-statements`, ForIn/OfHeadEvaluation with iterationKind async-iterate, ForIn/OfBodyEvaluation with iteratorKind async, AsyncIteratorClose), the async iterator protocol (GetIterator with kind async, `#sec-getiterator`; CreateAsyncFromSyncIterator and `%AsyncFromSyncIteratorPrototype%`, `#sec-%asyncfromsynciteratorprototype%-object`), `%AsyncIteratorPrototype%` (`#sec-asynciteratorprototype`) and `%Symbol.asyncIterator%`.
