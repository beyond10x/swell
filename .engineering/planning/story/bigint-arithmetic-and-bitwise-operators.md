## Context

Slice 12 brings `BigInt` (`epic:slice-12-bigint`; `docs/design/engine.md` decisions 3, 4, 6 and 13). This story brings the binary operators on BigInts (`+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`, `**`, `<<`, `>>`, `>>>`, `&`, `|`, `^`, through ApplyStringOrNumericBinaryOperator, `#sec-applystringornumericbinaryoperator`, steps 4 to 8: a `TypeError` when one operand is a BigInt and the other is not, the BigInt operation of Table 2 otherwise), `~` (`BigInt::bitwiseNOT`), `++` and `--` (`BigInt::add` of `1n`), their compound assignments, and the size limit with its charges: an operation whose result's lower bound of bits is above `bigint_bits` throws a `RangeError` before it computes, its steps and its memory are charged before it computes, and one whose result fits the limit but not the memory budget ends the run in `Terminated(MemoryBudget)`. It closes HOST-23 and HOST-24 and the epic's acceptance items 5 and 6. It is in order 3, built at the same time as `story:bigint-comparison-and-loose-equality` and `story:bigint-constructor-and-prototype`; it depends on `story:bigint-values-and-literals` (the record, `new_bigint`, `bigint_check_bits`, `bigint_charge`, `negate`) and on `story:swell-num-bigint-powers-shifts-and-bits` (`pow`, the shifts, the bitwise operations and their costs; through it `story:swell-num-bigint-core`'s add, sub, mul, div and rem, `limbs_with_capacity` and the bound `mul_cost(a, b).limb_ops ≤ 2·a·b + 64`).

Measured by the decomposition on `f962c324` with the full run of that commit (`test262-slice 12 381 760 0 0 381 39 342 0`): no BigInt can be made, so every case fails on the refused literal. The list of this story, `docs/plan/slice-12/story-arith.cases.txt`, holds the 46 cases of the key `arith` of `docs/plan/slice-12/scan.tsv`, all failing, 92 runs, every one on "BigInt literal is not supported before slice 12": under `language/expressions`, `exponentiation` 5, `addition`, `division` and `modulus` 4 each, `subtraction`, `multiplication`, `left-shift`, `right-shift`, `unsigned-right-shift`, `bitwise-and`, `bitwise-or` and `bitwise-xor` 3 each, and one each of `bitwise-not`, `prefix-increment`, `prefix-decrement`, `postfix-increment` and `postfix-decrement`. The 31 cases of these directories that make a wrapper object of a BigInt (`Object(2n)`, `-wrapped-values`, `-and-number`, `-non-primitive`), whose ToPrimitive reads `BigInt.prototype.valueOf`, are on the list of `story:bigint-json-number-and-joined-cases`. A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit on the wave branch whose body names that part and adds it, in the same commit, to `docs/plan/slice-12/close-later.tsv` (path, the story that takes it, one reason), which `sh docs/plan/slice-12/lists.sh` counts as a list; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

HOST-23 and HOST-24 (`docs/design/host-interface.md` lines 326 and 328, both `[slice 12]`) are this story's: with `bigint_bits` 2^20, `2n ** (2n ** 40n)` throws a `RangeError` and `last_entry_steps()` reads less than 10,000; with `bigint_bits` 0, `1n + 1n` throws a `RangeError` and `0n + 0n` answers `0n`.

Line numbers in this body are those of `f962c324`; every anchor is found by its name (a function, an arm, a quoted comment, a test). Slice 11 is delivered first and its stories write `interp/dispatch.rs`, `conformance/host/requirements.txt` and `website/data/status.json` too; the coordinator sets the edges between the two slices on the join.
