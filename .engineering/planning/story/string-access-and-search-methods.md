## Context

Sixteen builtins of `String` that read code units or search a String: `String.fromCharCode`, `String.fromCodePoint`, `String.raw`, and of the prototype `at`, `charAt`, `charCodeAt`, `codePointAt`, `concat`, `slice`, `substring`, `lastIndexOf`, `includes`, `startsWith`, `endsWith`, `isWellFormed` and `toWellFormed`. The base has the constructor, `indexOf`, `toString` and `valueOf` (the probe of `story:builtin-group-modules`). The eleven methods that transform or compare are `story:string-transform-and-compare-methods`, in a file of its own; `match`, `matchAll`, `replace`, `replaceAll`, `search` and `split` are slice 5 (`docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 5), `normalize` too, and `[@@iterator]` is slice 6.

Measured on the base (`309b850`, the run lines of `slot task test262` on it): the sixteen directories under `test/built-ins/String/` hold 326 cases of slice 3, 307 failing (`substring` 42 of 44, `slice` 34 of 36, `charAt` 28 of 29, `raw` 24 of 26, `charCodeAt` 23, `lastIndexOf` 23, `concat` 20, `includes` 20, `endsWith` 20, `fromCharCode` 14, `startsWith` 14, `codePointAt` 11, `at` 11, `fromCodePoint` 9, `isWellFormed` 7, `toWellFormed` 7). The static scan of the failing cases of the slice (the decomposition read each case and the harness files it includes for the names of the builtins missing on the base and of the parts of later slices) finds 309 failing cases that name a builtin of this story, no builtin of another story of the wave and no part of a later slice: the list `docs/plan/slice-03/story-string-access.cases.txt` (309 lines; by directory line: `built-ins/String` 291, `language/literals` 11, `built-ins/Object` 4, `language/comments` 1, `language/expressions` 1, `language/source-text` 1; the fourteen under `test/language/` use `String.fromCharCode`, `charAt` or `charCodeAt` as a tool). A failing case that names builtins of two stories of the wave is on `docs/plan/slice-03/cross-story.tsv` and on no list, and one that needs a later slice is on `docs/plan/slice-03/move-estimate.tsv`; the ten lists, 4,050 cases, were read against the scan again when this plan was revised, and no case of a list names a builtin of another story. That list is this story's bound; a case of it that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part and the coordinator takes it off the list in the wave record. 48 failing URI cases call `String.fromCharCode` or `charAt` as a tool and pass on the wave branch with `story:global-this-uri-functions-and-is-error`. The cases of `String.raw` written with a template literal are refused by the parser and move to slice 6 at the close; the function itself needs no template (`String.raw({ raw: ["a", "b"] }, 1)`) and is delivered, though `conformance/test262/later-parts.tsv` lists its name under slice 6.

Cases that pass on the base and stop passing with this story, which it cannot keep. Two cases of slice 6, `String/prototype/slice/this-value-tostring-throws-symbol.js` and `String/prototype/substring/this-value-tostring-throws-symbol.js`, call the method with `Symbol()` as the this value inside `assert.throws(TypeError, ...)`: on the base the member `String.prototype.slice.call` throws the `TypeError` because the method is missing, before `Symbol` is read; with the method the argument is evaluated and the run ends on `ReferenceError: Symbol is not defined` until slice 6. They are cases of slice 6 and are not moved; `story:slice-03-conformance-close` lists them among the runs that passed on the base of the wave and no longer do. The decomposition read every passing case of the other slices that names a builtin of slice 3 (52 cases) and found no other of this story.

Code it builds on, checked on the base: after `story:builtin-group-modules`, `builtins/string/mod.rs` has the function for the first two steps of a method (RequireObjectCoercible, ToString), `StringBuilder` (a step per code unit copied, the `RangeError` at `string_length` before the units exist, the memory asked before it grows), `string_index_of` (StringIndexOf) and `units_of`; `@@match` is a well-known symbol of the realm, read like the five others and under no mark; CodePointAt and UTF16EncodeCodePoint are functions of `builtins/string/mod.rs` after that story, tested there, under a mark whose reason names this story and `story:global-this-uri-functions-and-is-error`; the group module `builtins/string/access.rs` is that story's, with an id type without a variant and an empty list, and `mod.rs` needs no line for a builtin of the group; ToUint16 (`sec-touint16`) does not exist (`crates/swell/src/ops/conversion.rs` has `number_to_uint32` and `number_to_int32`). No test or fixture of the repository relies on one of the sixteen being absent.

## Change

Failing tests first: the Rust tests and language scripts below are written before the code and fail (`TypeError: the value is not a function`), and the 309 cases of the list fail on the base.

- `crates/swell/src/builtins/string/access.rs`: `String.fromCharCode` (`sec-string.fromcharcode`, with ToUint16), `String.fromCodePoint` (`sec-string.fromcodepoint`: a `RangeError` for what is not an integral Number from 0 to 0x10FFFF; the units by UTF16EncodeCodePoint of `string/mod.rs`), `String.raw` (`sec-string.raw`); `at`, `charAt`, `charCodeAt`, `codePointAt` (`sec-string.prototype.at`, `.charat`, `.charcodeat`, `.codepointat`, the last with CodePointAt of `string/mod.rs`), `concat` (`sec-string.prototype.concat`), `slice` and `substring` (`sec-string.prototype.slice`, `.substring`), `lastIndexOf` with StringLastIndexOf (`sec-string.prototype.lastindexof`, `sec-stringlastindexof`), `includes`, `startsWith`, `endsWith` with IsRegExp (`sec-string.prototype.includes`, `.startswith`, `.endswith`, `sec-isregexp`: the Get of `@@match`; a `TypeError` when it answers true), `isWellFormed` and `toWellFormed` (`sec-string.prototype.iswellformed`, `.towellformed`, with IsStringWellFormedUnicode, `sec-isstringwellformedunicode`, over CodePointAt). Sixteen builtins, each complete for every this value and argument. They are entries of the group's list in the order of the clauses (22.1.2 for the constructor, 22.1.3 for the prototype), after the keys of the base, as `story:builtin-group-modules` fixes the order. Every String made is built by `StringBuilder`, so it is bounded by `string_length` and its copy is charged; every search charges a step per code unit it compares, so a search of a long String in a long String ends in `Terminated(StepBudget)` and not in time the budget does not see; what a builtin holds across a `toString` or `valueOf` of an argument is on the kept stack.

- Marks: the two of `crates/swell/src/builtins/string/mod.rs` whose reason names this story (CodePointAt, UTF16EncodeCodePoint) are removed, and nothing else changes in that file. - Tests, each in both collection modes (`test-collect`): `crates/swell/tests/builtins_string_access.rs` (new): one test per builtin for its property and its steps, among them `from_char_code_wraps_at_2_16_and_from_code_point_refuses_what_is_not_a_code_point`, `raw_interleaves_substitutions_and_stops_at_the_raw_length`, `at_and_slice_count_from_the_end`, `code_point_at_reads_a_pair_and_a_lone_surrogate`, `substring_swaps_and_clamps`, `last_index_of_with_nan_and_with_a_position`, `includes_starts_with_and_ends_with_refuse_what_is_reg_exp_by_match` (a unit test in `access.rs` sets the `@@match` property, which no script can before slice 6), `is_well_formed_and_to_well_formed_on_lone_surrogates`, `a_method_called_on_undefined_or_null_throws_type_error`, `concat_past_string_length_throws_range_error`, `a_search_charges_a_step_per_code_unit`, `step_budget_ends_a_search_of_a_long_string`, and `conversions_reach_the_reentry_limit_on_the_promised_stack` (through `reach_through` of `tests/support/stack.rs`: `"a".concat({ toString: f })` and `"a".charAt({ valueOf: f })`, on seven eighths of `min_stack_bytes()`). `crates/swell/tests/language/` (new files): `builtin-string-from-char-code.js`, `builtin-string-char-at-and-code-point-at.js`, `builtin-string-slice-and-substring.js`, `builtin-string-search.js`, `builtin-string-raw.js`.
