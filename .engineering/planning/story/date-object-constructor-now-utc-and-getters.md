## Context

Slice 4 of 19, `numbers-and-dates` (`epic:slice-04-numbers-and-dates`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 4; decision 6, "numbers and dates, whole"; decision 9, the clock and the time zone come from the host). This story is the zone half of `Date`: the constructor with components, `Date.UTC` and the 17 getters in local time and in UTC, over the time zone hooks, charged. It depends on `story:swell-num-calendar-and-local-time` (the calendar, `make_full_year`, `local_time` and `utc_from_local` of `swell-num`, which this story calls with closures over the hooks) and on `story:date-object-clock-and-time-value` (the object, its slot, `time_clip`, the function table, the test host). The first consumer needs none of it: its two reads of `Date` are the clock, which the clock story delivers. This story is the half that was in one story of the first draft; the critics' cut puts it after the clock.

On the base (`c6d44d1`; the unit reads every place named in this story again on its base) there is no `Date`; after the clock story `new Date(2016, 6)` answers the interim seam `date_from_components` (NaN with no coercion), `Date.UTC` is the interim `date_not_built`, and the 17 getters are not properties. The hooks `system_time_zone_identifier` (default "UTC"), `get_named_time_zone_offset_nanoseconds` (default 0) and `get_named_time_zone_epoch_nanoseconds` (default one instant, or empty where `swell-num` finds the clause's assertion fails) exist in `crates/swell/src/host/hooks.rs` and are never called by the engine. `TestHost` (`crates/swell/tests/support/host.rs`) has no field for the zone.

The list of this story is `docs/plan/slice-04/story-date-zone.cases.txt`: the 197 cases of the key `date-zone` of `docs/plan/slice-04/scan.tsv` (a `Date` with a component, `Date.UTC` or a getter but `getTime`, no setter call, no text; made by the command in the head of `docs/plan/slice-04/scan.awk`). By directory: `built-ins/Date/prototype` 139 (the 17 getters at 7 cases each, 17 cases `S15.9.5_A10_T1` to `A26_T1` that read `Date.prototype.hasOwnProperty` of a getter, and 3 `setTime` cases that read a getter), `built-ins/Date` direct 25 (`S15.9.2.1_A1`, `S15.9.3.1_A1`, `A2`, `A5` and `A6` at 6, 6, 6 and 5 cases, and `makedate-float-arithmetic`), `built-ins/Date/UTC` 14 (`fp-evaluation-order`, `infinity-make-day`, `infinity-make-time`, `length`, `name`, `nans`, `no-arg`, `non-integer-values`, `overflow-make-day`, `overflow-make-time`, `prop-desc`, `return-value`, `time-clip`, `year-offset`) and `built-ins/Object` 19 (a Date read through a getter).
