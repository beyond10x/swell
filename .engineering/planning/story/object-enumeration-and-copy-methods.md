## Context

Seven builtins of the `Object` constructor that list or copy the own properties of an object: `Object.keys`, `Object.values`, `Object.entries`, `Object.assign`, `Object.hasOwn`, `Object.getOwnPropertyDescriptors` and `Object.getOwnPropertySymbols`. None exists on the base (the probe of `story:builtin-group-modules`). The other builtins of `Object` are `story:object-integrity-and-prototype-methods`, in a file of its own.

Measured on the base (`309b850`, the run lines of `slot task test262` on it): the seven directories under `test/built-ins/Object/` hold 178 cases of slice 3, 167 of them failing (`hasOwn` 54 of 56, `keys` 50 of 52, `assign` 21 of 25, `values` 15 of 16, `entries` 15 of 16, `getOwnPropertyDescriptors` 10 of 11, `getOwnPropertySymbols` 2 of 2). The static scan of the failing cases of the slice (the decomposition read each case and the harness files it includes for the names of the builtins missing on the base and of the parts of later slices) finds 126 failing cases that name a builtin of this story, no builtin of another story of the wave and no part of a later slice: the list `docs/plan/slice-03/story-object-enumeration.cases.txt` (126 lines, all under `test/built-ins/Object/`). A failing case that names builtins of two stories of the wave is on `docs/plan/slice-03/cross-story.tsv` and on no list, and one that needs a later slice is on `docs/plan/slice-03/move-estimate.tsv`; the ten lists, 4,050 cases, were read against the scan again when this plan was revised, and no case of a list names a builtin of another story. That list is this story's bound; a case of it that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part and the coordinator takes it off the list in the wave record, and a case that ends on a builtin of this story is this unit's to fix. 31 more failing cases name a builtin of this story and one of `story:object-integrity-and-prototype-methods`; they pass on the wave branch.

Two builtins of clause 20.1.2 are not delivered here. `Object.fromEntries` (`sec-object.fromentries`) and `Object.groupBy` (`sec-object.groupby`) take an iterable, and nothing is iterable before slice 6 builds iterators. The cases of their two directories (24 of slice 3: 23 failing on the base, 1 passing by the `TypeError` of the missing function) are not on this story's list: by the ruling of 2026-11-21 the close (`story:slice-03-conformance-close`) moves each of these directories whole to slice 6 by a `dir` line, its passing cases included. `Object.getOwnPropertySymbols` is delivered: it needs no `Symbol` constructor, only the Symbol-keyed properties the realm already has (`Array[@@species]`, `JSON[@@toStringTag]`); `conformance/test262/later-parts.tsv` lists the name under slice 6, and its two cases, which create a Symbol, move there by the token `Symbol`.

Code it builds on, checked on the base: `get_own_property_keys(value, symbols)` and `from_property_descriptor` are `pub(crate)` in the module that becomes `builtins/object/mod.rs`; EnumerableOwnProperties (`sec-enumerableownproperties`) is an operation of `crates/swell/src/ops/objects.rs` after `story:builtin-group-modules`, with its three kinds, complete and tested there; the kinds value and key+value carry a mark whose reason names this story, which calls the operation and does not write it. The group module `builtins/object/enumeration.rs` is that story's, with an id type without a variant and an empty list, and `mod.rs` needs no line for a builtin of the group. No test or fixture of the repository relies on one of the seven being absent.

## Change

Failing tests first: the Rust tests and language scripts below are written before the code and fail (`TypeError: the value is not a function`), and the 126 cases of the list fail on the base.

- `crates/swell/src/builtins/object/enumeration.rs`: `Object.keys`, `Object.values` and `Object.entries` (`sec-object.keys`, `sec-object.values`, `sec-object.entries`: each calls EnumerableOwnProperties of `ops/objects.rs` with its kind and makes the array by CreateArrayFromList); `Object.assign` (`sec-object.assign`: every source, String and Symbol keys in the order of [[OwnPropertyKeys]], Get on the source and Set on the target with `true`); `Object.hasOwn` (`sec-object.hasown`); `Object.getOwnPropertyDescriptors` (`sec-object.getownpropertydescriptors`); `Object.getOwnPropertySymbols` (`sec-object.getownpropertysymbols`). Seven builtins, each complete for every argument, entries of the group's list in the order of the clauses of 20.1.2, after the keys of the base and of the group `integrity`, as `story:builtin-group-modules` fixes the order. Each loop charges a step per key; the list of keys and the result array are charged to the memory budget; the keys, the target and the result are on the kept stack across every Get, Set and getter.
- Marks: the two of `crates/swell/src/ops/objects.rs` whose reason names this story (the kinds value and key+value of EnumerableOwnProperties) are removed, and nothing else changes in that file. No other story of the wave removes them.
- Tests, each in both collection modes (`test-collect`): `crates/swell/tests/builtins_object_enumeration.rs` (new): one test per builtin for its property and its steps, among them `keys_of_a_string_an_array_and_arguments`, `entries_skip_a_property_a_getter_deleted`, `assign_copies_symbol_keys_and_calls_setters`, `assign_stops_at_the_first_throw_and_keeps_what_it_copied`, `get_own_property_symbols_of_the_array_constructor_is_species`, `has_own_converts_the_object_before_the_key`, `keys_charge_a_step_per_key`, `step_budget_ends_keys_of_many_properties`, and `assign_getter_and_setter_reach_the_reentry_limit_on_the_promised_stack` (through `reach_through` of `tests/support/stack.rs`: `Object.assign({}, { get p() { return f(); } })` and `Object.assign({ set p(v) { f(); } }, { p: 1 })`, on seven eighths of `min_stack_bytes()`). `crates/swell/tests/language/` (new files): `builtin-object-keys-values-entries.js`, `builtin-object-assign.js`, `builtin-object-has-own.js`, `builtin-object-get-own-property-descriptors.js`.
