## Context

Slice 4 of 19, `numbers-and-dates` (`epic:slice-04-numbers-and-dates`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 4; decision 6, "The functions of `Math` that IEEE 754 does not fix", items 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7). The second of three stories that write the `Math` functions the platform `libm` would answer differently on different targets (HOST-135). It depends on `story:swell-num-math-double-double` (the pair arithmetic, the gates, `data/math/SOURCE`) and writes the exponential and logarithmic families and `pow`: `exp`, `expm1`, `sinh`, `cosh`, `tanh`, `log`, `log2`, `log10`, `log1p`, `asinh`, `acosh`, `atanh`, `cbrt` and `exponentiate` (`Number::exponentiate`, which `Math.pow` and `**` share). It lifts no test262 case; `story:math-functions` builds the builtins and `story:exponentiation-operator` the operator on it.

On the base (`c6d44d1`; the unit reads every place named in this story again on its base) none of these functions exists in `swell-num`; `crates/swell/src/builtins/math.rs` has `max` only (`story:math-constants-and-exact-functions` adds the exact ones). The design text on these algorithms was written and not run: the numbers in it (the series degrees, the accuracy of each group, 2^-80 as the cut) are claims, and this unit measures them.

Design it follows, by name, with the bullets and items of decision 6 it builds on: "The functions of `Math` that IEEE 754 does not fix", item 2 (`exp`, `expm1`, `sinh`, `cosh`, `tanh`: x = k ln 2 + r with ln 2 in three parts of 32, 32 and 53 bits, the Maclaurin series through r^18 in double-double, the result times 2^k in two factors when needed, `expm1` with the leading term exact below ln 2 / 2, below -38.5 it is -1, above 709.79 it is +∞), item 3 (`log`, `log2`, `log10`, `log1p`: x = 2^k m with m in [√2/2, √2), s = f/(2 + f) in double-double, the series through s^29, `log2(2^k)` exact for k from -1074 to 1023 and `log10(10^n)` exact for n from 0 to 22), item 5 (`asinh`, `acosh`, `atanh` over `log1p` and `sqrt` in double-double), item 6 (`pow`, the draft's special values written as the draft has them, then 2^(y log2 |x|) in double-double, a result that is exactly a Number returned exactly, `pow(2, -2^31)` is +0 by underflow) and item 7 (`cbrt`: `exp(log(|x|)/3)` in double-double and one Newton step; a perfect cube gives its root exactly); the paragraphs "Constants and how a reader checks them", "The accuracy the engine promises" (group A, `exp log log2 log10 pow cbrt`: less than 1 ulp, faithful; group B, `expm1 log1p sinh cosh tanh asinh acosh atanh`: at most 2 ulp; the results the draft fixes and the exact cases are exact in both) and "How a test holds the bound with no second implementation" with "The tool that makes the lines" (`bc -l`, steps 1 to 6); "Which `f64` methods an engine crate may call"; ECMA-262 21.3.2 for each function (`#sec-math.exp` and the others) and `#sec-numeric-types-number-exponentiate`.

**Prerequisite of the design author, not in the `Writes` of this story: the reference data of `Math`.** The design names one file `data/math/reference.txt` and one test `tests/math.rs`, and the work is cut in three stories that run in parallel. The sentence below is written into `docs/design/engine.md` by the design change that is merged into `wave/s10-1` before the unit starts (it is the same sentence as in `story:swell-num-math-double-double`, which holds the gate for it too), and the unit follows it as written; the start gate of the acceptance is its first line. The count of lines of this story's file is the design's 200 per function and the special values: about 3,500. The sentence:

> **Reference data of Math** (decision 6, in the paragraph "How a test holds the bound with no second implementation", in place of a single file and its one test). Each of the three stories that write `Math` functions in `swell-num` commits its own reference file, in the line format and by the procedure of "The tool that makes the lines": `data/math/reference-hypot.txt` (`story:swell-num-math-double-double`: the lines of `hypot`, the only function it writes), `data/math/reference-exp-log.txt` (`story:swell-num-math-exp-and-log`) and `data/math/reference-trig.txt` (`story:swell-num-math-trigonometry`). Each file has one line `<sha256>  <file>` in `data/math/SOURCE`, which `math-data-check` reads, and is read by the test file of its story (`crates/num/tests/math_dd.rs`, `math_exp_log.rs`, `math_trig.rs`, modules of the crate's one test binary).

**Not run, to be measured by the unit.** For each series the unit finds the degree by the design's rule (cut where the first omitted term is below 2^-80 of the result's magnitude on the whole reduced interval), states it beside the design's (exp through r^18, log through s^29) and reports a difference; it measures the largest error in ulp of each function over the reference data and over a denser walk of its own (the walk may not use the platform `libm`), states it beside the design's group bound, and reports a function that misses its group.

## Change

Failing tests first: the tests below are written before the code and fail on the base (the functions do not exist).

- `crates/num/src/math/exp.rs` (new): `exp`, `expm1`, `sinh`, `cosh`, `tanh` and the helper `exp_dd`, as item 2 writes them. `crates/num/src/math/log.rs` (new): `log`, `log2`, `log10`, `log1p`, `asinh`, `acosh`, `atanh` and `log_dd`, as items 3 and 5 write them (the split of x by `to_bits` and `from_bits`, subnormals scaled by 2^54 first, the exact pair `1 + x` by `two_sum` for `log1p`). `crates/num/src/math/pow.rs` (new): `exponentiate` with the draft's table of special values for the cases before the last line (a NaN exponent, a zero exponent, a NaN base, the infinities and zeros with the rule of the odd integer, an infinite exponent against |base| 1 which is NaN, and a negative base with a non-integral exponent which is NaN) and item 6 for the rest, `cbrt` (item 7). Each series is evaluated by Horner's rule in double-double, each coefficient a pair (the exact rational rounded to `hi`, its remainder rounded to `lo`), stored as `f64::from_bits` pairs. `crates/num/src/math.rs` gets `pub mod exp; pub mod log; pub mod pow;` and the `pub use` lines (the one shared file with the trigonometry story). Each function is straight code over a fixed number of terms: no loop depends on the argument but the two-factor scaling, which is a fixed pair of multiplications.
- The constants and the test that recomputes them. `ln 2` as three parts of 32, 32 and 53 bits, `ln 10`, `1/ln 2`, `1/ln 10` and the series coefficients are stored as bit pairs; `crates/num/tests/math_exp_log.rs` recomputes each from its defining fraction with exact integer arithmetic (`crates/num/tests/support/exact.rs` of the first story) and compares bit for bit: `ln 2` and `ln 10` by the series of 2 atanh of a rational, each to 1,400 bits. A constant that no test recomputes does not ship.
- The reference data and its test. `data/math/reference-exp-log.txt` (new): one line per case, the function, the bits of the argument or arguments (16 hex digits each) and the bits of the two Numbers that enclose the exact result (equal when it is a Number), 200 arguments per function drawn by the design's Knuth LCG (seed 1) with a binary exponent uniform in the range the file's header gives for the function, plus the special values, ±0, ±1, the powers of two and the thresholds of each function (709.78..., -745.13..., -38.5, 22, 2^-28 and 2^28 where the design names them). The header of the file and a line of `data/math/SOURCE` name the tool (POSIX `bc -l`, the version that made the file), say the procedure of the design in words (steps 1 to 6 there) and give the SHA-256; `math-data-check` of the first story accepts it. The lines are made once, outside the repository, by a POSIX `sh` script driven by `bc -l`: the script is not committed and no task, gate or other session calls it; it runs in the background and starts without `slot` (it measures and generates, it does not build or test: `AGENTS.md`, "Scripts that do not build stay off slot"); the unit states its wall time (the design expects minutes to tens of minutes of one core) and names the procedure of each step in the header. The test `crates/num/tests/math_exp_log.rs` reads the file with `include_str!` (no file I/O in the test), holds group A to "one of the two" and group B to a distance of at most 2 ulp from the nearer, and runs in under a second; it states its entry count and its time. A line the procedure leaves out (step 2: still in doubt after 4,000 digits) is counted in the header.
- The exact cases, written by hand into the test: `exp(±0)` is 1, `cosh(±0)` is 1, `log2(2^k)` is k for every k from -1074 to 1023, `log10(10^n)` is n for n from 0 to 22, `log10(10)`, `log10(100)`, `log10(1000)`, `cbrt` of every perfect cube from -1000 to 1000, `pow(2, 10)` is 1024, `pow(10, 2)` is 100, `pow(2, -2^31)` is +0, `pow(1, -2^31)` is 1, `pow(1, ±∞)` is NaN, `pow(-8, 1/3)` is NaN, `tanh(±∞)` is ±1, `exp(710)` is +∞, `exp(-746)` is +0, `expm1(-40)` is -1, `log(-1)` is NaN, `log(0)` is -∞, `log1p(-1)` is -∞, `atanh(±1)` is ±∞, `acosh(0.5)` is NaN, and the draft's table of `pow` entry by entry (the unit counts them). Every function gets NaN, ±∞, ±0 and a subnormal as arguments in one table.
- The `f64` operations and casts of this story, each with what fixes its result: `+ - * /` and `sqrt` (IEEE 754-2019 5.4.1: correctly rounded), `floor`, `abs` and `copysign` (exact, 5.9 and 5.5.1), `to_bits` and `from_bits` (exact), comparisons, and `as f64` and `as i32` (the Rust Reference, "Numeric cast": exact for an `i32` read as `f64`; an `f64` to `i32` cast follows a range test that the line names). The nearest integer k of the reduction is `floor` of the double-double quotient `x / ln 2` plus one half, decided by a comparison of the remainder, and never `round_ties_even`; k is below 2^11 in magnitude for the exponential and below 1,100 for the scaling. No `mul_add`, no `powi`, no `exp2`, no `cbrt`, no `round_ties_even`.
