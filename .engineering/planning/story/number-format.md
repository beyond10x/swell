## Context

Slice 4 of 19, `numbers-and-dates` (`epic:slice-04-numbers-and-dates`; `docs/design/engine.md` decision 6, bullet "Big integers in slice 4", added on the design branch; ECMA-262 21.1.3.2 to 21.1.3.5). This story makes `Number.prototype.toFixed`, `toExponential`, `toPrecision` and `toLocaleString` callable, on an exact unsigned integer type `Nat` that it moves out of `swell-num`'s private `Big` as its first commit. It has no edge to another story of slice 4: it reads none of their code and its 48 cases need none of their functions (the scan finds no key edge for it).

On the base (`c6d44d1`; the unit reads every place named in this story again on its base) `crates/swell/src/builtins/number.rs` has `Number`, `valueOf` and `toString`; `crates/num/src/to_string.rs` has `Number::toString` in every radix over the private `Big` (`from_u64`, `trim`, `bit_len`, `bits_from`, `shl`, `add`, `sub_assign`, `cmp`, `cmp_sum`) and `shortest_digits` (`pub(crate)`). `Big` has no multiplication, no division and no shift to the right.

The list is `docs/plan/slice-04/story-number-format.cases.txt`: the 48 cases of the key `number-format` of `docs/plan/slice-04/scan.tsv`: `toPrecision` 14, `toFixed` 12, `toExponential` 12, `Number/prototype` 4 (`S15.7.4_A3.3`, `A3.5`, `A3.6`, `A3.7`: the four methods exist as functions), `Object/getOwnPropertyDescriptor` 4 (`15.2.3.3-4-90` to `-93`), `toLocaleString` 1 (`prop-desc`) and `language/expressions/property-accessors/S11.2.1_A3_T2`. By edge: none reads another story. The guard is the 250 cases of `built-ins/Number` that pass on the base (`toString` 89, `valueOf` 10, the four value constants 16, 11 of `Number/prototype`, and the others of `toExponential` 2, `toFixed` 2, `toLocaleString` 3 and `toPrecision` 2).

Design it follows, by name: `docs/design/engine.md` decision 6, bullet "Big integers in slice 4" (the type, the move, the 2,048-bit bound, the charges); decision 4 (a temporary structure is credited when it drops); decision 3 (steps).

**Prerequisite of the design author, not in the `Writes` of this story: the text of `toLocaleString`.** ECMA-262 21.1.3.4 leaves the text to the implementation and ECMA-402 is not in the engine; test262 asserts only `prop-desc` of this method. The sentence below is written into `docs/design/engine.md` before the unit starts, and the unit follows it as written; the start gate of the acceptance is its first line. It is the same sentence that `story:date-text-formats` names. The sentence:

> **The text of toLocale*String** (decision 6, beside the `Date.parse` bullet). `Number.prototype.toLocaleString` gives the text of `Number.prototype.toString()` of the same Number, and `Date.prototype.toLocaleString`, `toLocaleDateString` and `toLocaleTimeString` give the text of `toString`, `toDateString` and `toTimeString` of the same Date, each through the same one conversion; there is no locale behaviour (`Intl` is excluded). `Date.parse` reads the text of `toString`, `toUTCString` and `toISOString`, so `Date.parse(d.toLocaleString())` is `d` to the second; it answers NaN for the text of `toDateString`, `toTimeString`, `toLocaleDateString` and `toLocaleTimeString`.

## What later stories reuse

`swell_num::nat::Nat` (`pub(crate)`) is what slice 12 puts a sign and `BigInt`'s operations on. Nothing else of slice 4 uses it.

## Domain relations

- `Nat`: an unsigned magnitude of limbs, least significant first, no zero limb at the top - stated (decision 6). A call of `toFixed`, `toExponential` or `toPrecision` -> its temporaries: at most six operands of 32 limbs, 1,536 bytes, charged as one temporary of 2,048 bytes before the call into `swell-num` and credited on return - stated (decision 6).

- The formatting. `crates/num/src/format.rs` (new, `pub mod format;`): `to_fixed(x: f64, fraction_digits: u32) -> String` (x finite and `abs(x) < 1e21`; the spec step: "let n be an integer for which n / 10^f - x is as close to zero as possible; if there are two such n, pick the larger n", so a tie rounds up in magnitude for a positive x and the sign is written after), `to_exponential(x: f64, fraction_digits: Option<u32>) -> String` (`None` is the digits of `shortest_digits(x, 10)`; `Some(f)` picks e and n with 10^f <= n < 10^(f+1) and n * 10^(e-f) - x closest to zero, the larger n of two) and `to_precision(x: f64, precision: u32) -> String` (the same choice with p digits, then the exponent form when e < -6 or e >= p, the fixed form otherwise). The caller has already dealt with NaN and the infinities, the range of the digits (0 to 100 for `toFixed` and `toExponential`, 1 to 100 for `toPrecision`) and the sign. The arithmetic is exact: x is read by `to_bits` as a 53-bit mantissa and an exponent, and the quotient or the product against the power of ten is made with `Nat` and `shr`, the half tested by comparing the remainder with half the divisor; no `f64` operation decides a digit. The `f64` operations of this module: `to_bits`, `is_sign_negative`, `abs` (a bit operation, exact on every platform), comparison with `1e21` (a literal, exact), nothing else.
