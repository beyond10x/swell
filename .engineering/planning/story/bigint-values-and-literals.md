## Context

Slice 12 brings `BigInt` (`epic:slice-12-bigint`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 12, decisions 3, 4 and 6). This story makes the BigInt a value of the engine: its heap record and arena, its charges and the `bigint_bits` limit, the literals, `typeof`, ToBoolean, ToString and ToPropertyKey, unary `-` and `!`, strict equality, SameValue and SameValueZero (with BigInt keys of `Map` and `Set`), and HOST-219. It is in order 2: it depends on `story:swell-num-bigint-core` (the type, the text both ways, `literal_value`, the cost and size functions) and is built at the same time as `story:swell-num-bigint-powers-shifts-and-bits`, with which it shares no file. The three stories of order 3 depend on it.

Measured by the decomposition on `f962c324` with the full run of that commit (`test262-slice 12 381 760 0 0 381 39 342 0`, equal to `conformance/test262/expected.txt` there): the lexer refuses every BigInt literal (`crates/syntax/src/lexer.rs` line 570, "BigInt literal is not supported before slice 12"), so no BigInt value can be made, and the engine answers `Terminated(EngineFault)` wherever one would arrive (the arms that say "No BigInt exists before slice 12"). The list of this story, `docs/plan/slice-12/story-values.cases.txt`, holds the 146 cases of the key `values` of `docs/plan/slice-12/scan.tsv` (the command is in the head of `docs/plan/slice-12/scan.awk`), 291 runs: 107 cases fail, every one of their 213 failing runs on "BigInt literal is not supported before slice 12" (18 of them inside `temporalHelpers.js`, which 9 cases of `built-ins/Array/fromAsync` include and whose BigInt functions they do not call); 39 cases pass already, the negative cases of `language/literals/bigint` (an exponent, a fraction, a legacy octal-like or non-octal prefix, a misplaced separator), whose expected `SyntaxError` the refusal gives today and the lexer's own errors must give after this story. By directory: `language/literals/bigint` 59, `built-ins/Set/prototype` 21 (the set methods given a BigInt where a set-like object or a size is read), `built-ins/Array/fromAsync` 14, `built-ins/RegExp/prototype` 10 (`this-val-non-obj`), `language/expressions/strict-equals` 7 and `strict-does-not-equals` 7, `built-ins/Promise` 4, `built-ins/Object` 3 (`setPrototypeOf`, `defineProperty`, `defineProperties`), `language/expressions/logical-assignment` 3, `language/statements/class/elements` 2 (a private field read and written on a BigInt receiver), `language/expressions/assignment/destructuring` 2, `built-ins/Map` 2 and `built-ins/Set/bigint-number-same-value` 1 (BigInt keys), and one each of `Math/sumPrecise`, `Number/prototype/toFixed`, `String/fromCharCode`, `Array/prototype/toSorted`, `AsyncIteratorPrototype`, `harness/compare-array-message`, `harness/testIntl-getLocaleBaseName`, `language/expressions/object/literal-property-name-bigint`, `unary-minus`, `unary-plus` and `logical-not`. A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit on the wave branch whose body names that part and adds it, in the same commit, to `docs/plan/slice-12/close-later.tsv` (path, the story that takes it, one reason), which `sh docs/plan/slice-12/lists.sh` counts as a list; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

The 3 cases of `strict-equals`, `strict-does-not-equals` and `unary-minus` that make a wrapper object (`Object(2n)`) are not on this list: ToObject of a BigInt is `story:bigint-constructor-and-prototype`'s, and the scan gives them to that story.

Line numbers in this body are those of `f962c324`; every anchor is found by its name (a function, a variant, a test, a quoted comment). Slice 11 is delivered first, and its stories write many of the frontend, heap and dispatch files this story writes, so the lines will have moved when the unit starts; the coordinator sets the edges between the two slices on the join.

HOST-219 (`docs/design/host-interface.md` line 1916, `[slice 12]`) is this story's: a BigInt kept only in a host object's data and listed by its `trace` survives `collect`.

## Domain relations

- A heap BigInt record → its limbs: one to one, owned, charged with the record and credited at its sweep; the record is immutable after it is made — inferable (inferred from `docs/design/engine.md` decision 4, "Heap": "arenas, one per kind of thing (objects, strings, `BigInt` digits, ...)", and "Memory budget": "`BigInt` limbs" charged; ECMA-262 6.1.6.2 makes a BigInt a primitive, so nothing changes it; no ess/1 document declares it: `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value).
- A host object's data → a BigInt it keeps: many to one, not owned, kept alive while the host's `trace` lists it — inferable (inferred from `docs/design/host-interface.md`, HOST-109, HOST-218 and HOST-219, decision 15; no ess/1 document declares it).
- A Map or Set table → a BigInt key: many to one, not owned; two BigInts of one value are one key — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 SameValueZero, `#sec-samevaluezero`, and `crates/swell/src/object/keyed_entries.rs` lines 128 to 162, `EntryKey`; no ess/1 document declares it).
