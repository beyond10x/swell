## Context

`Object` has five of the 24 properties clause 20.1.2 gives its constructor and four of the seven clause 20.1.3 gives its prototype outside Annex B (the probe of `story:builtin-group-modules`). This story delivers thirteen of the missing ones: the six that set and test the integrity of an object, `Object.setPrototypeOf`, `Object.is`, `Object.create`, `Object.defineProperties`, and `isPrototypeOf`, `toLocaleString` and `valueOf` of `Object.prototype`. They are the ones the rest of test262 uses as tools: `Object.create`, `Object.preventExtensions` and `isPrototypeOf` are the first missing name in 95 failing cases outside their own directories, and 37 of the 78 cases slice 2 moved to slice 3 wait for a builtin of this story (`Object.preventExtensions` 22, `isPrototypeOf` 6, `valueOf` 4, `Object.create` 3, `setPrototypeOf` 1, `defineProperties` 1) (`awk -F'\t' '$3 == 3 { print $5 }' conformance/test262/moves.tsv | sort | uniq -c`).

Measured on the base (`309b850`, the run lines of `slot task test262` on it): the thirteen directories of these methods under `test/built-ins/Object/` hold 1,294 cases of slice 3, 1,121 of them failing (`defineProperties` 491 of 627, `create` 289 of 317, `seal` 84 of 84, `isFrozen` 57, `freeze` 46, `isExtensible` 37, `preventExtensions` 36, `isSealed` 31, `Object.is` 17, `valueOf` 16, `toLocaleString` 9, `setPrototypeOf` 5, `isPrototypeOf` 3). A static scan of every failing case of the slice (the decomposition read each case and the harness files it includes, comments removed and string contents blanked, for the names of the builtins missing on the base and of the parts of later slices) finds 1,109 failing cases that name a builtin of this story, no builtin of another story of the wave and no part of a later slice: the list `docs/plan/slice-03/story-object-integrity.cases.txt` (1,109 lines; by directory line: `built-ins/Object` 1,052, `language/expressions` 19, `language/statements` 7, `built-ins/Array` 6, `language/global-code` 4, `built-ins/String` 4, `built-ins/Error` 4, `built-ins/Function` 4, `language/types` 3, `built-ins/Boolean` 3, `built-ins/ThrowTypeError` 2, `built-ins/JSON` 1). A failing case that names builtins of two stories of the wave is on `docs/plan/slice-03/cross-story.tsv` and on no list, and one that needs a later slice is on `docs/plan/slice-03/move-estimate.tsv`; the ten lists, 4,050 cases, were read against the scan again when this plan was revised, and no case of a list names a builtin of another story. That list is this story's bound. The scan reads names, not behaviour: a case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part and the coordinator takes it off the list in the wave record; a case that ends on a builtin of this story is this unit's to fix. 218 cases that name a builtin of this story pass on the base, most because the call of the missing function throws the `TypeError` the case expects; they run the builtin for the first time here.

Code it builds on, checked on the base and against `story:builtin-group-modules`: SetIntegrityLevel, TestIntegrityLevel and Invoke are operations of `crates/swell/src/ops/objects.rs` after that story, complete and tested there, each under a mark whose reason names this story; this story calls them and writes none of them. `set_prototype_of` exists with its internal method and carries `expect(dead_code, reason = "Object.setPrototypeOf: story:harness-builtins")` (`crates/swell/src/object/mod.rs`, line 259), which this story fulfils; the mark of `prevent_extensions` is gone after that story. `to_property_descriptor`, `from_property_descriptor`, `get_own_property_keys` and `create_array_from_list` are `pub(crate)` in the module that story moves to `builtins/object/mod.rs`; the group module `builtins/object/integrity.rs` is that story's, with an id type without a variant and an empty list, and `mod.rs` needs no line for a builtin of the group. No test or fixture of the repository relies on one of the thirteen being absent (`grep -rn` over `crates/`, `conformance/`, `tests/` for each name: none outside comments; the fixtures of `crates/test262/tests/fixtures/` name an identifier no slice defines).

## Change

Failing tests first: the Rust tests and language scripts below are written before the code and fail (`TypeError: the value is not a function`), and the 1,109 cases of the list fail on the base.

- `crates/swell/src/builtins/object/integrity.rs`: `Object.preventExtensions` (`sec-object.preventextensions`), `Object.isExtensible`, `Object.seal`, `Object.isSealed`, `Object.freeze`, `Object.isFrozen` (`sec-object.seal`, `sec-object.issealed`, `sec-object.freeze`, `sec-object.isfrozen`: each calls SetIntegrityLevel or TestIntegrityLevel of `ops/objects.rs`, which `story:builtin-group-modules` delivers), `Object.setPrototypeOf` (`sec-object.setprototypeof`), `Object.is` (`sec-object.is`), `Object.create` (`sec-object.create`), `Object.defineProperties` with ObjectDefineProperties (`sec-objectdefineproperties`: every descriptor is read before one is defined), `Object.prototype.isPrototypeOf` (`sec-object.prototype.isprototypeof`), `Object.prototype.toLocaleString` (`sec-object.prototype.tolocalestring`: Invoke of `ops/objects.rs` with "toString", the definition of ECMA-262 without ECMA-402) and `Object.prototype.valueOf` (`sec-object.prototype.valueof`): thirteen builtins, each complete for every argument, on Array, String and arguments exotic objects as on ordinary ones. They are entries of the group's list in the order of the clauses (20.1.2 for the constructor, 20.1.3 for the prototype), after the keys of the base, as `story:builtin-group-modules` fixes the order. A loop over own keys charges a step per key and the walk of `isPrototypeOf` a step per object it passes; what a builtin holds across a Get, a getter or a `toString` is on the kept stack.
- Marks: the three of `crates/swell/src/ops/objects.rs` whose reason names this story (SetIntegrityLevel, TestIntegrityLevel, Invoke) and the one of `set_prototype_of` in `crates/swell/src/object/mod.rs` are removed, and nothing else changes in those two files. The mark of SetIntegrityLevel also names `story:json-parse-and-raw-json` and the mark of Invoke `story:array-search-and-copy-methods`: that story deletes the same lines, and the merge takes the one change from both sides.
- A defect the cases of the list show in the object model of an earlier slice ([[DefineOwnProperty]] or [[Delete]] of an Array, an arguments object or a String object that is not extensible or has properties that are not configurable: `crates/swell/src/object/{array,arguments,string}.rs`) is fixed where it is, with a test; the report lists each. `crates/swell/src/object/ordinary.rs` is not this story's: it has one writer in the wave, `story:json-parse-and-raw-json`, for the slot [[IsRawJSON]]. A defect there, or in an operation of `ops/objects.rs`, is reported with the case and its failed run, and the coordinator extends the story or takes the case off the list (`AGENTS.md`, "How the work is run").
- Tests, each in both collection modes (`test-collect`): `crates/swell/tests/builtins_object_integrity.rs` (new): one test per builtin for its property (`length`, `name`, attributes, not a constructor) and its steps, among them `freeze_of_an_array_and_of_arguments`, `seal_keeps_values_writable`, `integrity_tests_of_primitives_and_of_empty_objects`, `set_prototype_of_refuses_a_cycle_and_the_immutable_prototype`, `define_properties_reads_every_descriptor_before_it_defines_one`, `create_with_a_null_prototype_and_properties`, `is_prototype_of_charges_a_step_per_object` (a chain of 1,000 prototypes costs at least 1,000 steps more than one, from `last_entry_steps()`), `freeze_charges_a_step_per_key`, `step_budget_ends_a_freeze_of_many_keys`, `to_primitive_of_an_ordinary_object_calls_value_of_first`, and `define_properties_getter_reaches_the_reentry_limit_on_the_promised_stack` (through `reach_through` of `tests/support/stack.rs`: `Object.defineProperties({}, { p: { get value() { return f(); } } })` and `({ toString: f }).toLocaleString()` re-enter until the `RangeError`, on seven eighths of `min_stack_bytes()`). `crates/swell/tests/language/` (new files): `builtin-object-freeze-and-seal.js`, `builtin-object-prevent-extensions-strict-assignment.js` (strict-only), `builtin-object-create-and-define-properties.js`, `builtin-object-set-prototype-of.js`, `builtin-object-is.js`, `builtin-object-prototype-value-of.js`.
