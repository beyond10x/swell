## Context

Slice 5 is delivered next by what the first consumer needs (`docs/plan/waves.md`, "The order after slice 6"; the 12 regular expression literals of its scripts and the calls on them are listed in `docs/plan/slice-05/consumer-regexps.tsv`). None of `String.prototype.match`, `matchAll`, `replace`, `replaceAll`, `search` and `split` exists on the base: the lists of `crates/swell/src/builtins/string/mod.rs` (line 281), `access.rs` (line 83) and `transform.rs` (line 82) name none of them, and slice 3 left them to slice 5 (`docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 5; the lines `dir test/built-ins/String/prototype/<method>/ 5` of the block). This story brings the three of those methods whose steps make no RegExp: `replace` (`#sec-string.prototype.replace`), `replaceAll` (`#sec-string.prototype.replaceall`) and `split` (`#sec-string.prototype.split`), each with the steps that hand an argument that is neither undefined nor null and has a method keyed by `@@replace` or `@@split` to that method, and GetSubstitution (`#sec-getsubstitution`), which `RegExp.prototype[@@replace]` of `story:regexp-symbol-methods-and-string-match-search` calls as well: it comes here with its own tests, and that story uses it and does not write it. `match`, `matchAll` and `search` make a RegExp from a String argument (RegExpCreate, then Invoke of the well-known method), so they come with `RegExp`. It runs beside `story:regexp-crate-and-pattern-parser` and `story:unicode-gen-table-list-and-source-digests`, which write no file of `crates/swell`. Lifts of the consumer: none of the 12 literals; the 8 calls of `split` with a String.

The list of this story is `docs/plan/slice-05/story-string-replace-split.cases.txt`: 89 failing cases (176 runs) of slice 5 whose text calls `replace`, `replaceAll` or `split` or which are in their directories, whose first failing run ends on no counted refusal and not on `RegExp is not defined`, and whose text, with its comments taken out, has neither `RegExp` nor a regular expression literal. By directory: `built-ins/String/prototype/split` 30, `replaceAll` 28, `replace` 22, `language/function-code` 6 (`"ab".replace("b", f)` with a strict or sloppy `f`), `language/statements` 2, `built-ins/JSON` 1. 61 of them end on `the value is not a function` on the base. Found by `docs/plan/slice-05/scan.awk` (its head gives the command; over the first failing run of each failing case of slice 5 on `e444aaf`, the run lines of `slot task test262`), whose assignment of every failing case of slice 5 is `docs/plan/slice-05/scan.tsv`. The three cases `replaceAll/getSubstitution-0x0024-0x003C.js`, `getSubstitution-0x0024N.js` and `getSubstitution-0x0024NN.js` evaluate the literal `/./g` and are on the list of `story:regexp-symbol-methods-and-string-match-search`, the first story after both this one and `story:regexp-objects-exec-and-literals`. ## Change

Failing tests first: the tests and scripts below are written before the code and fail on the base, where the three methods do not exist.

- `crates/swell/src/builtins/string/replace.rs` (new), a group file in the form of `story:builtin-group-modules` (the id type of its builtins, the list of the methods it defines on `%String.prototype%` in the order of the clauses, the function that runs one of them; `builtins/mod.rs`, the module comment): `replace`, `replaceAll` and `split` with every step of their clauses, in their order. RequireObjectCoercible of the this value; for a `searchValue` or `separator` neither undefined nor null, GetMethod of `@@replace` or `@@split` (the symbols through `Agent::well_known`, `realm/mod.rs` lines 52 to 58) and its Call with the arguments the clause gives; for `replaceAll`, IsRegExp first (`Agent::is_reg_exp`, `access.rs` line 220, `pub(crate)` already) and, when it is true, Get of `flags`, RequireObjectCoercible and ToString, and the TypeError when the flags have no `g`. Then the String paths: StringIndexOf (`Agent::string_index_of`, `mod.rs` line 493), the positions of `replaceAll` (each found with `advanceBy` = max(1, searchLength)), the Call of a functional replacer with (matched, position, string) and ToString of its result, GetSubstitution for a String replacement; `split` with ToUint32 of the limit, the separator converted before the limit of zero is read, as the clause orders it, and the empty separator splitting into code units.
- GetSubstitution (`#sec-getsubstitution`): `$$`, `$&`, `` $` ``, `$'`, `$n` and `$nn` with the rule for a two-digit reference past the count of captures, and `$<name>` with a namedCaptures object read by Get and ToString, a `pub(crate)` function of this file that takes the matched String, the String, the position, the captures (each a String or undefined) and namedCaptures (undefined or an object) and the template. The String paths here call it with no captures; the RegExp story calls it with its captures.
- Registration, in `builtins/string/mod.rs`: the `mod replace;` line, a variant `Replace(replace::ReplaceBuiltin)` of `StringBuiltin` (line 45), its arm where the groups run (line 358), and its list read with the lists of the groups (lines 311 to 332 and 545 to 558), as `access` and `transform` are.
- Steps and memory: each String a method builds goes through `StringBuilder` (`mod.rs`, the module comment: bounded by `string_length`, charged before it grows, a step per code unit it copies); each search charges the code units it reads before it reads them, as StringIndexOf does; the array of `split` grows through the dense elements of the engine, charged before each growth, and a step per element. A test reaches `string_length` with `replaceAll` (a `RangeError`) and the step budget with `split` (`Terminated(StepBudget)`).
- Re-entry: a functional replacer and a method keyed by `@@replace` or `@@split` are calls of the call path of the agent (a re-entry); a test calls `replace` with a replacer that calls `replace` again until the re-entry limit, on seven eighths of `min_stack_bytes()` (`reach_through` of `crates/swell/tests/support/stack.rs`). Each builtin does its work in functions of its own; nothing is added to the frame of `Agent::execute`, and `STACK_BYTES_PER_REENTRY` does not change.
- Tests, each in both collection modes (`test-collect`): `crates/swell/tests/builtins_string_replace.rs` (new): `replace_replaces_the_first_match_with_a_string_or_a_function`, `replace_all_replaces_every_match_and_advances_past_an_empty_search`, `split_splits_on_a_string_up_to_the_limit_and_into_code_units_on_the_empty_string`, `split_converts_the_separator_before_it_reads_a_limit_of_zero`, `an_argument_with_a_symbol_method_is_handed_to_it` (objects with `Symbol.replace` and `Symbol.split` answer their own result; the method is not looked up on undefined or null), `replace_all_throws_for_a_regexp_like_object_whose_flags_lack_g` (an object whose `Symbol.match` is true), `the_methods_throw_on_undefined_and_null_this`, `split_charges_each_element` (`last_entry_steps()` after splitting 1,000 parts exceeds that after 10 by at least 990), `split_of_a_long_string_ends_at_the_step_budget`, `replace_all_past_string_length_throws_a_range_error`, `a_replacer_re_enters_on_the_promised_stack`; a unit test of `replace.rs`, `get_substitution_reads_every_pattern`, which calls GetSubstitution with captures and named captures directly (every row of the table of the clause, two-digit references past the count, a `$<` with no named captures).
