## Context

Slice 4 of 19, `numbers-and-dates` (`epic:slice-04-numbers-and-dates`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 4; decision 6, "The functions of `Math` that IEEE 754 does not fix", the builtin half). This story makes the 22 `Math` functions that need an algorithm callable from a script: `acos`, `acosh`, `asin`, `asinh`, `atan`, `atan2`, `atanh`, `cbrt`, `cos`, `cosh`, `exp`, `expm1`, `hypot`, `log`, `log10`, `log1p`, `log2`, `pow`, `sin`, `sinh`, `tan`, `tanh`, over the three `swell-num` stories, and holds HOST-135, the requirement that a script prints the same bytes on every release platform. It depends on `story:math-constants-and-exact-functions` (the table, `MathBuiltin`, `coerce_all`) and on `story:swell-num-math-double-double`, `story:swell-num-math-exp-and-log` and `story:swell-num-math-trigonometry` (the functions). It has no edge to `story:number-functions`: the two cases that need both (`harness/nans.js` and `language/expressions/typeof/built-in-functions.js`) are on the joint list of `story:slice-04-conformance-close`. The unit reads every place named in this story again on its base (`wave/s10-1` moved since this text was written).

On the base (`c6d44d1`) `%Math%` has `max` only; after `story:math-constants-and-exact-functions` it has the constants and the 11 exact functions. The first consumer (surf) calls none of these. `swell-num` has none of the algorithms until the three stories before this one.

The list is `docs/plan/slice-04/story-math-functions.cases.txt`: the 189 cases of the key `math-functions` of `docs/plan/slice-04/scan.tsv`. By directory: `built-ins/Math` 153 (`pow` 26, `hypot` 11, `atan2` 10, `tan`, `log`, `exp`, `cos` and `asin` 8 each, `sin` and `acos` 7, `atan` 6, `acosh` 6, `tanh`, `sinh`, `log2`, `log1p`, `log10`, `expm1`, `cosh`, `cbrt`, `atanh` and `asinh` 4 each), `built-ins/Object` 12, `built-ins/Array` 10, `built-ins/String` 4, `language/types` 3, `language/statements` 3, `built-ins/isNaN` 2, and one each in `built-ins/RegExp` and `built-ins/isFinite` (the global `isNaN` and `isFinite` exist on the base). By edge: 182 read only the Math story and 7 also read the exact functions (the edge to `story:math-constants-and-exact-functions`). The two cases of the key that needed `story:number-functions` as well, `harness/nans.js` and `language/expressions/typeof/built-in-functions.js`, are not on the list (191 before): they are the `joint` rows of `scan.tsv` and `docs/plan/slice-04/close-joint.cases.txt`. The guard is the 13 cases of `built-ins/Math` that pass on the base (`max` 10, `prop-desc`, `proto`, `Symbol.toStringTag`). The cases that stay failing after this story are `Math.random` and `Math.sumPrecise` (`story:math-random`, `story:math-sum-precise`) and `Math.f16round` (slice 13).

Design it follows, by name: `docs/design/engine.md` decision 6, "The functions of `Math` that IEEE 754 does not fix" (item 6: `pow` is `Number::exponentiate`; item 8: `hypot` coerces every argument first, then answers by the order of the draft), "The accuracy the engine promises" and the paragraph "test262, pinned" (the cases assert special values and the exact results `cosh(±0)`, `cos(±0)`, `exp(±0)`, `hypot(3, 4)`, `log10(10)`, `log10(100)`, `log10(1000)`, `log2(2)`, `log2(4)`, `log2(8)`, `pow(1, -2^31)`, `tanh(+∞)`, which the three stories hold exactly); decision 3 (the step budget); `docs/design/host-interface.md` HOST-135 and HOST-133 (the same host answers give the same results); ECMA-262 21.3.2 for each function.
