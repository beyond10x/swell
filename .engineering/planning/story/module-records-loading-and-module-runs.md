## Context

Slice 11 brings modules (`epic:slice-11-modules`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 11; governing decisions 9, 10, 11, 3 and 13). This story makes module records and their loading: `Agent::parse_module`, `load_requested_modules` and `finish_loading_imported_module` of `docs/design/host-interface.md` section 9 ("Modules, and the load hand-over", line 885 on the base), with the `LoadRequest` token, and the runner's module runs up to the end of loading. On the base (`f962c324`) the host interface declares the types and no entry point: `pub struct Module(Root)` with `#[expect(dead_code, reason = "modules: slice 11")]` (`crates/swell/src/value.rs` line 102), `ModuleRef` (line 118), `Referrer`, `ImportAttribute` and `LoadRequest` (`crates/swell/src/host/hooks.rs` lines 53 to 114; line 70: "the referrer and of the payload come with slice 11"), `SUPPORTED_IMPORT_ATTRIBUTES` defaulting to `&[]` (line 176), `HostError::ModuleStatus`, `ModuleTerminated`, `EvaluationInProgress` and `ModuleStatus` (`crates/swell/src/host/outcome.rs` lines 63 to 84). The heap has no arena of modules (`crates/swell/src/heap/mod.rs` lines 306 to 313: strings, symbols, private names, objects, realms, environments, scripts). The ScriptOrModule component of a frame (`crates/swell/src/interp/frame.rs` line 175, `script: Handle`, traced as `Item::Script` at line 284), of a function object (`crates/swell/src/object/ecmascript_function.rs` line 66, traced at line 123), of the slot names of an environment (`crates/swell/src/env/mod.rs` line 94, `SlotNames.script`, traced at line 305, copied at line 566) and of a queued job (`crates/swell/src/host/jobs.rs` line 92, set at line 247) is a script's handle only. The runner fails every module run at once (`crates/test262/src/run.rs` lines 238 to 241, "a module run is not made before slice 11"), and its host keeps no load request (`crates/test262/src/host.rs` line 101).

The count. 193 cases (193 runs) are this story's (`docs/plan/slice-11/story-module-records-loading-and-module-runs.cases.txt`): every one has the flag `module` and a parse `SyntaxError` as its `negative` (180 under `language/module-code/` and `language/import/`, `export/`; 13 that use `import()` or `import.meta` in a module), and every one fails on the base on "a module run is not made before slice 11". They pass once the runner parses the case as a module and judges the parse; the grammar and early errors are `story:module-syntax-and-early-errors`'s and `story:import-call-and-import-meta-syntax`'s. Loading is shown by the tests of the host interface (13 requirements) and by the runner's own fixture tree; the cases that load and run a module graph need linking and are `story:module-linking-and-evaluation`'s.

Design it follows: `host-interface.md` section 9 (the `LoadRequest` token, neither `Clone` nor `Copy`; `parse_module`, `load_requested_modules`, `finish_loading_imported_module`, `module_host_defined`, `requested_modules`; `FinishError` and `FinishErrorKind`; parse, finish and the reads also on `HookCx` and `Cx` by decision 5's rule; "A dropped `LoadRequest` releases its roots at the next safe point"), decisions 6 (misuse), 10 (a termination of loading, HOST-76; a broken invariant, HOST-157) and 13 (`module_status`, the reads); ECMA-262 16.2.1.6.1.1 (`#sec-LoadRequestedModules`, `#sec-InnerModuleLoading`, `#sec-ContinueModuleLoading`, `#sec-FinishLoadingImportedModule`, the GraphLoadingState Record); `engine.md` decision 11, the bullets "Modules" (line 3561) and "`negative`"; and the paragraphs `**Modules.**` and `**Module records.**` of `engine.md` and the bullet "Modules" of section 11 as `story:modules-design-text` writes them.
