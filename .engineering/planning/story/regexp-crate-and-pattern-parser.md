## Context

Slice 5 (`epic:slice-05-regexp`) is delivered next, by what the first consumer needs (`docs/plan/waves.md`, "The order after slice 6"): the testharness.js tests of the HTML parser of surf parse with no refusal of slice 6, and of slice 5 there remain 12 regular expression literals, which parse and whose evaluation is refused (`crates/syntax/src/error.rs` line 26, `REGEXP_REFUSAL`). `docs/plan/slice-05/consumer-regexps.tsv` lists them by file and line (10 in `resources/testharness.js`, 1 in `html/syntax/parsing/resources/template.js`, 1 in `html/syntax/parsing/resources/test.js`) with the constructs of each and the call made on it. None uses a flag but `g`; two (`testharness.js` line 581, `template.js` line 68) write `{` and `}` as pattern characters, which only the grammar of Annex B (B.1.2) admits. The first wave of the slice makes the 12 evaluate and those calls answer, in six stories: this one and `story:string-replace-replace-all-and-split` side by side, then `story:regexp-literal-early-errors` and `story:regexp-compiler-and-backtracking-matcher` side by side, then `story:regexp-objects-exec-and-literals`, then `story:regexp-symbol-methods-and-string-match-search`. This story lifts none of the 12 by itself; it gives the next three the parser.

The crate `swell-regexp` does not exist (`docs/design/engine.md`, section 2: "`swell-regexp` and `swell-cli` are not created yet"). This story creates it with the pattern parser, its early errors, and the interface through which the crate charges its work: the crate cannot name `swell_syntax::Ledger` (`crates/syntax/src/ledger.rs`), since `swell-syntax` is to depend on it (decision 2). No test262 case runs on this crate alone; the cases of the grammar are on the list of `story:regexp-literal-early-errors`.

Design it builds on: decision 7 (the grammar of 22.2.1 and of B.1.2 in this slice), decision 3, "Depth bounds" (the nesting of a pattern counts against `parser_nesting` from zero at its start, and passing it is the `SyntaxError` naming `parser_nesting`, HOST-142 and HOST-143), and two changes a design author writes into decisions 7 and 3 before this story starts, named in the report of the decomposition: D1, the charging trait of `swell-regexp` (charge and credit bytes, charge steps, poll the interrupt; one implementation over the meter of `swell-syntax` for a literal, one over the accounting of the agent for `RegExp`), and D4, the parser and the compiler of a pattern hold an explicit stack and use no Rust recursion (decision 3 says so of the matcher only).

## Domain relations

- None. The epic adds no entity of the host model (`spec/domains/host.yaml`: the agent, the realm, the script, the module and their identifiers); a pattern, its tree and its program are values inside the engine, which ESS does not restate (`AGENTS.md`, "Rules").

- `src/budget.rs`: the trait the crate charges through (D1): charge bytes before the growth they pay for, or refuse and charge nothing; credit bytes charged before; charge steps (for the matcher of `story:regexp-compiler-and-backtracking-matcher`); poll the interrupt. A refusal is a stop of the crate: over the memory budget, over the step budget, or interrupted. Beside it a type that charges nothing and never stops, for tests and tools, as `swell_syntax::Unlimited`. Growth is by capacity, as `Meter::grow` does (`crates/syntax/src/ledger.rs`, "By capacity"): a vector doubles to at least 4 elements with `try_reserve_exact`, and a refusal of the allocator is a refused charge.
- `src/flags.rs`: the flags of a pattern from their code units (`#sec-regexpinitialize`, the list `d g i m s u v y`): an error for an unknown or repeated flag and for `u` with `v` (`#sec-parsepattern`, step 1).
- `src/ast.rs`, `src/parser.rs`, `src/early.rs`, `src/error.rs`: ParsePattern (`#sec-parsepattern`) of a pattern given as UTF-16 code units with its flags and a nesting limit, answering the tree or one error with its offset. The grammar of 22.2.1 (`#sec-patterns`) with its parameters [UnicodeMode], [UnicodeSetsMode] and [NamedCaptureGroups]: disjunctions and alternatives; the assertions `^`, `$`, `\b`, `\B`, lookahead and lookbehind, positive and negative; the quantifiers `*`, `+`, `?`, `{n}`, `{n,}`, `{n,m}`, greedy or lazy; the atoms (pattern characters, `.`, escapes, classes, groups capturing, named and non-capturing, modifiers `(?ims-ims:...)`); the escapes (decimal escapes as backreferences, `\k<name>`, `\d \D \s \S \w \W`, control escapes, `\cX`, `\0`, `\xHH`, `\uHHHH`, `\u{...}` and the surrogate pair of two `\u` escapes under `u`, identity escapes); class ranges and class escapes. With neither `u` nor `v` the grammar is that of B.1.2 (`#sec-regular-expressions-patterns`): ExtendedAtom with `{` and `}` as pattern characters and InvalidBracedQuantifier, ExtendedPatternCharacter, the quantifiable lookahead, legacy octal escapes, ClassControlLetter, the IdentityEscape of B.1.2 (`\k` an identity escape in a pattern with no group name), and class ranges with a class escape at an end (B.1.2.1). A pattern with a GroupName is parsed again with [+NamedCaptureGroups] (ParsePattern, step 3). The early errors of `#sec-patterns-static-semantics-early-errors`: a quantifier range whose minimum passes its maximum, a backreference past the count of groups, `\k` naming no group, a group name declared twice except in different alternatives (`regexp-duplicate-named-groups`), a class range whose ends are out of order or a class escape under `u`, a modifier repeated, in both lists, or the empty `(?-:`; and those of `#sec-patterns-static-semantics-early-errors-annexb`. The tree counts the capturing groups and keeps the group names; the pattern text itself is kept by the caller (for `source`, `story:regexp-objects-exec-and-literals`).
- Refused in this story, each a refusal of slice 5 counted by the engine (the message `<construct> is not supported before slice 5`, which `swell_syntax::message_kind` reads as a refusal, `crates/syntax/src/error.rs` line 46), and lifted by the story named, each with a test here that it is refused: the `v` flag (`class set expressions of the v flag`, the story of the `v` flag in a later wave of slice 5); a property escape `\p{...}` or `\P{...}` under `u` (`property escape`, the story of the property escapes); a group name with a code point outside ASCII, written or escaped (`group name outside ASCII`, the story of the identifier tables, which brings `ID_Start` and `ID_Continue`).
- Bounds. The nesting of groups, lookarounds and modifiers is counted from zero at the start of the pattern against the limit the caller passes (the host value `parser_nesting`); passing it is the error `nesting deeper than parser_nesting (<limit>)`, the text `swell_syntax::message_kind` reads as the nesting limit (`crates/syntax/src/error.rs` line 29; the crate writes the text itself, since it cannot name that constant). The parser keeps the open groups on an explicit stack charged through the trait and uses no Rust recursion (D4). Every node and list of the tree is charged before it grows and credited when the tree is dropped; the parser polls the interrupt at least once per 4,096 code units it reads (`swell_syntax::POLL_INTERVAL`), and on a stop it credits what it holds and answers the stop.
- Tests, in `crates/regexp/tests/` (new; `main.rs` generated): `parse.rs`: `every_production_parses_without_u`, `every_production_parses_with_u`, `the_annex_b_grammar_holds_without_u_or_v` (`{` and `}` alone, `a{`, `\c` in a class, legacy octal escapes, a quantified lookahead, `[\d-a]`), `the_annex_b_forms_are_errors_with_u`, `a_pattern_with_a_group_name_is_parsed_again_with_named_capture_groups` (`\k` an identity escape without one, a reference with one), `a_surrogate_pair_is_one_code_point_with_u`; `early_errors.rs`: one test per early error of the two clauses, each with the pattern that hits it and one beside it that does not, `duplicate_group_names_only_in_different_alternatives`, `modifiers_early_errors`, `flags_are_d_g_i_m_s_u_v_y_each_once_and_not_u_with_v`, `the_v_flag_a_property_escape_and_a_group_name_outside_ascii_are_refusals_of_slice_5`; `limits.rs`: `a_pattern_past_the_nesting_limit_is_the_nesting_error` (at a limit of 1,000: 2,000 nested groups fail with the message, 1,000 parse), `a_pattern_at_the_nesting_limit_parses_on_a_small_stack` (10,000 levels at a limit of 10,000, on a thread of 64 KiB), `every_node_is_charged_before_it_grows_and_credited_when_dropped` (a recording budget), `a_refused_charge_stops_the_parse_and_credits_what_it_holds`, `an_interrupt_stops_the_parse_within_4096_code_units`.
