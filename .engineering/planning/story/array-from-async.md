## Context

Slice 10 brings jobs, `Promise`, `async` functions and generators and `for await` (`epic:slice-10-promises-and-async`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 10; `feature Array.fromAsync 10` and `dir test/built-ins/Array/fromAsync/ 10` of the `swell-slices` block). This story brings `Array.fromAsync` (`#sec-array.fromasync`): an `async` builtin whose steps await the iterator results, the values of a sync iterable, the results of the mapper and the elements of an array-like, and settle a promise. It depends on `story:for-await-and-async-from-sync-iterator` (GetIterator with kind async, CreateAsyncFromSyncIterator, AsyncIteratorClose) and on `story:async-generators-and-their-request-queue` (5 cases of its list pass async generators as input); it reaches `story:async-functions-and-await` through them (69 cases use `async` functions and `asyncHelpers.js`). It needs no open story of slice 7: the 2 cases of `Array.fromAsync` that use `yield*` in an async generator are not on its list but in `docs/plan/slice-10/close-cross.cases.txt`, which the conformance close checks after `story:yield-star-in-async-generators`.

No refusal text names it: on the base `Array.fromAsync` is undefined, so a call of it is a `TypeError` of a call of undefined, and the runner counts its cases in slice 10 by `feature Array.fromAsync`. The search for text that says it waits, `grep -rn 'fromAsync' crates website/docs`, prints nothing on `397ffff`; after this story it prints the builtin, its registration and its tests.

The list of this story is `docs/plan/slice-10/story-from-async.cases.txt`: the 73 cases of the key `from-async` of `docs/plan/slice-10/later.tsv` that need no `yield*` (the command is in the head of `docs/plan/slice-10/scan-later.awk`), all under `built-ins/Array/fromAsync`; 69 have the flag `async`. They have 144 runs in the base run of the property-escapes join (the newest full run, made before generators and the flag `i` were merged and before the first wave of this slice; 18,935 of 44,812 cases pass): 136 fail on `async function is not supported before slice 10` (the text before the first wave; `asyncHelpers.js` has `async` functions), 4 on a `TypeError` of a call of undefined, 2 on a `Test262Error`. The 18 cases of `Array.fromAsync` that need a later slice (15 of slice 12, 1 each of slices 8, 9 and 13) are the conformance close's (`docs/plan/slice-10/close-later.tsv`). A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit of the list file on the wave branch whose body names that part; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

Design it builds on, by name (the text of the slice, merged at `843f178`): `docs/design/engine.md` decision 3, "`async` functions on suspended frames", items 2 to 5, and decision 4, "A built-in function with slots". One sentence is missing and is put to the design author as a question of this story, to be answered before it starts (the story follows the answer; the default is this): how a builtin awaits. `Array.fromAsync` runs its steps in an abstract closure started by AsyncBlockStart (`#sec-array.fromasync`, step 3), which suspends at each Await; a builtin has no frame of bytecode to suspend. The default: the state of the steps (the phase, the index, the iterator record or the array-like and its length, the array being filled, the mapper and its this, the promise capability) lies in an internal kind of its own, `ObjectKind::FromAsyncActivation`, with a null [[Prototype]] and never handed out, which the two functions of each `await` hold in their slots as they hold an activation (item 3), and each resumption runs the next steps in Rust until the next `await` or the end, one re-entry; its charge, steps, depth and terminations are an activation's (items 2, 4 and 5). Rejected: the steps written as script source compiled with the realm (a builtin with a source text and a frame, which no other builtin has, and which `Function.prototype.toString` would have to hide).

## What later stories reuse

The state of a builtin that awaits, which no other builtin of this slice needs; slice 11 (top-level `await`) and later async builtins may take its form.

## Domain relations

- `Array.fromAsync` call → its activation: one to one; the activation holds the state of the steps from the call until its promise settles, a termination ends it, or the sweep frees it once nothing that will resume it is reachable — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-array.fromasync` and `docs/design/engine.md` decision 3, "`async` functions on suspended frames", item 1; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the slots of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").

- Bounds. Steps: the call is the step of a builtin call; each `await` spends what an `await` spends (item 4: its step, its job, 3 steps at its resumption), and each element the steps of the calls it makes. Depth: each resumption is one re-entry from a job and returns before the next job; a list of any length takes no depth. Memory: the activation, the array and its growth (charged before it grows), the functions and reactions of the pending `await`; all freed once nothing that will resume it is reachable. Termination: as an activation (item 5): a termination while its steps run leaves its promise pending for good; one while it waits keeps its state and a later checkpoint resumes it.
