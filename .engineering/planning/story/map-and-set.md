## Context

Slice 9 brings `Map`, `Set`, `WeakMap`, `WeakSet`, `Proxy` and `Reflect` (`epic:slice-09-collections-proxy-reflect`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 9). ## What later stories reuse

The object kinds `Map` and `Set` (`object/collection.rs`) with their [[MapData]] and [[SetData]]; `%Map%`, `%Set%` and their prototypes; the iterator kind and its two prototypes; the private operations `map_data` and `set_data` (the table of a `this` value, or the `TypeError` of a `this` that is not one), which `story:set-methods` (GetSetRecord, the loops over the table), `story:map-upsert-and-group-by` (`getOrInsert*`, `Map.groupBy`) and `story:weak-map-and-weak-set` (the brand checks that a `WeakMap` is not a `Map`) call; the loop AddEntriesFromIterable with an adder, which `WeakMap` and `WeakSet` call with their own adder.

## Domain relations

- A `Map` or `Set` → its table: one to one, owned, made with the object and freed with it — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-map-constructor`, step 2, and `#sec-set-constructor`, step 2; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the slots of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").
- A Map or Set Iterator → its collection: many to one, each iterator with one cursor registered with the collection's table, released when the iterator is done — inferable (inferred from `#sec-createmapiterator` and `#sec-createsetiterator`; no ess/1 document declares it, for the same reason).
