## Context

Slice 7 brings generators (`epic:slice-07-generators`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 7). This story is the first of the slice: the grammar of generator declarations and expressions and of `yield`, their early errors, and their nodes in the tree, with the refusal of slice 7 moved behind the early-error pass. It was cut from the first draft of `story:generator-functions-and-suspended-frames` on the reading of the design, scope and acceptance critics (round 1): this part reads no instruction and is shown alone, by the tests of `swell-syntax` and the negative parse cases of its list. It is not a cut for parallelism: the two stories run in a row, and this one gives a check halfway. It needs none of the six items of "Suspended frames" (decision 3).

On the base (`4971b9b`) the parser refuses `function*` in `function_head` (`crates/syntax/src/parser/function.rs` line 45) and a generator method in `accessor_kind` (`crates/syntax/src/parser/expression.rs` line 1365), both with `generator function is not supported before slice 7` at the `*`. A grammar error or a refusal stops the parse and is its only error (`crates/syntax/src/lib.rs`, the documentation of `parse_script`, lines 146 to 149), so no early error of a generator is ever reported, and every negative parse case of slice 7 fails. The 4 cases of `language/statements/if` and `language/statements/labeled/decl-gen.js` pass in strict mode and fail in the other on the refusal of slice 18 (`crates/syntax/src/parser/statement.rs` lines 188 to 197): a generator declaration as an `if` clause or a labelled statement is a `SyntaxError` in every mode, since Annex B.3.3 and B.3.4 cover a plain FunctionDeclaration only.

The list of this story is `docs/plan/slice-07/story-generator-syntax.cases.txt`: the 115 cases of the key `generators` of `docs/plan/slice-07/scan.tsv` whose negative phase is `parse` (field 4 of `.cache/inventory/meta.tsv`), made by the command in the head of `docs/plan/slice-07/scan.awk`: `LC_ALL=C awk -F'\t' 'NR == FNR { if ($2 == "generators") w[$1] = 1; next } ($1 in w) && $4 == "parse" { print $1 }' docs/plan/slice-07/scan.tsv .cache/inventory/meta.tsv | LC_ALL=C sort`. By directory: `language/expressions/generators` 40, `language/statements/generators` 31, `language/block-scope/syntax` 13, `language/statements/switch` 9, `language/statements/if` 4, `language/expressions/yield` 2, `language/expressions/assignmenttargettype` 2, `language/expressions/assignment` 2, and 12 directories of one case each. On the coordinator's base run of `4971b9b` they have 218 runs, 213 failing: 208 end on `generator function is not supported before slice 7` and the other 5 are the non-strict runs of the five cases above. They are taken out of `story-generators.cases.txt`, the list of `story:generator-functions-and-suspended-frames`, which keeps the other 978 cases of the key. A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit of the list file on the wave branch whose body names that part; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

The refusal. Its text does not change: `generator function is not supported before slice 7` before and after this story, at the same place (the `*` after `function` of the first generator function of the source), counted by `Agent::refusals` as before (`crates/syntax/src/error.rs`, `message_kind`). What changes is when it comes: after the whole parse and the early-error pass, instead of at the `*`. So a source with a generator and no other error still ends on that refusal; one with an early error ends on its early errors; and one with a grammar error, or a refusal of another construct, anywhere in it ends on that, since the parse now goes on past the generator. The last changes the message on which some failing runs of other slices end (a case of slice 8 with a generator before its `class` now ends on `class is not supported before slice 8`), not whether they fail. The generator method keeps its refusal at parse time, text and place unchanged. The search for what expects the text, `grep -rn 'generator function is not supported\|"generator function"' crates conformance docs/design taskfiles tests website/docs`, prints on the base `crates/syntax/src/parser/function.rs` line 45, `crates/syntax/src/parser/expression.rs` line 1365, line 1 of `crates/syntax/tests/sources/refused/generator-{declaration,expression,method}.js`, and lines 305 to 309 of `conformance/test262/later-parts.tsv` (tokens by which earlier closes moved cases into slice 7; they stay); after this story it prints the same files, the line of `function.rs` where the parser records the refusal instead of line 45.

The two fixtures of the runner, `crates/test262/tests/fixtures/tree/test/language/expressions/yield/{inline,block}-lists.js` (`function* g() { yield 1; }`), have no early error, so after this story they still end on the same refusal at the same place, and no line moves: not `TREE_COUNT` and the lines at 213, 240, 390 and 447 of `crates/test262/tests/runner.rs`, not lines 108, 109, 153 and 154 of `crates/test262/tests/expected.rs`, not line 127 of `crates/test262/tests/sections.rs`, not lines 9, 13, 18 and 27 of `crates/test262/tests/fixtures/expected.txt` and `expected-changed-count.txt`. The story writes none of them, and its acceptance shows that by running the tests of `swell-test262` on an unchanged `crates/test262`.

Design it builds on: decision 3 of `docs/design/engine.md`, "Depth bounds" (the parser's nesting counter) and the stack bound of the parser (`STACK_BYTES_PER_LEVEL`, `crates/syntax/src/lib.rs` line 80, held by `every_nesting_kind_fits_the_stack_bound`); section 12, "A part before its slice" (the form of a refusal). ## What slice 10 reuses

The [Yield] flag of the parser state, saved and restored at each function boundary, never in a recursive frame: the form of [Await]. The early errors of a YieldExpression in formal parameters and in arrow parameters: the form of those of an AwaitExpression. The place of a refusal behind the early-error pass (`analyse_parsed` of `crates/syntax/src/lib.rs`), which a later construct may take until its run time exists.

## Domain relations

- YieldExpression → the generator function whose body holds it: many to one; a YieldExpression stands in the body of a generator, never in its parameters, in an arrow function's parameters or body, or in a plain function inside it — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-generator-function-definitions` and `#sec-generator-function-definitions-static-semantics-early-errors`, and from `crates/syntax/src/ast.rs:667`, the `Function` node that holds its body; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the grammar of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").
- Generator declaration → the scope that binds it: as a function declaration does, a var-scoped hoisted binding at the top level of a function or a script and a lexical one in a block or a `case` block, with no Annex B binding — inferable (inferred from `crates/syntax/src/early.rs:69`, the frames of the lexical names of a block, and ECMA-262 `#sec-block-static-semantics-early-errors`; no ess/1 document declares it, for the same reason).

## Change

Failing tests first: the fixtures and tests below are written before the code and fail on the base (the parser refuses `function*` at the `*`, before any early error).

- `crates/syntax/src/parser/mod.rs`: the [Yield] parameter of the grammar as a flag of the parser state beside `strict`, set for the parameters and the body of a generator, cleared for those of a plain function, a getter, a setter and a method and for the body of an arrow function (ConciseBody is [~Yield]), kept for arrow parameters ([?Yield]), saved and restored at each function boundary in the state, never in a recursive frame. `Parsed` (line 45) gets the refusal held back to the end of the parse: the construct and the span of the first generator function of the source, or none.
- `crates/syntax/src/parser/function.rs`: `function*` in `function_head` (line 41): the refusal at line 45 becomes the record of the held refusal (`"generator function"`, slice 7, the span of the `*`), the first one in the source kept; the function gets `generator: true`. The name of a GeneratorDeclaration is read with the [Yield] of its context, that of a GeneratorExpression with [+Yield]; the parameters and the body with [+Yield] (`#sec-generator-function-definitions`). `function_rest` (line 77) takes the [Yield] of the parameters and the body from the `generator` field of the function it is given, so that the generator method of a later story needs no change in this file.
- `crates/syntax/src/parser/expression.rs`: YieldExpression at the level of AssignmentExpression where [Yield] is set (`YieldExpression[In, Await]`): `yield` alone when a line terminator follows it or the next token cannot begin an AssignmentExpression, `yield` with its operand, and `yield *` with its operand (no line terminator before the `*`); where [Yield] is not set `yield` stays an IdentifierReference as on the base.
