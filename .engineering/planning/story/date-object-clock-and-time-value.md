## Context

Slice 4 of 19, `numbers-and-dates` (`epic:slice-04-numbers-and-dates`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 4; decision 6, "numbers and dates, whole"; decision 9, the clock and the time zone come from the host). This story is the clock half of `Date`: the object with its time value, `Date.now`, `new Date()` and `new Date(value)`, the host clock hook charged, and the two references the first consumer makes to `Date`, both `Date.now()` (the two reads of the busy wait). It has no prerequisite of the design and no edge to wait for: its consumer test reads `Date` only (the seven `Math` calls of the consumer are tested by `story:math-constants-and-exact-functions`, and the test of all nine references is the one of `story:slice-04-conformance-close`). It needs no calendar story: it needs `TimeClip` only, which it writes in `swell-num` itself (`TimeClip` has no owner in `story:swell-num-calendar-and-local-time`, which says so). The zone half (the constructor with components, `Date.UTC`, the getters in local time and UTC) is `story:date-object-constructor-now-utc-and-getters`; it depends on this story and on the calendar story.

The browser surf reads `Date` twice in the scripts its first tests load, both in the busy wait of `fake_set_timeout` in `resources/testharness.js`: `var start = Date.now();` and `if ((end - Date.now()) > 0)`. The script reads the clock and nothing else (no `new Date`, no field, no zone): its result depends on the host clock and not on the time zone; with a clock that does not advance the loop ends only at the step budget. With the seven `Math` calls of `story:math-constants-and-exact-functions` these are the nine references; this story holds the test of the two `Date` reads and the close the test of all nine.

On the base (`c6d44d1`) there is no `Date` (1,354 runs of the base part of this story end on `ReferenceError: Date is not defined`). The hooks are written and never called by the engine: `host_system_utc_epoch_nanoseconds` (required), `system_time_zone_identifier`, `get_named_time_zone_offset_nanoseconds`, `get_named_time_zone_epoch_nanoseconds`, `available_named_time_zone_identifiers` (`crates/swell/src/host/hooks.rs`; the engine calls a hook as `H::host_get_code_for_eval(&mut HookCx { agent: self }, ...)` does in `crates/swell/src/dynamic_code.rs`).
