## Change

Failing tests first: the tests below are written before the code and fail on the base of this story (`Math.sumPrecise` is not a function; `swell_num::sum` does not exist).

- The accumulator. `crates/num/src/sum.rs` (new), `pub mod sum;` in `lib.rs`: `Sum` holds two `[u64; 34]` (the positive items and the negative items, counting units of 2^-1074), and the state of the draft (not-a-number, plus-infinity, minus-infinity, minus-zero, finite; it starts as minus-zero, as the draft starts). `add(&mut self, x: f64)`: a NaN sets the state to not-a-number; an infinity sets plus-infinity or minus-infinity (both seen: not-a-number); a finite non-zero `x` splits by `to_bits` into the 53-bit mantissa and the exponent field, shifts the mantissa by the exponent into two adjacent limbs of the accumulator of its sign and propagates the carry as a binary counter does; `+0` takes the state out of minus-zero when it is the first non-`-0` item (the unit reads the draft step list and states the exact rule). `finish(&self) -> f64`: not-a-number gives NaN, the infinities give themselves, minus-zero gives `-0.0`, a finite state subtracts the smaller accumulator from the larger (34 limb operations with borrow), takes the sign of the larger, and rounds the magnitude to 53 bits to nearest with ties to even (the bits above, the next bit and a sticky bit of the rest), scales by 2^-1074 and answers ±infinity when it rounds to 2^1,024 or more; an exact zero is `+0.0`. The operations on floats are `to_bits`, `from_bits`, comparisons and, for the scale, construction of the result by `from_bits` of the rounded exponent and mantissa (no multiplication by a power of two that could be inexact in the subnormal range): each fixed by IEEE 754 or by the Rust reference on every platform. `const MAX_ITEMS: u64 = (1 << 53) - 1`. `size_of::<Sum>()` is the 544 bytes of limbs plus the state byte and its padding: the unit reports it against the design's 544.
- The builtin. `crates/swell/src/builtins/math.rs`: the variant `MathBuiltin::SumPrecise` (`length` 1, name `sumPrecise`) and its arm, which follows the draft: `RequireObjectCoercible(items)`, `GetIterator(items, sync)`; the record's [[Iterator]] and [[NextMethod]] are kept (`Agent::keep`, released to the mark on every way out); a loop of `iterator_step_value`, one `charge_steps(1)` per iteration beside the step of the call of `next` (a termination of it ends the builtin with no sum, no `return` call), a value that is not a Number is a `TypeError` with `iterator_close`, the count past `MAX_ITEMS` is a `RangeError` with `iterator_close`, a Number goes to `Sum::add`; at the end, `charge_steps(34)` once, then `Sum::finish`. A throw of `next` or of the reading of `done` or `value` closes nothing, as `ops/iteration.rs` already does. Nothing is allocated on the heap and nothing is charged to the memory ledger. `object/builtin_function.rs` routes `MathBuiltin` already.
- Tests. In `swell-num` (`crates/num/tests/sum.rs`, new, `main.rs` generated): an independent oracle in the test (the partials algorithm of Shewchuk as `math.fsum` states it: the exact running partials by two-sum, then the correctly rounded total, with the overflow case of the oracle left to the hand-written cases below, because the oracle's intermediate sum cannot pass 2^1,024) against `Sum` over 2,000 lists drawn by the Knuth LCG of the design with fixed seeds (lengths 0 to 40; exponents spread over the full range; subnormals; cancelling pairs; the same list in reverse order and in a fixed shuffle gives the same bits); hand-written cases: `[]` is `-0`, `[-0]` is `-0`, `[-0, -0]` is `-0`, `[-0, 0]` is `+0`, `[1, -1]` is `+0`, `[2^53, 1]` is `2^53` (a tie to even), `[2^53, 1, 2^-1074]` is `2^53 + 2` (the sticky bit), `[2^53 + 2, 1]` is `2^53 + 4`, `[MAX, 2^970]` is infinity (a tie, to even, rounds up to 2^1,024), `[MAX, 2^969, 2^968, 2^967]` is `MAX` (the sum is below the tie by 2^967), `[1e308, 1e308, 0.1, 0.1, 0.1, -1e308, -1e308]` is `0.30000000000000004` (the design's example, whose sum passes through 2^1,025), three items `2^-1074` give `3 * 2^-1074`, a NaN anywhere gives NaN even after infinities, `+inf` and `-inf` together give NaN, 100,000 items of `2^-1074` give `100000 * 2^-1074`, and 10,000 items of `MAX` followed by 10,000 of `-MAX` give `+0` (the carries through all 34 limbs; the sum of the first half is above 2^1,037, well under the 2^2,151 the limbs hold). In `swell` (`crates/swell/tests/math_sum_precise.rs`, new; `tests/main.rs` generated), each test in both collection modes: the property checks of the list (`length`, `name`, the descriptor); an Array, a generator and an iterator object with a counting `next` and `return` give the same sum; a non-iterable (`undefined`, `null`, a Number) is a `TypeError`; a String item or a Symbol item is a `TypeError` and `return` was called once; a throwing `next` is not followed by a `return` call; an infinite iterator ends in `Termination::StepBudget` with `return` never called and the agent alive for the next entry; an interrupt raised by a host function from inside `next` ends in `Termination::Interrupted` with no `return` call; 40,000 items cost 40,000 steps plus the 34 and the steps of the calls of `next` (the test states the sum it reads from `Agent::steps`); the count check: `swell_num::sum::MAX_ITEMS` and a helper `check_count(n: u64) -> Result<(), ()>` that the builtin calls, tested at `MAX_ITEMS` (Ok) and `MAX_ITEMS + 1` (the `RangeError` path, through a library test in `builtins/math.rs` that calls the helper, since 2^53 items cannot be run); memory: the reading of the heap bytes before and after a call over 100,000 items is the same (the unit states the readings).
