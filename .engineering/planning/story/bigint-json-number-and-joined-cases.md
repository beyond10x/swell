## Context

Slice 12 brings `BigInt` (`epic:slice-12-bigint`; `docs/design/engine.md` decisions 3, 4 and 6). This story brings the two builtins of earlier slices that still fault on a BigInt, `JSON.stringify` (SerializeJSONProperty, `#sec-serializejsonproperty`: step 2, the `toJSON` of a BigInt read through GetV; step 4, a BigInt object gives its [[BigIntData]]; step 10, a BigInt is a `TypeError`) and `Number ( value )` (`#sec-number-constructor-number-value` step 1.b: the Number of a BigInt, rounded to nearest, ties to even), and it owns the joined cases: the cases that need two or more of the three stories of order 3 at once (a wrapper object made by `Object(2n)` or an object whose `valueOf` answers a BigInt, given to an operator). It is in order 4, the only story of its order; it depends on `story:bigint-comparison-and-loose-equality`, `story:bigint-arithmetic-and-bitwise-operators` and `story:bigint-constructor-and-prototype`.

Measured by the decomposition on `f962c324` with the full run of that commit (`test262-slice 12 381 760 0 0 381 39 342 0`): the list of this story, `docs/plan/slice-12/story-joint.cases.txt`, holds the 43 cases of the key `joint` of `docs/plan/slice-12/scan.tsv`, all failing, 86 runs: 84 on "BigInt literal is not supported before slice 12" and the 2 of `built-ins/JSON/stringify/value-bigint-cross-realm.js` on "the value is not a function" (the other realm has no `BigInt`). By directory: `language/expressions` 33 (`bitwise-and`, `bitwise-or`, `bitwise-xor`, `left-shift`, `right-shift` and `unsigned-right-shift` 3 each, `addition`, `subtraction`, `multiplication`, `division`, `modulus` and `exponentiation` 2 each, `bitwise-not`, `equals` and `does-not-equals` 1 each: the `-wrapped-values`, `-and-number`, `-non-primitive` and `bigint-and-object` cases), `built-ins/JSON/stringify` 6, and one each of `built-ins/JSON/rawJSON/bigint-raw-json-can-be-stringified.js`, `built-ins/Number/bigint-conversion.js`, `built-ins/BigInt/wrapper-object-ordinary-toprimitive.js` and `built-ins/BigInt/prototype/toString/a-z.js`. The 35 cases that are not `JSON` or `Number` (the 33 of `language/expressions` and the two of `built-ins/BigInt`) need no code of this story: they pass when the three stories of order 3 are joined, and this story shows that with its part. A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit on the wave branch whose body names that part and adds it, in the same commit, to `docs/plan/slice-12/close-later.tsv` (path, the story that takes it, one reason), which `sh docs/plan/slice-12/lists.sh` counts as a list; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

The epic's Out of scope line "`Number` and `Math`: slice 4" does not hold for one arm: `built-ins/Number/bigint-conversion.js` is a case of this slice, and its step 1.b of `Number ( value )` is written here; `story:slice-12-conformance-close` lists the line for the coordinator to reword ("`Number` and `Math`: slice 4, except the BigInt arm of `Number()`").

Line numbers in this body are those of `f962c324`; every anchor is found by its name (a function, a step comment, a test).

This story takes over the entry of `docs/plan/waves.md`, "What later slices take over", "The slice that completes `JSON`: the stack one re-entry takes ...": its arms are the last BigInt steps of `JSON`, and they sit in `json_serialize_property`, on the `toJSON` re-entry path, where a unit "has 1,560 bytes per re-entry before a stack test fails; it then measures again and the constant follows, or the frame is taken apart".
