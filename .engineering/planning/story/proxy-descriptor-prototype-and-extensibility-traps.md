## Context

Slice 9 brings `Map`, `Set`, `WeakMap`, `WeakSet`, `Proxy` and `Reflect` (`epic:slice-09-collections-proxy-reflect`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 9). `story:proxy-objects-and-access-traps` brings the Proxy object, its six access traps and a Proxy arm in every internal method; it leaves seven arms forwarding to the target without reading the handler. This story gives those seven their traps and the invariants their clauses check (`#sec-invariants-of-the-essential-internal-methods`): [[GetPrototypeOf]] (`getPrototypeOf`), [[SetPrototypeOf]] (`setPrototypeOf`), [[IsExtensible]] (`isExtensible`), [[PreventExtensions]] (`preventExtensions`), [[GetOwnProperty]] (`getOwnPropertyDescriptor`), [[DefineOwnProperty]] (`defineProperty`) and [[OwnPropertyKeys]] (`ownKeys`). It is in order 3: it depends on `story:proxy-objects-and-access-traps` (the kind, the dispatch arms and `object/invariants.rs`) and on `story:exponentiation-operator` of slice 4 (2 cases of its list use `**`, refused on the base: `Array/prototype/splice/create-species-length-exceeding-integer-limit.js` and the incoming case below).

Measured by the decomposition on `62c7cf59` with the full run on the code of wave big-1 before the close of slice 4 (2026-11-26; its line `test262-slice 09 1785 3541 0 0 1785 267 1518 0` equals `conformance/test262/expected.txt` at `62c7cf59`): the list of this story, `docs/plan/slice-09/story-proxy-traps-of-shape.cases.txt`, holds the 204 cases of the key `proxy-traps-of-shape` of `docs/plan/slice-09/scan.tsv` (cases that read `Proxy` and define one of the seven traps, or sit in its directory; the command is in the head of `docs/plan/slice-09/scan.awk`), all failing (402 runs: 364 on `Proxy is not defined`, 30 on `the value is not a constructor`, 4 on the refusal of `**`, the rest on a `Test262Error`): `Proxy/ownKeys` 27, `Proxy/defineProperty` 24, `Proxy/getOwnPropertyDescriptor` 21, `Proxy/getPrototypeOf` 19, `Proxy/setPrototypeOf` 17, `Proxy/isExtensible` 12, `Proxy/preventExtensions` 11, `language/statements/with` 6, `language/expressions/object` 6, `Proxy/set` 4, `Object/seal` 4, `Object/keys` 4 and the rest in directories of fewer cases (`Object/freeze`, `Object/assign`, `Object/entries`, `JSON/stringify`, `Array/prototype`, `for-in`, `Reflect` with a Proxy). ## Change

Failing tests first: the tests below are written before the code and fail on the base of this story (the seven traps are not read: a handler's `ownKeys` is never called).

- `crates/swell/src/object/proxy.rs`: the seven methods (`#sec-proxy-object-internal-methods-and-internal-slots-getprototypeof` and the six after it): ValidateNonRevokedProxy, GetMethod of the trap, without a trap the target's own method (as today), with one the Call of the trap and the checks of its clause. [[GetPrototypeOf]]: the result an Object or `null`, else a `TypeError`; for a non-extensible target SameValue with the target's prototype. [[SetPrototypeOf]]: ToBoolean of the result; true for a non-extensible target whose prototype differs a `TypeError`. [[IsExtensible]]: the result equal to the target's. [[PreventExtensions]]: true while the target is extensible a `TypeError`. [[GetOwnProperty]]: the result an Object or `undefined`; ToPropertyDescriptor and CompletePropertyDescriptor; `undefined` for a non-configurable target property or for any property of a non-extensible target a `TypeError`; IsCompatiblePropertyDescriptor; non-configurable for a configurable or absent target property a `TypeError`; non-configurable and non-writable for a writable target property a `TypeError`. [[DefineOwnProperty]]: FromPropertyDescriptor of the argument to the trap; ToBoolean; the checks of settingConfigFalse and of a non-writable data property. [[OwnPropertyKeys]]: CreateListFromArrayLike of the result with the element types String and Symbol (`crates/swell/src/builtins/function.rs` line 200, made `pub(crate)` by `story:reflect-namespace`: a parameter of element types, or a wrapper beside it); a repeated key a `TypeError` (a `KeyedMap` of the keys seen, `crates/swell/src/heap/map.rs`, charged by `KeyedMap::table_bytes_for` before it is made, dropped at the end of the call); every non-configurable key of the target present; for a non-extensible target exactly the target's keys. `crates/swell/src/object/invariants.rs`: IsCompatiblePropertyDescriptor (`validate_and_apply_property_descriptor` with `object` `None`), CompletePropertyDescriptor, and the checks above, each a `TypeError` with a message that names the trap and the key.
