## Context

Slice 4 of 19, `numbers-and-dates` (`epic:slice-04-numbers-and-dates`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 4; decision 6, the `Date` bullets). This story gives `Date.prototype` its 14 setters over the two conversions of the zone story. It depends on `story:date-object-constructor-now-utc-and-getters` (the zone story: `local_time_of`, `utc_of_local`, the getters' table, the test host fields) and on `story:date-object-clock-and-time-value` (the object, the slot, `time_clip`, the interim `date_not_built` this story replaces). The first consumer needs none of it.

On the base (`c6d44d1`; the unit reads every place named in this story again on its base) there is no `Date`; after the clock story the 14 setters are properties whose body is the interim `date_not_built` (a `TypeError`): `setMilliseconds` and `setUTCMilliseconds` (`length` 1), `setSeconds` and `setUTCSeconds` (2), `setMinutes` and `setUTCMinutes` (3), `setHours` and `setUTCHours` (4), `setDate` and `setUTCDate` (1), `setMonth` and `setUTCMonth` (2), `setFullYear` and `setUTCFullYear` (3). `setTime` is the clock story's.

The list is `docs/plan/slice-04/story-date-setters.cases.txt`: the 178 cases of the key `date-setters` of `docs/plan/slice-04/scan.tsv`, all under `built-ins/Date/prototype`: `setHours` 23, `setFullYear` 20, `setMinutes` 18, `setMonth` 17, `setSeconds` 17, `setMilliseconds` 14, `setDate` 12, `setUTCHours` 11, `setUTCMonth` 9, `setUTCSeconds` 9, `setUTCMilliseconds` 8, `setUTCMinutes` 8, `setUTCFullYear` 6, `setUTCDate` 6. 110 of them read only the clock story's `Date` and 68 also a getter (the zone story): the two edges. No case of the list passes on the plan's base run (the guard file is empty: `built-ins/Date` has no passing case there; the base run the coordinator makes on the join the unit starts from has the cases that the clock and zone stories lift, and `check.sh` reads that run, so its `regressed` lines cover them). The cases that read the text of a date are the key `date-text` (165): not on this list.

Design it follows, by name: `docs/design/host-interface.md` decision 17, items 1, 4 and 5 (the identifier is read by each conversion, so `setHours` reads it twice; one built-in call makes at most 61 hook calls: `LocalTime` 2, then `UTC` 59; a NaN time value makes none; HOST-211), `docs/design/engine.md` decision 6 ("`MakeDay` far outside the time range": the setters can never reach a year above 2^53, `TimeWithinDay` being below 86,400,000; "`UTC ( t )` and the local time that does not exist"); ECMA-262 21.4.4.18 to 21.4.4.34 (the setters; `#sec-date.prototype.sethours` and the others), `#sec-localtime`, `#sec-utc-t`, `#sec-timeclip`.

## What later stories reuse

Nothing in code: the setters are leaves. The table of setters (name, length, field written, local or UTC) is what `story:date-text-formats` does not read.

## Domain relations

- Date object -> its time value: the setters are the only writers of the slot besides `setTime` (inferable from ECMA-262 `#sec-properties-of-date-instances` and the clock story). Agent -> host time zone: the local setters use it through the conversions of the zone story (the entity `swell.host.Agent` in `spec/domains/host.yaml`; HOST-209 to HOST-211 state the behaviour).
## Change

Failing tests first: the tests below are written before the code and fail on the clock story's result (the setters throw their interim `TypeError`).

- The setters, `crates/swell/src/builtins/date.rs` (the arms of `DateBuiltin` for the 14 names replace `date_not_built`; no other file of `crates/swell/src` is written). One function over a table row (the arguments it takes (at most 4), the first field it writes, local or UTC), in the order the clauses give: `t = ThisTimeValue(this)` first, read before any `ToNumber` (a `valueOf` that calls `setTime` on the same Date does not change the `t` used: the cases `date-value-read-before-tonumber-*`), then `ToNumber` of each argument present in order, all of them even when `t` is NaN, then: for `setFullYear` and `setUTCFullYear` a NaN `t` becomes +0 (and a local `setFullYear` then makes no `LocalTime` call) and every other setter answers NaN at once when `t` is NaN, with no store and no hook call; for the local ones `t = local_time_of(t)` (2 hook calls); the new date from `MakeTime` and `MakeDate` over `Day(t)` (`MakeDay` over the new year, month and date for the three date setters, with the unchanged fields read from `date_fields(t)`); the local ones `utc_of_local(newDate)` (at most 59 calls); `time_clip`; the store into the slot; the return of the stored value. An argument that is not present keeps its field (`setHours(h)` leaves minutes, seconds and ms). A `date_fields` that answers `None` (not reached for a valid time value, whose local time stays within `LOCAL_TIME_LIMIT`) gives NaN stored. A local setter makes at most 61 hook calls and one that finds no instant stores NaN and throws nothing (HOST-210 holds for it as for the constructor). Each call of a hook is the zone story's closure and charges one step first. The loops are the search of `swell-num` (charged per probe) and the argument coercions (at most 4).
- Tests, in `crates/swell/tests/date_setters.rs` (new; `tests/main.rs` is generated), each in both collection modes: the table of the 14 setters over a Date of `2016-07-04T05:06:07.008Z` at the three zone offsets of the zone story (0, +3,600 × 10^9, -(5 × 3,600 + 1,800) × 10^9): each setter with one, two, three and four arguments (as its `length` allows), `NaN`, `Infinity`, a fractional value, and the expected time value written out (not computed by the function); `setFullYear` on a NaN Date gives the year with month 0 and date 1 at 00:00:00.000 local; every other setter on a NaN Date answers NaN and makes no hook call (`calls` empty); the coercion order (`valueOf` that logs for each argument, in order, each once, after `t` was read, even when `t` is NaN); `this` that is not a Date throws a `TypeError` before any coercion; `new-value-time-clip` (a result above 8.64e15 stores NaN); the call counts: `setHours` on an ordinary Date makes 2 + 3 = 5 calls and `setUTCHours` none, and on a host with a 48-hour gap at the new local time a local setter makes at most 61 (the test prints the count and asserts `<= 61`); a host that answers another identifier at its second call (`LocalTime` read "+01:00", `UTC` read "-02:00": the result is computed from the two answers as given, as decision 17 item 1 says); a host whose epoch list is empty for every local time (HOST-210 form): `setHours` stores NaN after the 2 + 31 = 33 calls; the lengths, names, descriptors and `not-a-constructor` of the 14; `interim_bodies_throw_a_type_error_that_names_the_function` of `crates/swell/tests/date_clock.rs`, rewritten here: it asserted that each setter throws a `TypeError` that names it, and asserts afterwards that the 14 setters are no longer in its table of interim bodies and that `Date.prototype.setHours.call(new Date(0), 1)` answers a Number (one body remains in the table, `Date.parse`, which `story:date-text-formats` replaces); a step budget that ends the call inside the search (`steps_per_entry` 10: `Termination::StepBudget`, the Date unchanged: the store comes last) and an interrupt raised from the epoch hook (`Termination::Interrupted`, the Date unchanged). No loop depends on a script value.
- HOST-186 and HOST-187 readings: the 14 setters exist on every realm since the clock story and this story adds no function: the unit runs `host_objects::host_186_a_host_function_charges_every_byte` and `host_objects::host_187_a_realm_of_300_host_functions_and_40_host_objects` in both modes before and after and reports that the two readings are equal. The Symbol sweep needs no entry: a Symbol `this` throws a `TypeError` in `ThisTimeValue` and a Symbol argument in `ToNumber`; the unit runs it in both modes.
- Stale text found by a command that prints nothing after the change: `grep -n "date_not_built" crates/swell/src/builtins/date.rs` prints, after this story, the definition, the one use of `Date.parse`'s interim (`date_not_built("parse")`) and nothing for a setter: `grep -c 'date_not_built("set' crates/swell/src/builtins/date.rs` prints 0 (it prints 14 on the unit's base: one line per setter of the layout the clock story fixes, `DateBuiltin::SetHours => date_not_built("setHours"),`); `website/docs/limits.md` is left to the conformance close.
