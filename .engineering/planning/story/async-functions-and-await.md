## What later stories reuse

The activation, the `await` instruction and its two functions with slots, which the async generators of this slice use for their own `await` and `yield`, and `for await` for its steps; `%AsyncFunction.prototype%`, which `%AsyncFunction%` of a later story makes the `prototype` of its constructor; the instantiation of an `async` template, which async methods of object literals (a later story) take as they are.

## Domain relations

- Activation of an `async` function → suspended frame: one to zero or one; the activation holds the frame in a box in its kind and owns it from the first `await` that suspends until the call completes, a termination drops the frame, or the sweep that frees the activation drops it — inferable (inferred from `crates/swell/src/interp/suspend.rs:52`, `SuspendedFrame`, `docs/design/engine.md` decision 3, "Suspended frames", item 2, and `docs/design/engine.md` decision 3, "`async` functions on suspended frames", item 1; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the slots of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").

- Activation → the promise of the call: one to one; the promise outlives the activation and settles when the body completes or throws, or stays pending after a termination — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-async-functions-abstract-operations-async-function-start` and `docs/design/host-interface.md` section 10; no ess/1 document declares it, for the same reason).

- Awaited promise → the activations waiting on it: one to many, through its reactions, whose handlers are the two functions of each `await`; a function references its activation in a slot and does not own it — inferable (inferred from `#sec-await` and `docs/design/engine.md` decision 3, "`async` functions on suspended frames", items 1 and 3; no ess/1 document declares it).
