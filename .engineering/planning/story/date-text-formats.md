Design it follows, by name: `docs/design/engine.md` decision 6, "`Date.parse` and the String argument of `new Date`" (the three forms and the work: one pass, a String of up to 32 code units costs the step of the call and each code unit past 32 one step, charged before the first is read; a date-time form without an offset adds the steps of its hook calls) and the sentence that `toString` writes no zone name; `docs/design/host-interface.md` decision 17, items 1, 2, 4, 5 (`toString` makes one conversion: 2 hook calls, 1 with an offset-string identifier, and uses its one offset answer for both the local time and the `GMT±hhmm`; `new Date(string)` and `Date.parse` of a form with no offset at most 59), HOST-127 (the second half) and HOST-211; ECMA-262 21.4.2.1, 21.4.3.2, 21.4.4.35 to 21.4.4.45 (the string methods; `#sec-date.prototype.tostring`, `#sec-date.prototype.tojson`, `#sec-date.prototype-%symbol.toprimitive%`), `#sec-todatestring`, `#sec-timezoneestring`.

**Prerequisite of the design author, not in the `Writes` of this story: the text of `toLocaleString`, `toLocaleDateString` and `toLocaleTimeString`.** The draft leaves the output to the implementation (21.4.4.39 to 21.4.4.41; `Intl` is excluded) and the design says only that it is not fixed. The pinned test262 has four cases for each (`length`, `name`, `not-a-constructor`, `prop-desc`), none of the output. The sentence below is written into `docs/design/engine.md` before the unit starts, and the unit follows it as written; the start gate of the acceptance is its first line. The sentence:

> **The text of toLocale*String** (decision 6, beside the `Date.parse` bullet). `Number.prototype.toLocaleString` gives the text of `Number.prototype.toString()` of the same Number, and `Date.prototype.toLocaleString`, `toLocaleDateString` and `toLocaleTimeString` give the text of `toString`, `toDateString` and `toTimeString` of the same Date, each through the same one conversion; there is no locale behaviour (`Intl` is excluded). `Date.parse` reads the text of `toString`, `toUTCString` and `toISOString`, so `Date.parse(d.toLocaleString())` is `d` to the second; it answers NaN for the text of `toDateString`, `toTimeString`, `toLocaleDateString` and `toLocaleTimeString`.
