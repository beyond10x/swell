## Context

Slice 10 brings jobs, `Promise`, `async` functions and generators and `for await` (`epic:slice-10-promises-and-async`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 10). This story is the grammar of async generator declarations and expressions (`#sec-async-generator-function-definitions`: `async function*`, with [+Yield, +Await] in its parameters and body), their early errors and their nodes in the tree, with the refusal held back to the end of the parse, in the form `story:async-function-syntax-and-early-errors` gave `async` functions and `story:generator-syntax-and-early-errors` gave generators. It reads no instruction and runs nothing: it is shown by the tests of `swell-syntax` and by the negative parse cases of its list. It depends on `story:async-functions-and-await`, which leaves the place of the held refusal (`Parser::held`) holding none for `async` functions and writes the same parser files before it, and needs no open story of slice 7: the one case of its list with `yield*` (a `yield*` in a parameter default) is a negative parse case that ends on its early error, and the held refusal of `yield*` of slice 7, if still in the code, comes after the early errors as every held refusal does.

The refusal this story moves: `async generator function is not supported before slice 10`, which `story:async-function-syntax-and-early-errors` refuses at the `*` of `async function*` at once, so no early error of an async generator is reported. It keeps its text and is held back to the end of the parse at the `async` of the first async generator of the source (the place `Parser::held` gives a held refusal, the first one of the source kept), so a source with an async generator and no other error still ends on it, and one with an early error ends on its early errors. The refusal of an `async *` method of an object literal (`async generator method is not supported before slice 10`, `story:async-methods-in-object-literals`) stays at its token; its negative cases are on the list of `story:async-generator-methods-in-object-literals`. The search for what expects the text, `grep -rn 'async generator function is not supported\|"async generator function"\|async generator function` (slice 10)' crates conformance docs/design taskfiles tests website/docs`, prints on its base the two places where `story:async-function-syntax-and-early-errors` refuses at the `*` (`crates/syntax/src/parser/{function,expression}.rs`) and line 1 of `crates/syntax/tests/sources/refused/async-generator.js`; after this story it prints the places where the parser records the held refusal, the documentation of `held` (`crates/syntax/src/parser/mod.rs`) and of `parse_script` (`crates/syntax/src/lib.rs`), which name it in the words `async generator function` (slice 10), and the fixture; `story:async-generators-and-their-request-queue` removes them all.

The list of this story is `docs/plan/slice-10/story-ag-syntax.cases.txt`: the 154 cases of the key `ag-syntax` of `docs/plan/slice-10/later.tsv` (negative parse cases with an async generator and no `for await` and no async generator method in their text; the command is in the head of `docs/plan/slice-10/scan-later.awk`). By directory: `language/expressions/async-generator` 81, `language/statements/async-generator` 32, `language/block-scope/syntax` 17, `language/statements/switch` 13, `language/statements/if` 4, `language/statements/with` 1. They have 297 runs in the base run of the property-escapes join (the newest full run, made before generators and the flag `i` were merged and before the first wave of this slice; 18,935 of 44,812 cases pass), every one failing on `async function is not supported before slice 10` (the text before the first wave). The negative parse cases with an async generator and `for await` (20, on the list of `story:async-generators-and-their-request-queue`, which comes after `story:for-await-and-async-from-sync-iterator`), those with a `yield*` in an `async function*` (36, on the list of `story:yield-star-in-async-generators`) and those with an `async *` method (38, on the list of `story:async-generator-methods-in-object-literals`) are not on this list: their early errors are reached only once `for await`, `yield*` and the method parse. A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit of the list file on the wave branch whose body names that part; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

Design it builds on: decision 3 of `docs/design/engine.md`, "Depth bounds" (the parser's nesting counter) and the stack bound of the parser (`STACK_BYTES_PER_LEVEL`, `crates/syntax/src/lib.rs` line 80); section 12, "A part before its slice" (the form of a refusal). No sentence is missing from the design.

## What later stories reuse

The parse of an async generator ([+Yield] and [+Await] together, saved and restored at each function boundary in the state), `generator` and `asynchronous` both set on `Function`, and the held refusal, which `story:async-generators-and-their-request-queue` lifts.

## Domain relations

- YieldExpression and AwaitExpression → the async generator whose body holds them: many to one; both stand in its body, never in its parameters — inferable (inferred from ECMA-262 `#sec-async-generator-function-definitions-static-semantics-early-errors` and `crates/syntax/src/ast.rs:675`, the `Function` node; no ess/1 document declares it: the host model `spec/domains/host.yaml` holds no engine value, and ESS does not restate the grammar of ECMA-262, `AGENTS.md`, "Rules").
