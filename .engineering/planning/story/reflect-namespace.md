## Context

Slice 9 brings `Map`, `Set`, `WeakMap`, `WeakSet`, `Proxy` and `Reflect` (`epic:slice-09-collections-proxy-reflect`; `docs/design/engine.md`, section 12, item 9). This story brings `Reflect` (`#sec-reflect-object`): a plain object, the global `Reflect`, with its 13 functions and `@@toStringTag` `"Reflect"`. Each function is a thin call of one internal method of `crates/swell/src/object/mod.rs` (lines 402 to 690 on `62c7cf59`) with the checks of its clause, so it works on every object kind of the base and, once `story:proxy-objects-and-access-traps` adds the Proxy arms of the dispatch, on a Proxy with no change here. It is in order 1 and depends on no story. Every later story of the slice depends on it: 70 of its failing cases and many cases of the other lists read `Reflect.construct`, `Reflect.ownKeys` or `Reflect.getOwnPropertyDescriptor` through the harness (`isConstructor.js`, `compareArray.js` callers, `proto-from-ctor-realm` cases), so the lists of the later stories assume it (the key order of `docs/plan/slice-09/scan.awk`).

Measured by the decomposition on `62c7cf59` with the full run on the code of wave big-1 before the close of slice 4 (2026-11-26; its line `test262-slice 09 1785 3541 0 0 1785 267 1518 0` equals `conformance/test262/expected.txt` at `62c7cf59`): `Reflect` is not defined. The list of this story, `docs/plan/slice-09/story-reflect.cases.txt`, holds the 480 cases of the key `reflect` of `docs/plan/slice-09/scan.tsv` (the command is in the head of `docs/plan/slice-09/scan.awk`): 213 fail (426 runs), 143 under `built-ins/Reflect` (`set` 18, `setPrototypeOf` 13, `ownKeys` 12, `getOwnPropertyDescriptor` 12, `get` 11, `defineProperty` 11, `deleteProperty` 10, `construct` 10, `has` 9, `getPrototypeOf` 9, `apply` 9, `preventExtensions` 8, the rest 11) and 70 elsewhere (`Reflect.construct` with a `newTarget` in `proto-from-ctor-realm` and `is-a-constructor` cases of `Date`, `Promise`, `Array`, `Function`, `GeneratorFunction`, `AsyncGeneratorFunction`, the six `NativeErrors` and others); and 267 pass (534 runs), 246 of them `not-a-constructor.js` cases that pass today only because `isConstructor.js` answers false when `Reflect` is not defined (its `Reflect.construct` throws a `ReferenceError` inside its `try`). Once `Reflect.construct` exists those 267 test for real: a builtin that is a constructor where its clause says it is not fails them, and that failure belongs to this story (its fix is the `constructor` flag of that builtin's `BuiltinId`, a one-line hunk in the file of that builtin). A case of the list that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part, and the coordinator takes it off the list by a commit on the wave branch whose body names that part and adds it, in the same commit, to `docs/plan/slice-09/close-later.tsv` (path, the slice or story that takes it, one reason), which `sh docs/plan/slice-09/lists.sh` counts as a list, so the partition still holds, and which check (7) of `story:slice-09-conformance-close` reads; the acceptance reads the list as committed there when the wave closes.

Design it builds on, by name: `docs/design/engine.md` decision 3 ("The step budget"; "Depth bounds": a builtin that calls script re-enters, counted against `reentry_depth`) and decision 4 ("Memory budget"); the builtin group convention of `crates/swell/src/builtins/mod.rs` (lines 1 to 44). No sentence of the design is missing for `Reflect`.

## What later stories reuse

`Reflect` itself, through the harness of test262 and through the tests of every later story of the slice; `CreateListFromArrayLike` made `pub(crate)` (`crates/swell/src/builtins/function.rs` line 200 on `62c7cf59`), which `story:proxy-objects-and-access-traps` (the `apply` and `construct` traps' argument arrays) and `story:proxy-descriptor-prototype-and-extensibility-traps` (the result of `ownKeys`, with the element types of `#sec-createlistfromarraylike`) call.
