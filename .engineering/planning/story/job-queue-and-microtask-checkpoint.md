## Context

## What later stories reuse

The queue and the loop of the checkpoint: one outermost entry, one step per entry dequeued, the order of one FIFO for every kind of entry, the flag of a running checkpoint, what a termination leaves, the counts of `CheckpointReport` and `pending_microtasks`, and `Agent::clear_microtasks`. `story:promise-constructor-then-and-jobs` adds the entry of a promise job (its record, its root, its charge, the hooks around it) to the same queue, loop and `clear_microtasks`, with the tests of HOST-198 to HOST-200, and cases with `then` to the runner's fixture tree of this story; `story:async-functions-and-await` resumes an activation from a promise job of that loop.

## Domain relations

- Agent → microtask queue entries: one to many, owned; an entry lives from its enqueue until the checkpoint that dequeues it or `Agent::clear_microtasks` drops it, across entries, and is dropped with the agent — declared for jobs (`spec/domains/host.yaml:165`, the relation `jobs` of the agent, `owns`, many, and the lifecycle `Enqueued` → `Ran` of `swell.host.Job` at `:366`); inferable for host microtasks, which the model does not hold (inferred from `docs/design/host-interface.md:954`, one queue for both kinds, and `crates/swell/src/host/agent.rs:143`, the `Agent`). The model has no state for a job that started and was ended by a termination (section 10: "not run again") or one `Agent::clear_microtasks` dropped (HOST-200); this story does not change `spec/`: `story:ess-target-checkpoint-and-promise-hooks` adds that state with the other declarations the text leaves incomplete.

- Host microtask → realm: many to one; the realm given when it was queued is its current realm while it runs — inferable (inferred from `docs/design/host-interface.md:1048`, "A host microtask's realm", and HOST-192; no ess/1 document declares a host microtask).

- Running checkpoint → its entries: one to many; the checkpoint is one outermost entry and every entry it runs spends from its one step budget — inferable (inferred from `docs/design/host-interface.md:1062`, "The checkpoint is one entry", and `spec/domains/host.yaml:50`, the comment "a microtask checkpoint is one entry", which the model marks UNMAPPED).
