## Context

The nine methods of `Array.prototype` that change their receiver or sort: `pop`, `shift`, `unshift`, `splice`, `reverse`, `fill`, `copyWithin`, `sort` and `toSorted`. The base has `push` of this family and none of the nine (the probe of `story:builtin-group-modules`).

Measured on the base (`309b850`, the run lines of `slot task test262` on it): the nine directories under `test/built-ins/Array/prototype/` hold 242 cases of slice 3, 232 failing (`splice` 55 of 58, `sort` 47 of 47, `copyWithin` 29 of 31, `pop` 19, `toSorted` 19, `shift` 18, `unshift` 16, `fill` 15, `reverse` 14). The static scan of the failing cases of the slice (the decomposition read each case and the harness files it includes for the names of the builtins missing on the base and of the parts of later slices) finds 196 failing cases that name a builtin of this story, no builtin of another story of the wave and no part of a later slice: the list `docs/plan/slice-03/story-array-mutation.cases.txt` (196 lines; by directory line: `built-ins/Array` 188, `built-ins/Object` 6, `language/statements` 1, `language/expressions` 1). A failing case that names builtins of two stories of the wave is on `docs/plan/slice-03/cross-story.tsv` and on no list, and one that needs a later slice is on `docs/plan/slice-03/move-estimate.tsv`; the ten lists, 4,050 cases, were read against the scan again when this plan was revised, and no case of a list names a builtin of another story. That list is this story's bound; a case of it that ends on a part outside this story is reported with that part and the coordinator takes it off the list in the wave record. Of the 47 failing cases of `sort`, 23 are on the list: the others are written with arrow functions, `const` or `Symbol` (among them the stability cases of 5, 11, 513 and 2,048 elements) and move to slice 6 at the close, so the tests of this story hold the stability of the sort.

Code it builds on, checked on the base: `Array.prototype.push` (`array_push_steps` in the module that becomes `builtins/array/mod.rs`) is the model for a Set with `true` per item and the `TypeError` above 2^53 - 1; `index_key`, `length_of_array_like`, `delete_property_or_throw`, `has_property` and CreateDataPropertyOrThrow are `pub(crate)`; the group module `builtins/array/mutation.rs` is `story:builtin-group-modules`'s, with an id type without a variant and an empty list, and `mod.rs` needs no line for a builtin of the group; the elements of an Array are found without a scan after `story:property-index-and-dense-elements`, on which the cost of a sort rests. No test or fixture of the repository relies on one of the nine being absent.

## Change

Failing tests first: the Rust tests and language scripts below are written before the code and fail (`TypeError: the value is not a function`), and the 196 cases of the list fail on the base.

- `crates/swell/src/builtins/array/mutation.rs`: `pop`, `shift`, `unshift` (`sec-array.prototype.pop`, `.shift`, `.unshift`), `splice` (`sec-array.prototype.splice`, with ArraySpeciesCreate), `reverse` (`sec-array.prototype.reverse`), `fill` (`sec-array.prototype.fill`), `copyWithin` (`sec-array.prototype.copywithin`), `sort` and `toSorted` with SortIndexedProperties and CompareArrayElements (`sec-array.prototype.sort`, `sec-array.prototype.tosorted`, `sec-sortindexedproperties`, `sec-comparearrayelements`). Nine builtins, each complete for every this value and argument: an array-like of any length, holes kept or closed as the clause says, a non-writable length, a frozen receiver. They are entries of the group's list in the order of the clauses of 23.1.3, after the keys of the base and of the groups `iteration` and `search`, as `story:builtin-group-modules` fixes the order. The sort is stable and is a merge over a list of the values, charged to the memory budget, with a step per comparison and per element moved; a comparator that answers inconsistently or throws ends the sort in a defined order of what was written or in its throw, never in a loop without end; undefined sorts last and holes after it. Every loop charges a step per index, so `shift` or `reverse` of 2^32 - 1 absent indices ends in `Terminated(StepBudget)`; O, the list and the removed elements are on the kept stack across every Get, Set, getter and comparator call.
- Tests, each in both collection modes (`test-collect`): `crates/swell/tests/builtins_array_mutation.rs` (new): one test per builtin for its property and its steps, among them `pop_and_shift_of_an_empty_array_like_set_length_zero`, `unshift_refuses_a_length_above_2_53`, `splice_of_every_argument_count_and_the_species`, `reverse_keeps_holes_as_holes`, `fill_and_copy_within_clamp_from_both_ends`, `a_receiver_that_refuses_writes_throws_and_keeps_its_elements` (a receiver of three elements whose elements and `length` were defined again by `Object.defineProperty` as neither writable nor configurable: each of the eight methods that write throws a `TypeError` and every element and the length are as before, and `toSorted` answers a new array; `Object.freeze` is not called), the unit test `a_frozen_receiver_throws_and_keeps_its_elements` in `mutation.rs` (the same on an Array frozen by SetIntegrityLevel of `ops/objects.rs`, the operation `story:builtin-group-modules` delivers), `sort_is_stable_for_11_513_and_2048_elements`, `sort_without_a_comparator_compares_strings_by_code_unit`, `sort_puts_undefined_last_and_holes_after_it`, `sort_with_a_comparator_that_throws_or_lies_ends`, `to_sorted_leaves_the_receiver_unchanged_and_reads_holes_as_undefined`, `sort_refuses_a_comparator_that_is_not_callable_before_it_reads`, `sort_charges_a_step_per_comparison` (2,048 elements cost at most a stated multiple of n log n steps and at least n, from `last_entry_steps()`), `step_budget_ends_a_sort_and_a_reverse`, `memory_budget_ends_a_sort_of_a_long_array_like`, and `comparator_and_conversions_reach_the_reentry_limit_on_the_promised_stack` (through `reach_through` of `tests/support/stack.rs`: `[1, 0].sort(f)`, `[1, 0].toSorted(f)` and `[0].fill(0, { valueOf: f })`, on seven eighths of `min_stack_bytes()`). `crates/swell/tests/language/` (new files): `builtin-array-pop-shift-unshift.js`, `builtin-array-splice.js`, `builtin-array-reverse-fill-copy-within.js`, `builtin-array-sort.js`. No test or script of this story calls a builtin another story of the wave delivers: a result is compared element by element or through `join`; the elements a sort test orders are literals or made in a loop with `push`, their keys are not built with `String.fromCharCode` or read with `charAt`; the species test of `splice` is a unit test of `mutation.rs` that sets `constructor[@@species]` through the well-known symbol of the realm, as the tests of `map` do; a receiver that refuses writes is made as said above.
